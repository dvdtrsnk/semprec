Low observations of the recheck, recorded in the ADR or below as follow-ups and left unchanged:

- With no row, the hook's `INSERT` can still meet the reauthorization's uncommitted insert; a `40P01` needs the mailbox to be trashed and its restore begun between two statements of that transaction.
- The sync job's new condition depends on the status the pass read: a pass that fails before the credential read writes `error`, and later passes then report the missing credential as an ordinary failure until the sign-in. Whether every missing credential should read as `needsReauthorization` is an open question about the sync job.
- `mailSyncJob.ts` (code) and `providerTypes.ts` (docstring) are outside the record's `Touches`. The first-round finding asked for this sync job change; the owner should accept it knowingly.
- The ADR consequence that names the generic `DELETE /api/items/:id` as one way a trashed mailbox keeps its sync state row is stale on this tip: gh-2031 landed before this chain and refuses that delete for a user and an agent. The ADR was not edited at landing.

Observations the requirements reviewer recorded without making them findings: a pass that read the credential before a removal reconciles once with that deleted credential after a removal and restore (declared, in the ADR, tested in `mailboxRemoval.test.ts`); the row check of `enterSyncPass` is reached only by the changed gh-1690 test; a restored iCloud or generic mailbox has no flow to store a new password, so "the user then signs in again" cannot happen for it; the `40P01` case is documented and not tested.

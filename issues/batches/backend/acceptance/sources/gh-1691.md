Findings and dispositions: none. No finding of any severity was raised.

Open for the owner. Two declared deviations change what the issue's text says, and the reviewers passed both without a finding and named them for the owner:

- The folder sync state delete is wider than "rows of its folders": the purge of a Mailboxes item deletes the `mail_folder_sync_state` row of every Folders item of the tenant that has no `Folders.mailbox` edge. The purge deletes the mailbox's edges before the hooks run, so the purged mailbox's folders cannot be told apart from a folder that had no mailbox before. Both the requirements and the rules reviewer found no row among them that a sync pass can still use. No decision record `issues/decisions/gh-1691.md` exists.
- The hook takes the mail-thread guard after the purge's locks, the reverse of ingest and the reconcilers. The ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-11-mail-purge-hook-takes-the-thread-guard-after-the-purge-locks.md`, added by this source, records the order and accepts its consequences: a purge of an Email waits for a holder of the guard up to the 60-second statement timeout while it holds the tenant's purge lock, and it can deadlock with a holder that then locks the purged item, where PostgreSQL aborts one side. Neither consequence was reproduced by a test (row 12).

Observations recorded without a finding, none of them changed:

- Requirements reviewer: the hook also soft-deletes the Threads item an emptied thread's row is bound to; the Task names only the row, and the alternative leaves a live Threads item bound to no row. A Folders item that is purged before its mailbox keeps its `mail_folder_sync_state` row, because the hook returns for a Folders item; the Task limits the hook to Emails and Mailboxes items.
- Requirements and security reviewers: when the Files item of an attachment is purged before its Email, the Email's purge removes the last `mail_attachments` reference and the `blobs` row and its bytes stay without a reference and without a deleter. Before this source the stale `mail_attachments` row kept the blob referenced for good. The issue puts blob bytes and Files items out of scope.
- Rules reviewer: the `mail_drafts` and `mail_message_move_sync_state` rows of a purged Email remain; the ADR lists them as outside its decision.
- Security reviewer: the sweep walks tenants one after another, so while one tenant's sync holds the guard, each of that tenant's expired Emails costs up to the statement timeout and delays, but does not skip, the tenants after it in that run. An availability effect; no data crosses tenants.
- Worker: the `credential_access_log` rows of a purged mailbox's credential and the `mail_threads` row of a purged Threads item stay; the Threads item of an emptied thread can be restored from the Trash as an item no thread row is bound to; follow-up 2 of the handover of sp-962cf217 (the locked read of the purge does not recheck the cutoff) now also decides which mail rows are deleted.

## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1807.md`. No decision record exists for this source. Worker summary: GET /api/inbox/cards (custom route listInboxCards, handler createInboxCardsRouteHandler) over a new read model inboxCardsStore.ts. The stack is the pending approvals of tick and MCP runs, read through the same resolver as GET /api/approval-requests?status=pending&source=tick,mcp (listApprovalRequestsQueuePage, oldest first, delegated runs resolved to their root run), followed by the open Processing proposals cards of kind inbox and transcript (statuses proposed, computing, computeFailed, needsClarification, invalid) in the order proposed (computing and computeFailed included), invalid, needsClarification, transcript. Inside a group the order is the capture date and time of the Inbox item (the instant of the transcript date for a transcript card), then id. At most 200 cards are returned; total counts every open card except computing and computeFailed ones, approvals past the first page included. Each card has cardId, source, cardKind, baseKind (inboxCardKind.ts), status, version (item updatedAt, null for an approval), resolvedSource (inboxItem with id/text/date/time/type {id,name,emoji}, transcript with id/name/completedAt, or run), context, targets [{databaseKey,label}] localized by the Inbox catalogs entries card.target.<baseKind> and the effective envelope (the stored proposal). Route matrix slice routeMatrix/inboxCards.ts, ROUTE_CASES case 'inbox cards' and ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES mapping added together.

Deviations and spec seams declared by the worker: (1) The Task does not name the field that holds the resolved source; it is resolvedSource, a union tagged by kind (inboxItem, transcript, run). (2) version is null for an approval card, which has no item. (3) The group of a transcript-kind card is always the last (transcript) group, whatever its status; the other groups hold cards of kind inbox only. The Task's wording leaves the group of an invalid or needsClarification transcript card open. (4) Labels: the Task says kinds and targets are labelled by cardKind through the Inbox catalogs; targets carry the label card.target.<baseKind> (11 kinds: task event page journal project area company person health file speaker), a kind label field is not part of the card because the Task's field list has none. A database without a key is left out of targets. (5) Ordering and cut at 200 are done in memory over a light query of all open cards (group, source date and time), then only the page is loaded; a card trashed between the two reads is left out (tested with a proxied client). (6) Spec seam 1: a new route matrix slice file routeMatrix/inboxCards.ts appended to ROUTE_MATRIX, plus the ROUTE_CASES case and the ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES mapping. (7) inboxCatalogs.ts (new) holds the cached Inbox catalog loader that invalidReason.ts had privately; invalidReason.ts now imports it (not in Touches). (8) A stored envelope that does not parse as an envelope still throws from parseStoredProposalEnvelope and fails the whole read, as journalInboxCompute does. (9) The route reads no query parameters.

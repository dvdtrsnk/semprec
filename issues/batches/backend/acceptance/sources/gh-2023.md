## Specification

Worker summary: `reserve()` catches a refusal caused by the tenant's own budget state (no budget row, daily cap, monthly cap) after the budget transaction rolled back and awaits `announceTenantRefusal` before it throws a fresh plain `BudgetExceededError`; one `sempStatus` invalidation per tenant, window kind and timezone, deduplicated in an in-process `AnnouncementLedger` whose entry lasts to the end of the window (the exhausted window as the gh-1456 summary names it, monthly winning; `noBudget` lasts to the end of the local day); operator-wide refusals and calls without a tenant scope announce nothing; a failing publish is logged and swallowed and the window released; `semprec-ai-gateway` wires `wireRealtimeHooks` over its own pool; new ADR `2026-11-13-the-gateway-announces-a-tenants-first-budget-refusal-of-a-window`.

Fixed in `5748c4cbd`: an awaited publisher in the data layer (`publishInvalidation` / `setInvalidationPublisher` in `realtimeHook.ts`, installed by `wireRealtimeHooks`, and `publishSempStatusInvalidationAndWait`) that rejects with the failure and logs nothing; the gateway publishes through it. Tests: `budgetRefusalRealtime.test.ts` fails a `pg_notify` through the real wiring (same refusal, one attempt, no frame, next refusal announces once), `tenantRefusalAnnouncement.test.ts` covers a rejecting publisher, `sempStatusInvalidation.test.ts` covers the new entry point. The fixer's mutation check (wiring without `setInvalidationPublisher` fails the new case) was not repeated by the queue runner.
- Low (requirements): no test failed if the `serve.ts` wiring of the realtime hooks was removed. Fixed in `16df81fb9`: the composition moved to `createGatewayServer` (`gatewayServer.ts`), which `serve.ts` calls before listening and `budgetRefusalRealtime.test.ts` builds its server with.

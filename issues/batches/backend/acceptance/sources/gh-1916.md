## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1916.md`. Worker summary: `inbox/proposalReferences.ts` (`envelopeReferencesOf`, `referencedItemIdsOf`) and `inbox/proposalReferenceInvalidation.ts` (`invalidateProposalsForItem`, `restoreProposalsForItem`, and the Projects update, item delete and item restore hooks registered in `domainWriteHooks.ts`). Every writer of a card's proposal stores `referencedItemIds` and lists it in `allowedSystemKeys`. A card of a project that becomes `archived`, or of an item that is deleted, becomes `invalid` with `invalidReason { code, field, itemId, label }` and an `invalidated` history entry in the same transaction; it returns to `proposed` (history kind `reopened`) when the item comes back and the stored envelope validates. Cards are found by jsonb containment and locked `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`. ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-an-item-hook-invalidates-the-open-cards-of-an-item-without-waiting-for-a-card-a-confirm-holds.md`.

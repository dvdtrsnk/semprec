Observations and follow-ups recorded without a finding, none of them changed:

- Requirements reviewer, for the owner: a duplicate whose page holds a subpage block or a link to an item that is trashed, purged or unknown can never be merged until that content is removed; the merge fails atomically with a `ValidationError` on field `markdown`, which names no input of the merge. Disposition: declared deviation, recorded in the ADR; lifting it needs a decision outside this Task. gh-2017 decides how its operation reports it.
- Requirements reviewer: a relation of another database that targets Journal, other than Inbox `journalDay`, is not re-pointed. Disposition: Task step 3 limits the generic move to relation properties of the Journal database and no such relation is seeded.
- Requirements reviewer: the merge locks entry rows before page document rows, while `handleItemSearchPageTextTask` locks the document first and the entry row after, so the two can deadlock and Postgres aborts one. Disposition: follow-up; the order predates the change and the ADR claims no deadlock freedom beyond the reservation and the two entries.
- Rules reviewer: `moveRelationEdgesWithClient` and `moveJournalDayEdges` do not call `requireManagedTransaction` themselves; both current callers run under the merge's own check. Disposition: recorded in the handover.
- Rules reviewer: `mergeJournalDuplicates` does not mark the transaction rollback-only on failure, so "a failure leaves both entries as they were" holds only when the caller lets the error escape. Disposition: recorded in the handover as a contract of the caller (gh-2017).
- Rules reviewer: when the reservation row is missing and the locked re-read finds fewer than two live entries, the `null` return leaves the reservation row the lock step inserted. Disposition: follow-up; judged no worse than the missing row.
- Fixer and recheck, for gh-2017: the result has a third field, `refusedEdges`; the Task output of gh-2017 is `{ survivorId, mergedItemId }`, so it maps the fields explicitly and its owner decides whether to show `refusedEdges`.
- Worker: the contended-transaction test harness is copied in `journalRestore.test.ts` and `mergeJournalDuplicates.test.ts` and could move to `testSupport`; `journalDayRelinkHook.ts` could use `moveRelationEdgesWithClient`. Disposition: follow-ups.

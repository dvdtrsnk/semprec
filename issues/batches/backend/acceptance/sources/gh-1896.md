## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1896.md`. Worker summary: POST /api/mail/drafts/:id/send (custom route mailDraftSend, transactional-semantics). sendMailDraft (mail/mailDraftSend.ts) reads the draft in one transaction (item lock, then mail_drafts row lock), builds the send input (From, recipients, subject, text body, the draft's attachments with the blob's mime type, In-Reply-To and References from a reply or reply-all source message, the provider thread id of a source in the same mailbox) and calls sendDraftEmail as actor user with the transport of the mailbox's sync mode. sendDraftEmail now refuses a mailbox in needsReauthorization (409 mail_reauthorization_required) before the claim, raises MailSentFolderMissingError (409 mail_sent_folder_missing, was a ValidationError), takes attachments and providerThreadId, and in its finalizing transaction sets mail_drafts.sent_at (when the draft has a row), announces the mailThread invalidation and returns threadId and sentAt. A lost claim is the new MailSendClaimLostError (a ConflictError) which the route answers as 409 mail_draft_already_sent with details {emailId, threadId}; a repeated call after the send gets the same answer from the draft row. Transport failures map by class: MailReauthorizationRequiredError to 409 mail_reauthorization_required, MailRecipientRejectedError to 422 mail_recipient_rejected, MailSendAuthFailedError to mail_auth_failed, MailServerUnreachableError to mail_server_unreachable; any other failure propagates unchanged and the claim is undone as before. 400 without a recipient in To, Cc or Bcc; 409 mail_draft_not_editable for an item without a row; 404 for unknown, trashed, malformed or another tenant's id. errorContract.ts gained mail_reauthorization_required (409), mail_recipient_rejected (422), mail_sent_folder_missing (409); mail_auth_failed and mail_server_unreachable were present (400). createDispatcher builds mailServerClients.send (createMailTransportFactory from the container's oauth clients and outboundHttp, attachment storage from its blob storage) when the caller's container has none.

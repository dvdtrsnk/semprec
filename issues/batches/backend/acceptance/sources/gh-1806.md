## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1806.md`. No decision record exists for this source.

Worker summary: calendar/timelineSleepSource.ts: reads the sleep settings item, the plans and the night exceptions of [from-1 day, to] with its own queries, asks resolveSleepNights for those nights in the context's tenant zone and returns one entry per night (kind sleep, id sleep:<night>, readOnly false, night, planId, bedTime, wakeTime, alarm, changed, bedAt, wakeAt, prepReminderAt). Returns nothing when there is no settings item, several settings items (logged with the count), enabled or showInCalendar is not true. Plans and exceptions that do not parse (strict HH:MM, types) are left out with one log line carrying ids, the prep reminder is off for unusable minutes (outside 0..1440). The entry sorts by its night after the day's timed Tasks. Registered in timelineSources.ts. ADR docs/adr/2026-11-12-a-sleep-night-in-the-timeline-is-a-derived-entry-the-user-edits.md records readOnly false for a derived kind (narrows decision 6 of the timeline ADR).

Deviations and spec seams declared by the worker: (1) The Task presupposes 'the sleep settings reader of the sleep module'; no exported reader exists (findSleepMuteUntil of gh-1794 keeps its SELECTs private and loadSleepNights of gh-1809 is not delivered and is not a blocker of gh-1806). The source reads the three sleep databases itself, as gh-1794 did, rather than blocking; gh-1809's loader can replace it. (2) The Task lists the resolver fields without planName and durationMinutes; the entry carries exactly the listed ones. (3) readOnly false for a derived entry contradicts decision 6 of docs/adr/2026-11-10-calendar-timeline-merges-a-list-of-sources.md; recorded in the new ADR as the spec requires. (4) Because a seeded tenant has sleep on and shown by default, every seeded tenant's timeline now holds sleep entries; the fixtures of the existing core timeline tests (timelineRead.test.ts, timelineRoute.test.ts) and of the birthday and holiday tests switch showInCalendar off through the new helper calendar/__tests__/hideSleepInCalendar.ts, and journalDayEvents.test.ts compares the Journal events with the timeline's event entries only. (5) A sleep entry has no title/date/calendarId (not in the spec); the merge sorts it by night.

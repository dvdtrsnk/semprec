## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1899.md`. Worker summary: POST /api/mail/drafts now prefills the modes reply, replyAll and forward from the source message (new backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailDraftPrefill.ts, called from the create route handler). Reply goes to Reply-To else sender; replyAll adds the source's To and Cc minus all addresses of the mailbox and duplicates; forward has no recipients; Cc and Bcc are always empty. from is the delivered-to address when it is one of the mailbox's addresses, else the mailbox's first address. Subject gets Re: or Fwd: unless it already starts with it (case-insensitive). in_reply_to is the source Message-ID and references are the source's plus that id (forward sets neither; it keeps the source thread id). The body is the person's text, an empty line and the quote: a localized header (new keys in mail/i18n cs.json and en.json, date by Intl in the tenant's system timezone) and the source's plain text, every line behind '> '; a forward uses a Forwarded message header with sender, date, subject and recipients. A forward copies the source's attachments through attachFileToDraft (own copies, the original keeps its files). createEmailDraftInMailbox gained optional inReplyTo and references. Computed fields in the request body are ignored; sourceEmailId is required for the three modes (400); a mode new body keeps its required fields.

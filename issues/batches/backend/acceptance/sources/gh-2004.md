## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-2004.md`. Worker summary: `importSubscribedEvents` in `calendar/subscribedEventsImport.ts` parses a downloaded ICS body with `ical.js` 2.2.1 (exact pin) completely before any write (a malformed feed throws `CalendarFeedParseError`), converts timed events to the tenant wall clock with `calendarTime.ts`, stores floating times unchanged and all-day events at midnight of the first and the inclusive last day, skips RECURRENCE-ID components and stores RRULE components once, upserts by `(calendar, uid)` without rewriting unchanged rows, moves disappeared UIDs to the Trash, and writes through the choke point as the system owner process `calendar`. It is registered with `registerCalendarFeedConsumer` in `domainWriteHooks.ts`.

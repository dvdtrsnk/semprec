## Specification

The record is `issues/items/gh-1867.md`. Worker summary: POST /api/mail/drafts, GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/mail/drafts/:id, registered in the mail manifest and the route matrix. mail/draft.ts gains the operations createEmailDraftInMailbox (checks mailbox, sender address, thread via alias translation, source message and Drafts folder; then createEmailDraft with metadata writes item, Drafts edge and mail_drafts row in one transaction), readEmailDraft, updateEmailDraft (locks the Emails item then the mail_drafts row; order of refusals 404, 409 mail_draft_not_editable, 409 mail_draft_already_sent, 409 version_conflict against the item's updatedAt, 400 mailbox/thread move, 403 from_address_not_registered, 404 source; records last_edited_by from the writer kind; always rewrites subject/sender/recipients/body so updatedAt moves) and discardEmailDraft (trashes the item, deletes the row to free the thread, announces). mailDraftRouteHandlers.ts holds the four handlers with strict zod bodies, length caps (address 320, subject 998, body 500000, 100 recipients per list), no control characters in header values, no NUL or lone surrogate in the body. MailDraftAlreadySentError and MailDraftNotEditableError (409) were added and registered in errorContract.ts. mailDraftsStore.ts gained getMailDraft(..., forUpdate) and deleteMailDraft. Every successful change publishes the thin mailThread invalidation for the draft's thread (translated, once, after the commit); a draft without a thread announces nothing.

# Decision: when the warm-up runs, what it leaves out, and the files it touches

- Question: The Task of sp-a4e32028-4e3a-4320-9191-522c2edc851c runs the warm-up after the queue replay finished, but `replaySoon` ran what follows a replay even when it found one under way, before that one finished. It warms the Inbox count, which no screen reads, and it says nothing of a warm-up that started online when the connection drops. The trigger pushes `OfflineLayer` past detekt's limit of functions per class, and the offline set's decision ([[2026-11-23-mobile-keeps-the-answers-of-the-offline-set]], on the same unmerged branch) names warming as a later decision. What does the issue deliver, and in which files?
- Options considered: for the order, a flag the warm-up polls, or the replayer itself running what follows each replay that it finished, whatever started it (a trigger or a retry); for the count, warming it as listed or leaving it out; for a dropped connection, letting every remaining read wait for its timeout or stopping the reads once the process is offline; for the limit, a suppression or moving the kept-answer reads of the layer into a class of their own in the same file; for the record, a new ADR or the offline set's decision stating the warm-up.
- Decision: the replayer runs what follows a replay it finished (`QueueReplay.kt`); the count is left out, since an answer nobody reads only costs a request; the reads stop once the process turns offline; the kept-answer reads move into `KeptReads` and the schedule into `OfflineWarmupSchedule.kt`; the offline set's decision, not accepted on `develop` yet, states the warm-up.
- Spec change (Task 2): "the overview, the sleep overview of today and the Inbox cards, count and types. A read that fails is left as it is; cancellation stops it." became "the overview, the sleep overview of today and the Inbox cards and types (no screen reads the Inbox count). A read that fails is left as it is; cancellation stops it, and so does the process turning offline."
- Spec change (Touches): "OfflineWarmup.kt (new), OfflineLayer.kt (the trigger)" became "OfflineWarmup.kt (new), OfflineWarmupSchedule.kt (new), OfflineLayer.kt (the trigger; its kept-answer reads in a class of their own), QueueReplay.kt (what runs after a replay)", and "- docs/adr/2026-11-23-mobile-keeps-the-answers-of-the-offline-set.md (the warm-up)" was added.

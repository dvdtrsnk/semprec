# Decision: a write made while offline does not wait for the queue

- Question: Task item 3 of sp-c12f129d-e765-4ef1-967c-a1e4070e162b made every write wait up to 10 s for its queue entry, offline included. Should a write made while the process is known to be offline wait?
- Options considered: (a) wait up to 10 s in every case, as written; (b) return the made-up answer at once while offline and wait only while the process is online with entries still queued, so that the order of the writes and the server's answer are kept whenever the server can answer.
- Decision: (b). Offline, the replay cannot succeed, so the wait would only delay every change by 10 s against the epic's demand that the app is fast; online with entries queued, the wait still returns the server's own answer and refusal.
- Spec change (Task item 3):
  - Old: "While offline, or while anything is queued, the request is appended, the replay is started, and the caller waits up to 10 s for that entry: the server's answer when it was sent, its refusal when it was dropped, the made-up answer otherwise."
  - New: "While offline, the request is appended, the replay is started and the made-up answer is returned at once. While anything is queued and the process is not offline, the request is appended, the replay is started, and the caller waits up to 10 s for that entry: the server's answer when it was sent, its refusal when it was dropped, the made-up answer otherwise."

# Decision: removing areas and tagging projects

- Question: Should the Areas layer stay, and if not, what replaces it for organizing projects?
- Options considered: (a) keep Areas; (b) remove Areas and add a Tags database related to Projects; (c) remove Areas
  and add a user-owned `multi_select` `tags` property on Projects, filtered in the projects collection through the
  filter sheet and a server-declared tag bar, any-of semantics, delivered as one sequential batch.
- Decision: (c), chosen by the owner in the planning conversation of 2026-11-27, together with: no tag filter for
  tasks, and tag renaming, recoloring and deletion out of scope. The four implementation records were approved on the
  owner's explicit instruction to proceed with the plan.

**Blocked by:** #249, #91

## Context

Second of three parts of the repository-workflow and CI-gate slice of the `deployment` batch (approved behavior in #187). #249 delivered the CI triggers for pull requests and pushes to `develop` and `main`, the promotion-only guard for `main`, and the stable required-check name contract (both branches were already protected). This part expands `.github/workflows/ci.yml` with the first three named tiers from the three-tier test infrastructure of #114 and the dependency boundary checks of #173. #188 completes the gate with the e2e and pi-agent contract tiers.

## Task

- Expand `.github/workflows/ci.yml` with three jobs whose names match the contract from #249: `unit` (the pure unit tier), `integration` (the integration tier running against an ephemeral PostgreSQL service started inside the workflow), and `dependency-check` (the static dependency-boundary checks from #173).
- All three run on every pull-request update and every merge to `develop` or `main` (the triggers from #249).
- Add the three job names to the required checks of both the `develop` and the `main` branch protection, replacing the single `ci` job where it becomes redundant, and update the required-check contract document from #249.
- Job names are stable identifiers: renaming a job is a contract change and must update branch protection and the documented contract together.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `unit`, `integration`, and `dependency-check` jobs, the ephemeral PostgreSQL service, required-check registration, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- The `e2e` and `pi-agent-contract` jobs — #188.
- Branch reconciliation and protection setup — #249.
- Feature-test expansion — never (see #187).

## Acceptance criteria

- On a pull request and on a merge to `develop` or `main`, the `unit`, `integration`, and `dependency-check` jobs run and report under exactly those names.
- The `integration` job runs the integration tier against a PostgreSQL instance created for that run and torn down afterwards; no external database is required.
- A failure in any of the three jobs blocks merging of the pull request.

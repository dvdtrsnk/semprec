## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions: tasks-rollover-1

#### Adopted decisions
- Night job vs read model without writing Tasks.date -> night job core.tasksDayRollover writes date via ownership handover -> design note recommends it; every reader (Tasks, calendar, Overview, search) sees one stored value.
  -> Decided by the product owner: the nightly job stays the only writer of the rollover; no parallel read model (#1674).
- When does `since` vanish -> never cleared -> the design does not clear it; traces need it after completion too.
- Timezone for travelling users (device vs System settings.timezone) -> system timezone setting -> conservative; matches existing heartbeat/getSystemTimezone.
  -> Decided by the product owner: one zone from the System settings `timezone` is used for the rollover and for display; when the device zone changes, the app offers to switch that setting (client work over the existing settings write and the zone exposed by #1531). Automatic following of the device zone is not done in v1 (#1674, #1602).
- effectiveDate before #1780 -> equals date; E2 extends the rule and the job filter.
- Trace outcome set -> carried | doneOn {date} | movedToEvent {id,title} | missed (a related issue design note shape; "movedOn" variant in the third design note dropped).
- Task completed before its planned day -> Decided by the product owner: the task is shown as done on the day it was completed and disappears from its planned day; the day chain ends on the completion day, as everywhere else; no traces (#1884; the display-day rule of #1544 follows it).
- Ownership handover documented by a doc comment on rolloverTaskWithClient, no new ADR (existing single-writer ADR covers the pattern); the design note's "note in the ADR" was not followed because ADRs are append-only.
- #1884 tier_hint was null -> medium.

- A task's time shown in the calendar but not in the list -> Decided by the product owner: the time is shown the same in the calendar and in the list; there is one canonical set of data, the fix is in the client (no backend change).

#### User-visible behavior (epic paragraph)
A task left undone at midnight (in the user's timezone) is moved to the next day as an all-day task, day after day until it is done, and appears in Today as "moved from 13 October". The system remembers the first planned day and its time, so the calendar shows traces for every day the task was carried ("not done, moved on", "not done, done on 16 October", "not done, moved to event X"), the first day at its original time and later days all-day, and the live task says where it came from. Moving the task to another day or time keeps the first day and the traces. A task completed before its planned day shows as done on its completion day and no longer on the planned day. Midnight and "today" follow the timezone in the system settings; the app offers to switch it when the device zone changes. Tasks hidden inside a running event wait until the event ends; completed, cancelled, deleted and recurring tasks are not moved by this job.

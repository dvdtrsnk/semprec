**Model tier:** medium

## Context

Model tier rationale: A read-only aggregate route over several mail tables with precise counting rules and isolation tests, no writes.

Before a user removes a mailbox, the confirmation dialog must say what is affected. Removing a mailbox (#1690) soft-deletes its folders and messages (#2029), while threads that also contain messages from other mailboxes survive and attachment files stay.

This route reports those counts. It is a read-only aggregate that no generic read can express. The tables involved are the Emails and Folders items related to the mailbox (an Email relates to its Folders, a Folder to its Mailbox), `mail_message_meta` (thread id per message) and `mail_attachments` (`message_item_id`).

Decision: the counting rules below are the definition of what the removal soft-deletes; #2029 applies the same rules.

Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for #1454, #1456 because both edit the same registry, manifest, cutover, route matrix or hook files listed in Touches.

## Task

Load `io-hardening` and `testing`.

1. Add `GET /api/mail/mailboxes/:id/removal-impact` as a custom route of the mail module (justification `single-consumer-read`), authenticated and tenant-scoped; an unknown or foreign mailbox answers 404.
2. Respond `{ messages, threads, drafts, attachmentsKept }` where:
   - `drafts` counts the non-deleted Emails whose folder is one of the mailbox's Folders with `specialPurpose` `drafts`;
   - `messages` counts the other non-deleted Emails in any of the mailbox's Folders;
   - `threads` counts the threads whose every non-deleted message belongs to this mailbox (a thread with a message from another mailbox is not counted);
   - `attachmentsKept` counts the attachment rows of the counted messages and drafts; the stored files are kept.
3. An Email that is in several folders of the mailbox is counted once.

## Scope

### In scope

- The impact route and its counting rules.

### Out of scope

- The removal itself (#1690) and the soft-delete of folders and messages (#2029).
- The mailbox list and detail response (#1572).

## Acceptance criteria

- When a mailbox has messages, drafts and attachments, then the counts equal the numbers of non-deleted rows by the rules above.
- When a thread contains messages from this and another mailbox, then it is not counted.
- When a message sits in two folders of the mailbox, then it is counted once.
- A mailbox of another tenant answers 404.

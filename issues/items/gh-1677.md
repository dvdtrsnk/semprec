**Blocked by:** #1674
**Model tier:** high

## Context

Model tier rationale: A scheduled batch job writes user-owned status through an ownership handover while racing the series advance under a shared lock order, and it must be correct across outages and re-runs.

A recurring task that is not completed on its day must not roll to the next day like a one-off task. It is marked as missed and the series continues with the next occurrence. Today there is no nightly action and no `missed` status. The series advances only on `done` (`backend/packages/data/src/tasks/taskRecurrenceItemUpdateHook.ts`), so an occurrence nobody completes stays open forever and the next occurrence never appears. The `status` property is owned by `user` (`backend/packages/data/src/seed/seedTenDatabases.ts`), so a job needs an ownership handover (`docs/adr/2026-09-10-single-writer-ownership-model.md`).

This issue extends the nightly action `core.tasksDayRollover` created by #1674 (same batching, same handover function, widened to allow `missed`) with the closing of recurring occurrences. It uses the `missed` status from #1668 and the date functions and lock order from #1670 (`latestOccurrenceOnOrBefore`).

Decision: the nightly job is the writer of `missed` for stored occurrences (a read-model computation is not used); materialized future occurrences are kept; intermediate days are not materialized, because the projection of #1672 shows them as missed.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `error-handling`, `testing` and `adr-conventions`.

1. In the `core.tasksDayRollover` action add a step for recurring occurrences: select open (`notDone`) occurrences with a `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate` before today whose next occurrence by the rule is on or before today. For each, in its own batch transaction, lock the item, then the `task_recurrence` row `FOR UPDATE` (order items, then `task_recurrence`), and set `status` to `missed` through the ownership handover of #1674.
2. For each such series, ensure an occurrence exists at `latestOccurrenceOnOrBefore(today)`: if no non-deleted item with that `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate` exists, create it from the template as `notDone` and point `task_recurrence.item_id` at it. Older open occurrences of the same series become `missed` too. An ensured occurrence dated before today stays `notDone` (it shows as overdue).
3. `done`, `wontDo` and `missed` occurrences are never changed. The rules for one-off tasks (moving to today, first planned day) are not applied to occurrences of a series.
4. The step is idempotent: a second run on the same day changes nothing. After an outage of several days one run reaches the same end state.
5. Tests per the acceptance criteria, including a concurrent completion racing the job.

## Scope

### In scope

- Marking stale open occurrences of recurring tasks as missed and ensuring the latest occurrence at midnight.

### Out of scope

- Rolling over one-off tasks: #1674.
- The `missed` option and who may write it from clients: #1668.
- Advance on completion: #1670. Virtual occurrences: #1672.

## Acceptance criteria

- A daily occurrence of 15 October left open: after the run on 16 October it is `missed` and an occurrence for 16 October exists.
- A weekly Monday occurrence of 12 October stays `notDone` until Sunday 18 October; the run on 19 October marks it `missed` and ensures an occurrence for 19 October.
- A weekly occurrence of 5 October and a run only on 16 October (outage): 5 October becomes `missed` and an occurrence for 12 October (`notDone`) is created, not 16 or 19 October.
- A second run on the same day changes nothing; done, wontDo and missed occurrences stay as they are.

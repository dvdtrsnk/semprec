## Decisions

- **Clustering.** Findings are grouped by mechanism and by the files their fixes touch, so no two sub-issues share a file and all seven are eligible at once with no `Blocked by:` edges. Reason: batching small same-kind fixes is what this pipeline is for, and disjoint `## Touches` make parallel implementation safe.
- **Duplicate reports are one finding.** The two `backdateBaseline` reports (PR 774), the three `queueConcurrency` casts, the two `SMTP_PORT` coverage reports and the three `failEventsFor` reports describe one defect each, so each group is covered by one acceptance criterion.
- **`queueConcurrency.test.ts` location.** Its three cast findings were reported at lines 42 and 46; the code is now at line 69.
- **Not widened.** `isUniqueViolation` is duplicated in `databasesStore.ts` and `viewsStore.ts` with the same cast; only `propertiesStore.ts` was flagged, so only it is in scope.
- **Small is not a reason to reject.** No finding was rejected as `not-worth` or `invalid`.

### Decisions: recipes-1

#### (a) Decisions adopted
- Ingredients only in the page, or also structured? -> Page only (bulleted list and numbered list blocks) -> sufficient for the design and no shopping list exists yet. -> Decided by the product owner: text on the page only; a shopping list is outside v1.
- Relation Recipes -> Areas? -> Not added -> the design has none on a recipe; can be added later as a one-directional relation. -> Decided by the product owner: not for now; the Recipes row in the Area detail is dropped from the design, so Areas do not list Recipes among their related collections.
- Recipes in Trash: can the system collection be deleted or hidden? -> Decided by the product owner: Recipes is a system module and cannot be deleted (archiving a system database is already refused), only hidden from the overview; the deleted database shown in the design's Trash is a user collection that the design renames, so the sample data matches across screens. Hiding from the overview is #2002.
- Unused blob age and frequency -> 24 hours, daily job -> as in the design note.
- Standalone upload content types -> `image/*` only for now -> needed only for recipe photos, covers; widened by later issues when needed.
- Extraction failure -> no recipe proposal, item handled as before -> avoids creating empty recipes.
- Dependency of recipes 03 -> it needs the proposal-edit contract of #1471, #1892, #1912 and #1920 -> Decided by the product owner: yes, recipes 03 waits for those four issues.

#### (b) Open questions for the product owner

#### (c) User-visible behavior
Recipes appears as a new collection with Category (colored), Time, Servings, Favorite and Photo, shown as a gallery by default, for new and existing accounts. Users can hide Recipes from the overview but cannot delete it. Users can upload a photo that is stored only as an image (no Files entry) and unused uploads vanish after a day. Semp can propose a recipe from a link or photo captured in the Inbox, with ingredients and method written onto the recipe page.

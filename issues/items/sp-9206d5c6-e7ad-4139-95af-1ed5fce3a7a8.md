## Context

Model tier rationale: Adds delayed reconcile triggers to every accepted IMAP/SMTP send, inside the transaction that records the send, on the path that decides when a self-addressed message or a bounce reaches the inbox.

End-to-end finding F34 on the dev server against Purelymail (with IDLE session logs): IDLE signals external deliveries within a second, but Purelymail does not push its own internal deliveries that follow our send to the IDLE session — a message to the account's own address and a DSN for a capped send both landed in INBOX about 5 s after the send, and no sync ran for more than two minutes (session up, no end or reconnect, no job). Other providers behave similarly for DSNs.

Verified in the code: an accepted IMAP/SMTP send (`transport.send` answering `needsSentCopy`) records its Sent copy with `recordPendingSentCopy` — in `sendDraftEmail`'s finalization transaction (draft send and the approval executor's send for an agent or MCP request), and through `recordPendingSentCopyAfterSend` for the invitation and invitation-reply jobs and for a send whose finalization failed. Nothing after the send asks for a reconcile, so a delivery the server does not push waits for the next trigger.

Decisions:

- The follow-ups are `mailAccountSync` jobs (the normal task, so a run during a pass only asks that pass for one more round) with their own job keys per mailbox and slot, `run_at` now + 15 s and now + 60 s, `jobKeyMode: "preserve_run_at"`: a later send while a slot is pending keeps that slot's time, so sends coalesce to one pending follow-up per slot and a stream of sends cannot postpone it forever.
- They are enqueued by `recordPendingSentCopy`, so in the transaction that records the send and its copy, and only where a copy is recorded: Gmail and Graph sends (no `needsSentCopy`) are unaffected.

## Scope

### In scope

- Delayed follow-up `mailAccountSync` jobs after an accepted send that records a Sent copy.

### Out of scope

- The sync pass and its single-flight, IDLE, the Sent copy job, Gmail/Graph sends, the periodic sweep.

## Acceptance criteria

- An accepted draft send enqueues exactly two follow-up `mailAccountSync` jobs for its mailbox, keyed per slot, due about 15 s and 60 s after the send.
- A second send while both are pending leaves one job per slot with its first due time.
- A send the server refuses enqueues none; a send whose provider files its own Sent copy enqueues none.
- A copy recorded after the send (`recordPendingSentCopyAfterSend`, the invitation path) enqueues the two follow-ups.
- The existing Sent copy and send tests stay green.

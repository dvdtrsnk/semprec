## Context

Model tier rationale: One more thread action route that reuses the thread helpers, the local folder-edge move and the desired-move recording of #1917 and the durable move queue of #1746; the new parts are the target rule per message and three 409 cases, all with existing fixtures.

Archiving and trashing a conversation (#1917) move its messages into the Archive or Trash folder of each mailbox, locally and, through the durable move queue of #1746, at the provider. Nothing is ever deleted permanently. The product owner decided that everything archived or trashed can be restored: a thread in Archive or Trash can be moved back to the inbox. Today there is no way to do that; the only writes to `Emails.folder` are system-owned (`EMAILS_RELATION_CONTEXT`), so a client cannot move the edges itself.

#1746 accepts the desired purposes `inbox` and `sent` and performs the provider writes for them (IMAP move to the Inbox or Sent folder, Gmail untrash and the `INBOX` label, Graph move to `inbox` or `sentitems`). This issue adds the route that records those moves and moves the folder edges locally.

Decisions:
- The route acts on the counted messages of the thread in every account that are currently in the Archive or Trash folder of their mailbox (for a Gmail API account the synthetic Archive of #1496). Messages in any other folder, including Junk, are not touched.
- An incoming message goes to the folder with `specialPurpose` `inbox` of its own mailbox; the user's own message (the own/incoming classification of `mail/mailThreadActions.ts`) goes back to the folder with `specialPurpose` `sent`, so the user's sent mail never appears in the Inbox folder. The thread then shows in `box=inbox` again.
- A thread with no message in Archive or Trash fails with 409 `mail_thread_not_archived_or_trashed`; a mailbox without the needed target folder fails with 409 `mail_inbox_folder_missing` or `mail_sent_folder_missing`. In each case nothing is changed.
- The local edge move uses `EMAILS_RELATION_CONTEXT` and the desired move (`inbox` or `sent`) is recorded in the same transaction, as for archive and trash. Each successful call sends one `mailThread` invalidation after commit.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Implement `POST /api/mail/threads/:threadId/restore` in `mail/mailThreadRoutes.ts`, declared in `mailModuleManifest.ts` (`handlerExport` `createMailThreadRestoreRouteHandler`, justification `transactional-semantics`) with a `routeMatrix.ts` entry. Resolve merged ids and return 404 for unknown or foreign ids, using the helpers of `mailThreadActions.ts`.
2. In one transaction: select the messages above; for each, find the target folder of its mailbox by the rule above, remove its current folder edges and create the target edge (preserving nothing from the old UID metadata), and call `recordDesiredMailMessageMove` with `inbox` or `sent`; then enqueue the account sync through `enqueueMailAccountSync`. Fail with the 409 codes above before writing anything.
3. Response `{ threadId, moved: <number of messages> }`.
4. Add tests: restore of an archived thread puts its messages back in the Inbox folder and the thread back in `box=inbox`; restore of a trashed thread puts incoming messages in Inbox and own messages in Sent; a thread with messages in two accounts is restored in both; messages in Junk or Inbox are not touched; Gmail restores from the synthetic Archive and from Trash; a thread with nothing in Archive or Trash returns 409 and changes nothing; a mailbox without an Inbox or Sent folder returns 409 and changes nothing; move rows are recorded as pending with `inbox` or `sent`; one invalidation per call; a merged id works; an unknown or foreign id is 404.

## Scope

### In scope

- The restore route, the local edge moves back to Inbox or Sent, the desired-move recording and the 409 cases.

### Out of scope

- The provider writes for the `inbox` and `sent` purposes: #1746.
- Archive and trash: #1917.
- Restoring messages from Junk, and moving a single message instead of a thread.
- Permanent deletion.
- A web client control for restore.

## Acceptance criteria

- When an archived thread is restored, then its messages are in the Inbox folder of their mailboxes and the thread appears in `box=inbox` again.
- When a trashed thread is restored, then its incoming messages are in the Inbox folder and the user's own messages are in the Sent folder of their mailboxes.
- When a thread has no message in Archive or Trash, then restore returns 409 `mail_thread_not_archived_or_trashed` and no edge changes.
- When a mailbox has no Inbox or no Sent folder needed by a message, then restore returns 409 `mail_inbox_folder_missing` or `mail_sent_folder_missing` and no edge changes.
- When a call succeeds, then each moved message has a pending move row with `inbox` or `sent` and exactly one `mailThread` invalidation is sent.

## Context

Model tier rationale: One more id-less scope on the established realtime catalog whose publication hangs off the problems store from the preceding issue, with tenant-wide delivery and after-commit semantics already covered by existing fixtures.

The Semp status screen (problems and services), the problem count in the profile and the badge change when a problem is raised, merged into an existing one, resolved, or when a service changes state. Nothing tells a connected client today. This issue adds the realtime scope `sempStatus`, with no payload fields, following exactly the shape and wiring of the earlier scopes in this chain (#1863, #1866): the `invalidation` message type with a `scope` discriminator in `backend/packages/realtime/src/pgNotifyPublisher.ts`, the `OutboundFrame` variant in `backend/packages/realtime/src/protocolV1.ts`, the `InvalidationEvent` variant in `backend/packages/data/src/realtimeHook.ts`, the mapping in `syncServer.ts` and the parser in `backend/packages/realtime-client/src/serverFrame.ts`. Rules of `docs/adr/2026-10-16-tenant-scoped-realtime-fan-out.md` apply: thin payload, tenant from Postgres, no replay, publish only after commit with `runAfterCommit`.

Decision: delivered to every socket of the tenant (no `userId`): problems are raised by background work with no acting user.

Publication points are the write operations of the persisted Semp problems store delivered by #1459: raising a problem, merging a repeated occurrence into an existing problem, resolving a problem, and changing the state of a service. Publish once per such write after commit. The missing or exhausted budget problem is derived at read time by #1871, not written to this store; its refreshes are published by #2023 (first refusal) and #2024 (window reset). Problems that are only counted (not changed) publish nothing.

The next scope in the chain is #1918.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `canonical-keys`, `io-hardening` and `testing`.

1. Add the `sempStatus` variant to `InvalidationEvent`, `RealtimeMessage` (+ `parseRealtimeMessage`), `OutboundFrame` (`{ type: "invalidate", scope: "sempStatus" }`), the `invalidation` case of `syncServer.ts`, and `parseServerFrame`.
2. Create `backend/packages/data/src/sempStatus/sempStatusInvalidation.ts` exporting `publishSempStatusInvalidation(client)` that defers `notifyInvalidation({ scope: "sempStatus" })` with `runAfterCommit`.
3. Call it inside the raise, merge, resolve and service-change operations of the problems store from #1459, in the same transaction as the write. An idempotent replay that changes nothing publishes nothing.
4. Tests: one frame per kind of write; none on a no-op replay or on rollback; tenant isolation.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `sempStatus` scope end to end and its publication on every change of problems and services.

### Out of scope

- Raising any new kind of problem: the problem-raising issues; the budget problem and its refreshes: #1871, #2023, #2024.
- The agent runs and project scopes: #1863 and #1866.
- Notification preference and badge scopes: #1918.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a problem is raised, merged, resolved or a service changes state, then every socket of that tenant receives `invalidate` with scope `sempStatus` and no other field.
- When a write changes nothing (replay) or the transaction rolls back, then no frame is sent.
- When a socket of another tenant is connected, then it does not receive the frame.
- The frame carries no problem text.

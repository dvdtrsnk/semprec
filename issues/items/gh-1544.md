**Model tier:** medium

## Context

Model tier rationale: One mechanism (completion-day rules inside the Tasks pre-write transform) over existing seams and fixtures, with several edge cases around time zones and repeat writes that need care.

A task is completed by setting `status` to `done` through `PATCH /api/items/:id` (`ifVersion` required). Today nothing records when: `items` only carry `updated_at`, and no completion property exists. Clients need the day a task was completed to show it on that day (a task completed on the 14th shows there even if its date is the 16th), to end a carried-over chain at the completion day, and to show the right day in the timeline.

What this builds on: the Tasks pre-write transform from #1422 (resulting-state validation, hooks see the prepared patch), the schema from #1540 (`Tasks.completedOn`, a user-owned date, and `Tasks.completedAt`, a system-owned date with time with `ownerProcess` `tasks`), and the tenant time zone helpers (`getSystemTimezone` in `backend/packages/data/src/timezone.ts`; the calendar time module from #1418 when present). What builds on it: #1670 (advance a series past the completed occurrence), #1781 (completing inside an event) and #1884 (carry-over and traces in the timeline).

Behavior of the recurrence hook today: `taskRecurrenceItemUpdateHook` (`tasks/`) fires on every patch with `status: "done"`, but it only advances an active recurrence, so a repeated `done`, or `done` then `notDone` then `done`, creates no second instance, and `done` back to `notDone` leaves two open instances. The series rules are owned by #1670; this issue does not change that hook.

Decision: `completedOn` may not be in the future. A client completing a future occurrence early sends no `completedOn` (so it becomes today); the occurrence day is kept elsewhere (#1720).

Decision (product owner): `completedOn` may be earlier than the task's date or first planned day; the user enters it as it really happened, and that is also how completing a past trace of a moved task works.

Decision (product owner): a completed task is sorted and shown by its completion day (`completedOn`); completing a task never rewrites its planned `date`.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Extend the Tasks pre-write transform (registered by #1536) with completion rules, judged on the resulting state:
   - A transition from a non-`done` status to `done` without a `completedOn` in the patch sets `completedOn` to today in the tenant time zone and `completedAt` to the current instant.
   - A transition to `done` with `completedOn` in the patch stores that day; `completedAt` is the current instant.
   - A repeated PATCH of `status: "done"` on a task that is already `done` leaves an existing `completedOn` and `completedAt` unchanged.
   - A PATCH of only `completedOn` on a `done` task is allowed and changes only `completedOn`; `completedAt` stays.
   - `completedOn` later than today, or `completedOn` on a task that is not `done` after the write, is rejected: 400 `validation_failed`, field `completedOn`.
   - A transition to `notDone` or `wontDo` sets `completedOn` and `completedAt` to null.
   - None of these rules writes `date`; the planned day stays as stored.
   - `completedAt` cannot be written by clients (system-owned); only the transform sets it.
2. Write the day a task is shown and sorted on, as a pure helper `taskDisplayDay(task)` exported from the tasks module for the timeline and list issues: for a series occurrence it is `occurrenceDate`; otherwise, for a `done` task with `completedOn`, it is `completedOn` (also when that is earlier or later than the planned day); otherwise `effectiveDate`. It reads properties only; consumers are #1717 and #1602, which show and sort a completed task by this day. Tests cover each branch.
3. Tests (real database): the acceptance cases below plus the time-zone boundary (a write at 23:30 UTC for a tenant at UTC+2 stores the next local day) and idempotency of a replayed create with `status: "done"`.

## Scope

### In scope

- Setting, preserving and clearing `completedOn` and `completedAt`, the validation above, and the display-day helper.

### Out of scope

- Recurrence advance and what happens to a series on `done`/`notDone`: #1670.
- Marking a past occurrence as missed when reopened: #1668.
- Completing a task inside an event (`completedInEvent`): #1781.
- The first planned day (`since`): #1602.
- Client behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- When `PATCH {status: "done"}` is sent, then `completedOn` is today in the tenant time zone and `completedAt` is set.
- When `PATCH {status: "done", completedOn: "2026-10-14"}` is sent, then `completedOn` is 14 October.
- When `completedOn` is later than today, or is sent for a task that stays `notDone`, then the response is 400 and nothing is stored.
- When `{status: "done"}` is sent again on a completed task, then `completedOn` and `completedAt` are unchanged.
- When `{status: "notDone"}` or `{status: "wontDo"}` is sent, then `completedOn` and `completedAt` are null.
- When a task dated 22 October is completed on 16 October, then `completedOn` is 16 October, `taskDisplayDay` returns 16 October, and the task's `date` is still 22 October.
- When a task that moved on from 14 October to 16 October (`since` 14 October) is completed with `completedOn` 14 October, then `taskDisplayDay` returns 14 October.
- When a task dated 16 October is completed with `completedOn` 12 October (earlier than its planned day), then the write succeeds and `taskDisplayDay` returns 12 October.

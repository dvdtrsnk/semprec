## Context

Inbox is a real database internally but should appear as a simple daily list in Journal.

## Task

Register the `journal-inbox` view type. Query Inbox items through their `journalDay` relation and render them as an ordinary list within the matching daily Journal view. Cache the displayed derived state in `items.computed` through the established computed-value mechanism and invalidate/recompute it after relevant Inbox or proposal changes. Keep canonical stored keys English; resolve Czech/English labels through i18n.

## Scope

### In scope

- View registration, day scoping, list rendering, computed cache, and invalidation.

### Out of scope

- A generic Inbox-management screen and Shortcuts capture UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each Journal day displays only its related Inbox items.
- Cached state updates after capture, proposal transition, and deletion.
- The daily presentation does not expose internal database machinery by default.
- The view works in both supported locales.

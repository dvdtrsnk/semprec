## Context

Model tier rationale: Widens one shared membership definition (impact report and removal job) by one case, with no lock or write order change.

Found in the end-to-end test campaign after PR #2089. An IMAP pass that sees a vanished UID removes the Emails–Folders edge and marks the Email's meta row (`mail_message_meta.folder_copy_removed_at`); its final transaction, or a later pass's, decides whether the Email still has a folder copy and trashes it if not. When the mailbox is removed or its Mail is switched off before any pass decides it, the `mailboxRemoval` job (`mail/mailboxRemovalJob.ts`) never sees it: `MAILBOX_REMOVAL_MEMBERS_CTE_SQL` finds members only through edges to the mailbox's live Folders. The Email stays live with no folder edge, its thread stays listed, and on Mail-off its meta row is kept, so a later sync converges onto it instead of ingesting anew. No later pass decides it either: a removed mailbox has no sync state, and after Mail-off the folder sync states are gone.

Decision: the members CTE also takes the live Emails whose meta row names the mailbox, carries the mark and has no edge to any live Folder (an Email with a remaining copy in this mailbox is already a member; one with a copy in another mailbox's Folder is kept). The impact report counts them, the job trashes them like the other members, and Mail-off releases their meta rows.

## Scope

### In scope

- Membership of marked Emails without any folder edge in the removal and the Mail-off removal, and in the impact counts.

### Out of scope

- Emails without a meta row, and the IMAP pass's decision itself.

## Acceptance criteria

- After a removal or Mail-off, an Email of the mailbox whose last folder copy vanished undecided is trashed with the other members (with the mailbox's deletion time for a removal, its meta row deleted for Mail-off), and the impact report counted it.
- A marked Email of another mailbox stays live; the existing removal, impact and Mail-off tests stay green.

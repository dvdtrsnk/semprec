## Context

Model tier rationale: This reshapes how every speaker card is produced (a card for every unmapped speaker, with or without a suggestion and an envelope) and widens what a transcript card may carry and confirm (relations on an Event proposal, a new Person with the speaker edge), in the shared proposal confirm and revise paths; the role-switch and atomicity rules of those paths are easy to break and affect every card kind.

Today the transcription worker's speaker step (`runSuggestSpeakersStep` in `backend/services/semprec-transcribe/src/transcriptionTask.ts`, `backend/packages/data/src/transcription/transcriptSpeakerSuggestion.ts`, `backend/services/semprec-transcribe/src/speakerSuggestion.ts`) asks the model once, only when the transcript is linked to an Event with named participants, and creates a `transcript` card only for each speaker the model mapped to a participant. The card has a `relation` envelope through `speakers` with `{ speaker }` metadata, no reason, no quotes, no alternatives. A speaker the model could not place gets nothing, so the user is never asked "who is this speaker?", and the card cannot offer a person who is not a participant of the Event, nor a new person. The role of a transcript card (Event or speaker) is derived from its stored envelope, so a card without an envelope could be revised into an Event proposal.

This issue makes the question complete. Every unmapped speaker gets a card; the card carries what the user needs to decide (a reason, two or three quotes, when the speaker first talks, the source transcript, the alternatives) in its `context` (the property added by #1451); when the model is not sure the card is `needsClarification` with no envelope and the user's choice is a revise (#1870). It also covers the envelope work that cards need for this: an Event proposal on a transcript card may carry relations (the envelope relations and their atomic write on confirm of #1476, for example the project of a new meeting), and a speaker card may propose a new Person created together with the speaker edge. The tenant's own person (#1509) is always a candidate. The proposed meeting of #1818, the manual assignment of #1770 and the re-asking of #1942 build on this issue.

Decisions:

- By the product owner: candidates are the participants of the transcript's Event (with a name) and the tenant's own person, even when the own person is not a participant. A person already mapped to another speaker key is excluded from the suggestion and from the alternatives, because the model cannot map one person to two keys.
- The model is called once for all unmapped speakers and only when there is at least one candidate. With no candidate the cards are created without a call, as `needsClarification` cards with empty alternatives.
- Quotes are chosen by code, not by the model: up to three of the speaker's segments with at least six words, the longest first, then shown in time order, each shortened to 140 characters. `firstStartsAt` is the start of the speaker's first segment. The reason is written by the model in the tenant user's UI language (`users.locale`).
- `context.alternatives[].title` is always `null`: by the product owner, no job-title property is added in v1. `invitationStatus` comes from the Event participant edge or is `null`.
- By the product owner: one person under two speaker keys is not supported; a person already mapped to a speaker is not offered for another key.
- A speaker card keeps its role for life; the role of a card without a stored envelope is read from `context.role`.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `canonical-keys`, `ai-gateway`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Request and parser (`speakerSuggestion.ts`): `responseSchema` `{ mappings: [{ speaker, personId: string | null, reason }] }`, prompt as today plus the instruction to answer `personId: null` when unsure and to write `reason` as one short sentence in the given language. Parse with zod; ignore entries for unknown speakers or non-candidates (treated as unsure).
2. Context reading (`transcriptSpeakerSuggestion.ts`): `readSpeakerSuggestionContext` returns the unmapped speakers, the candidates (Event participants and own person, minus persons mapped to other keys) with the invitation status of each, and the data for quotes and source; it no longer returns `null` just because no Event is linked or no participant exists.
3. Cards (`proposeSpeakerMappings` and the step): create one `transcript` card per unmapped speaker under the existing idempotency key `transcript-speaker-suggestion:<transcriptId>:<speaker>` (an existing card of any status keeps the key from creating a second). With a confident candidate: status `proposed`, `relation` envelope as today. Otherwise status `needsClarification` and no envelope. The `context` is `{ role: "speaker", speaker, ordinal, quotes: [{ segmentIndex, text }], firstStartsAt, source: { transcriptName, date }, reason, alternatives: [{ personId, name, title, invitationStatus }] }`, validated by the context schema of #1451; the `reason` and `candidates` card properties are not used. The step still calls the model outside any transaction and writes the `suggestSpeakers` checkpoint with the cards in one transaction; it still writes no `speakers` edge.
4. Role without an envelope: in `proposalActions.ts` (`transcriptCardRole`, `assertValidEnvelopeForCard`) take the role from `context.role` when the card has no stored envelope, so a speaker card cannot be revised into an Event proposal, and a revised envelope must match that role.
5. Relations on Event proposals of transcript cards: `assertValidEnvelopeForCard` accepts the envelope `relations` of #1476 on a transcript card's Events `database` envelope; confirm writes the Event, those relations (through the atomic write of #1476) and the Transcripts `event` edge in one transaction. An invalid relation target writes nothing and fails with `400 validation_failed`.
6. New person on a speaker card: allow a `database` envelope targeting People (`name`, optional `relationship`) on a speaker card; confirm creates the Person and the `speakers` edge with metadata `{ speaker }` taken from `context.speaker`, in one transaction; the edge write keeps the existing speaker-edge validation.
7. Detail read: extend `openQuestions` in `transcriptDetail.ts` (#1576) so cards without a stored envelope count too (role and speaker read from `context.role` and `context.speaker`), with status `proposed` or `needsClarification`.
8. Tests: a transcript with three speakers (one confidently suggested, one unsure, one with no candidates) gets three cards with the right statuses, quotes, first start, reason and alternatives; the own person is offered without being a participant; a person mapped to another key is not offered; a retried step creates no second card or model call; revising an envelope-less speaker card to an Event envelope fails; the Event card with `relations { project }` confirms atomically and an invalid project writes nothing; the new-person card creates Person and edge atomically; transcript text is not logged.

## Scope

### In scope

- A card for every unmapped speaker with reason, quotes, first start, source and alternatives; envelope-less clarification cards; the role rule for them; relations on transcript-card Event proposals; the new-Person proposal with the speaker edge on confirm; the `openQuestions` update.

### Out of scope

- Adding the chosen person to the Event's participants on confirm, and remembering rejected speakers across a diarization rerun: #1671.
- Asking again when an Event is linked or gains participants, and refreshing clarification cards then: #1942.
- The revise route used to pick an alternative: #1870; recomputing a card from an instruction: #1940.
- Manual assignment from the transcript screen: #1770.
- The relationship catalog for the new person: #1549.
- A person under two speaker keys.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a transcript with an unmapped speaker is processed, that speaker has a card with two or three quotes, the time of their first utterance, a reason, the alternatives and the source transcript, even when the transcript has no Event.
- When the model is unsure, the card is `needsClarification` with no envelope and cannot be revised into an Event proposal; the user can pick an alternative through revise.
- When the card proposes a new person and is confirmed, the person and the speaker edge exist afterwards, created in one transaction.
- When a transcript card's Event proposal carries `relations { project }`, confirm writes the Event, the project relation and the transcript edge together, and an invalid project writes nothing.
- When the step is retried, no card is duplicated and no second model call is made.

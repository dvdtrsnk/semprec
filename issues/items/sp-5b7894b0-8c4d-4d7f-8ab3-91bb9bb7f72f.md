## Context

Model tier rationale: A query condition and the reauthorization path that must make a stopped account due again.

Review finding on the internet-accounts branch, verified in the code: `listCalendarAccountsDueForSync` (`calendar/sync/calendarSyncStore.ts`) selects every account whose status is not `ok`, so a `needsReauthorization` or `needsConsent` account is queued by every five-minute sweep, although `handleCalendarAccountSyncTask` documents that such a failure is not retried because only the user can change it. Each of those passes calls the provider with a refused credential or grant.

Leaving them out needs a way back: the password replacement already sets `needsReauthorization` to `never` and queues a pass, but an OAuth reauthorization (`replaceMailboxCredential` in `mail/mailOauthRouteHandlers.ts`) clears no calendar status and queues no calendar pass; today only the sweep picked such an account up again.

Decisions:

- The sweep selects accounts without a state row, `never` or `error`, and `ok` accounts last synced at least the due age ago.
- `clearCalendarSyncReauthorization` becomes `clearCalendarSyncCredentialRefusal`: `needsReauthorization` or `needsConsent` → `never`, failure record cleared. The password replacement and the OAuth reauthorization both call it, and the OAuth reauthorization queues `calendarAccountSync` with Calendars on, as the password replacement does.

## Task

2. `mail/mailOauthRouteHandlers.ts`: `replaceMailboxCredential` clears the refusal and queues the calendar pass with Calendars on.
3. `mail/mailboxPasswordRouteHandlers.ts`: the renamed call.

## Scope

### In scope

- The calendar sweep's selection and the reauthorization paths' calendar status.

### Out of scope

- The mail sweep, the pass's failure recording, the mailbox list's derived `calendarStatus`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The sweep queues no pass for a `needsReauthorization` or a `needsConsent` account, however long ago it last synced, and still queues never-synced, `error` and due `ok` accounts.
- An OAuth reauthorization of a mailbox with Calendars on whose calendar status is `needsReauthorization` or `needsConsent` sets it to `never` with no failure record and queues `calendarAccountSync` next to `mailAccountSync`.
- The existing calendar sync, password route and OAuth route tests stay green.

## Context

Model tier rationale: It changes what the offline layer answers for a kept read (projecting device-only captures into the diary's period answer) and when kept answers turn stale, across the outbox, the read path and the journal ViewModel; mistakes show stale or duplicated entries.

The owner tested build 84 of the Inbox capture outbox (`issues/handovers/sp-5809efdd-4261-49e1-96bc-a3b42602ec71.md`, ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-27-inbox-captures-wait-in-a-device-outbox-that-outlives-the-session.md`). Two defects in the diary's Inbox section:

1. Without a connection a capture (from the diary's capture row or from the Action-button shortcut) is counted in the sync banner but does not appear in the diary's Inbox section until the device is online again.
2. With a connection a capture does not appear in the diary's Inbox section either, even with the diary open; it appears only after switching to another tab and back.

Cause of 2: the outbox's successful send makes `OfflineLayer.changes` emit, and `JournalViewModel` reloads on changes, but the kept answer of `GET /api/journal/periods/day/<day>` is still fresh (kept less than 5 minutes ago with no change signal after it), so the reload is answered from the device without the new item. Adding a capture also does not announce anything, because it no longer goes through `AuthenticatedApi.send`.

## Task

1. **Captures shown at once.** Every answer of `GET /api/journal/periods/day/<day>` that the offline layer returns (kept or from the server) lists, after the server's Inbox entries of that day, the outbox captures of the signed-in owner whose day is that day and that the server's entries do not already contain, in creation order. Each projected entry has the capture's id, time, text, the emoji of its type when the kept answer of `GET /api/inbox-types` names it, its attachment's name, and the status `waiting` (a new value of the diary's Inbox status, `JournalInboxStatus.WAITING`, mapped from the wire value `waiting`, which the server never sends).
2. **Fresh after a capture.** Adding a capture and every successful send of one make the kept answers stale (as a successful write does) and make the layer's change flow emit, so every open diary reads its period again at once: offline it shows the projected entry, online the server's entry replaces it.
3. **Apple.** The diary's Inbox row shows a `waiting` entry with a clock symbol and the accessibility label "Waiting to be sent" (English and Czech).
4. Narrow the outbox ADR above with these two rules, in the same ADR file only if it is still unmerged on `develop`, else in a new ADR that narrows it (load `adr-conventions`).

## Scope

### In scope

- The projection into the day period answer, the stale-and-emit rule, the `waiting` status and its row.

### Out of scope

- Projecting captures into `GET /api/inbox/cards` or the badge counts.
- Opening attachments (a sibling issue of this batch).
- Android UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- Offline, a capture made for today appears in today's diary Inbox as `waiting` immediately, with its text, time, type emoji and attachment name, also when it was made by another SDK of the same process (the shortcut).
- Online, a capture appears at once as `waiting` and is replaced by the server's entry after the send, without leaving the diary; the server entry and the projected one never both show.
- A capture of another day appears only in that day's answer; captures of another owner never appear.
- Adding and sending a capture make kept answers stale and the change flow emit; tests prove each.
- The Apple row shows the clock symbol and label for `waiting`; the CoreKit mapping test covers the new status.

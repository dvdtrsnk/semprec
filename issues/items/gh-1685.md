## Context

Model tier rationale: One transaction both amends owner-controlled guidance under optimistic concurrency and changes a finding's state, with a schema migration, authorization by the guidance owner and a rule that a decided finding must stay decided.

A `behavior` finding says "the agent keeps doing X, and the instructions say nothing about it" (#1629); the owner is offered "add it to Allowed" or "forbid it". #1630 lists the active findings with `suggestedAllowedLine`, `suggestedNotAllowedLine` and `guidanceUpdatedAt`. Instructions are the four Markdown sections saved by `saveGuidanceSections` (#1548: owner only, `expectedUpdatedAt` check, 32 KiB limit, drift heartbeat kept). Findings are stored in `agent_guidance_drift_findings` with `status` `active|resolved` (migration 0035); `upsertActive` (`backend/packages/data/src/guidanceDrift/agentGuidanceDriftFindingsStore.ts`) reopens a resolved finding that has the same fingerprint.

This issue adds `POST /api/agent-guidance-drift/:id/resolve` with body `{ choice: "allow" | "forbid" | "dismiss", expectedGuidanceUpdatedAt }`, a custom route of the systemDatabases module manifest declared as `transactional-semantics` (`docs/adr/2026-09-11-module-manifest-custom-routes.md`), answering 200 `{ finding: { id, status }, guidance: { sections, updatedAt } }`.

Decisions:

- In one serializable transaction: load the finding (404 `not_found` if unknown or not active); `allow` appends the finding's `suggestedAllowedLine` to the `allowed` section, `forbid` appends `suggestedNotAllowedLine` to `notAllowed`, `dismiss` changes no guidance. The appended line is written as a Markdown list item `- <line>` on a new line at the end of the section (a missing or empty section gets just the item). The guidance save goes through `saveGuidanceSections` with the caller's `expectedGuidanceUpdatedAt`, so non-owners get 403 `owner_violation`, a stale value 409 `version_conflict` (same 409 body as the guidance route), and in both cases the finding stays active and nothing is appended.
- `allow` and `forbid` apply to `behavior` findings only. For a `permission` finding they answer 400 `validation_failed` with `details: { field: "choice", reason: "not_applicable" }`; `dismiss` is accepted for both kinds.
- The finding becomes `resolved` (for `allow` and `forbid`) with `resolved_at`, `resolved_by` (the caller) and `resolution_choice`; `dismiss` sets the new status `dismissed` and `resolution_choice = 'dismiss'`. A decided finding is never reopened: `upsertActive` leaves a `resolved` finding that has `resolution_choice` set, and a `dismissed` one, unchanged and reports no transition (a finding resolved by the nightly tick, without a choice, may still reopen).
- A `agent_guidance_drift_resolved` notification is written to the guidance owner for the manual resolve and carries `kind` and `resolutionTitle` in its payload: "Instructions of project {project} match the permissions again" for `permission`, "Instructions of project {project} match what Semp does again" for `behavior` (Czech and English strings through the notification i18n catalogs, in the owner's locale). The title is produced in the notification-writing seam (`backend/packages/notifications/src/guidanceNotificationWriter.ts`) from the payload's `kind` (defaulting to `permission` when absent), so the nightly auto-resolve notification gets its title without editing the tick. The manual resolve sends no push (decided by the product owner: the user just took the action and already knows); the suppression itself is not done here, it comes from the notification classification and push filtering work (#1440, #1443), where the resolved kind is never pushed.

Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for #1674, #1680 because both edit the same registry, manifest, cutover, route matrix or hook files listed in Touches.

## Scope

### In scope

- The resolve route, the migration, the no-reopen rule and the resolved notification title.

### Out of scope

- Listing findings: #1630. Creating and auto-resolving them: #1629.
- Enforcing a forbidden behavior (grants, approval requirements): not planned yet; it waits for a product decision. "Forbid" only writes an instruction line.
- Not sending push for resolved notifications (#1440, #1443) and showing the history or the web cards.
- Undoing a decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- When the owner resolves a `behavior` finding with `allow` and the current `expectedGuidanceUpdatedAt`, then the `allowed` section ends with the suggested line as a `- ` list item, the finding is `resolved` with `resolution_choice` `allow` and `resolved_by` the owner, and a later nightly tick with the same fingerprint does not reopen it.
- When the choice is `forbid`, then the line lands in `notAllowed`; when `dismiss`, then guidance is unchanged and the finding is `dismissed`.
- When another device saved the guidance meanwhile, then the answer is 409 `version_conflict`, no line is appended and the finding remains active.
- When the caller is not the guidance owner, then 403 `owner_violation` and nothing changes.
- When `allow` or `forbid` is sent for a `permission` finding, then 400 `validation_failed` with reason `not_applicable`.
- The resolved notification carries the kind-specific `resolutionTitle` in the owner's language, and tenant B cannot resolve tenant A's finding (404).

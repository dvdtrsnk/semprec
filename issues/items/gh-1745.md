## Context

Model tier rationale: The third use of an established lifecycle-hosting pattern with an existing webhook receiver and subscription client, differing only in configuration and the notification URL.

After #1739, the lifecycle dispatcher in `backend/services/semprec-api/src/mailLiveSyncLifecycles.ts` hosts IMAP IDLE and Gmail watch; Graph accounts still get the noop lifecycle and rely on the sweep. The Graph pieces exist: `createGraphWebhookLifecycleFactory` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/graphWebhookLifecycle.ts`) keeps one subscription per account alive (register, renew, re-register after a 404), `createGraphSubscriptionRestClient` (`mail/graphSubscriptionClient.ts`) talks to `/subscriptions`, and the public receiver `POST /api/mail/graph/webhook` is already routed in `backend/services/semprec-api/src/app.ts` (handled by `mail/graphWebhookNotifications.ts`). Graph accepts only a public HTTPS `notificationUrl`.

The Graph sync transport is #1508 (it also provides the refresh with rotation).

Decision: `notificationUrl` is `APP_BASE_URL` plus `/api/mail/graph/webhook`. When `APP_BASE_URL` is not an `https://` origin without a path (the local default is `http://localhost:3000`), or the Graph OAuth client is not configured, the Graph lifecycle is not hosted, one warning is logged, and Graph accounts stay on the noop lifecycle and the sweep.

## Task

Load `io-hardening`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. In `mailLiveSyncLifecycles.ts` set `hosted.graph_api` to `createGraphWebhookLifecycleFactory(pool, createGraphSubscriptionRestClient(getAccessToken), { getCredential, notificationUrl, onError })`: `getAccessToken` uses `refreshAndPersistAccessToken` (#1481) with `MICROSOFT_TOKEN_URL` and the Graph OAuth client; `getCredential` uses `getDecryptedCredential` (purpose `graph_subscription`); `onError` logs without credentials.
2. Compute `notificationUrl` and the "hosted or not" decision in a small pure function so it can be tested; log the single warning at startup when not hosted.
3. No change to the webhook receiver or to shutdown ordering beyond what `mailLiveSync.stop()` already does for lifecycles.
4. Tests with a fake Graph transport: a Graph account gets a subscription created with the right `notificationUrl`; renewal after a 404 registers a new subscription; stopping the root stops the lifecycle; with an `http://` or path-bearing `APP_BASE_URL`, or no client configured, the Graph lifecycle is not hosted and one warning is logged; other sync modes are unaffected.

## Scope

### In scope

- Hosting the Microsoft Graph subscription lifecycles and the notification URL rule.

### Out of scope

- The webhook receiver and its validation: unchanged.
- The Graph sync transport: #1508.
- Gmail and IMAP hosting: #1739, #1550.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a Graph account is active, then a subscription pointing to the deployment's `/api/mail/graph/webhook` is created and renewed, and a notification enqueues a sync without waiting for the sweep.
- When `APP_BASE_URL` is not a plain `https://` origin, or the Graph OAuth client is missing, then the process starts, logs one warning, and Graph accounts are synced by the sweep.
- When the process stops, then the Graph lifecycles are stopped with the root before the pool ends.
- When logs are inspected, then no token appears.

## Context

Model tier rationale: A main dump and a later deletion ledger encode different instants; choosing a precise recovery contract requires snapshot/provenance and replay evidence rather than weakening a consistency test.

Independent batch19 R1 at 7550fba23bcd86cc963287797d2ec07ed8832c13 found that a purge committing after the main dump snapshot but before the independent ledger query leaves a legitimate older tenant in the dump and its newer tombstone only in the ledger. The monthly test currently rejects that exact state/count. This issue owns F3 and corrects the earlier gh-1078 ordering/check assumption without rewriting it.

Apply `docs/adr/2026-11-03-later-replay-ledger-covers-snapshot-tombstones.md` within its exact scope. Selected contract: later replay ledger covers every exact restored main tombstone, permits newer additional IDs, and rejects tenant/tombstone coexistence. Keep backup order and actual newer deletion evidence. Add a separate strict artifact parser and file-only/database validator; preserve existing tenantDeletionLedger parser blank/dedup behavior and manual replay compatibility. The actual database side is one bounded read statement, with fixed safe diagnostics. Monthly validation is provenance/coverage only, not replay or activation; the final runbook executes actual replay after validation. No exported-snapshot owner, broad constraint change, accepted-history rewrite or fake count-only proof.

## Task

1. Before implementation, present the parent with a bounded source/tool feasibility comparison: either bind a snapshot ledger to the same main-dump database snapshot while retaining separate newest replay evidence, or expressly distinguish a newer replay ledger from the consistency evidence associated with a given main dump. Pin one supported concrete protocol, artifact identity and failure/cleanup contract in a parent-owned decision before source edits. Do not silently drop newer deletion evidence or weaken equality without replacing its claimed invariant.
2. Under the selected protocol, preserve a well-formed, bounded, tenant-ID-only newest replay ledger, its use after an older restore, fail-closed activation until replay succeeds and historical missing-ledger/RPO disclosure. A valid concurrent purge must not be called corruption merely because two observations have different snapshots.
3. Prove with a real controlled dump/purge/ledger sequence that a purge after the dump's snapshot cannot produce a false consistency PASS or an unexplained failure under the declared semantics. Also prove prior tombstones cannot disappear, malformed/duplicate/contradictory ledger evidence is refused, and failures leave no falsely successful backup or owned resource leak.
4. Update both backup/restore-test commands and their documentation only as required by the chosen protocol. Do not alter production purge, historical retention, tenant schema or restore activation guards.

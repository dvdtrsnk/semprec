**Blocked by:** #244, #175, #91

## Context

Issue #244 delivered the idempotent `provision.sh` with host packages, the service user, and the `/opt/semprec` tree. This slice completes provisioning with the systemd layer: the five service units, the timer/shell skeletons whose bodies later issues fill, and journald configuration. #177 (backup) and #178 (restore test) fill their timer bodies; #171 fills the dead-man switch; the trash purge timer wraps the purge task from #156.

## Task

Extend `provision.sh` so it also installs, idempotently:

- **Five service units**: `semprec-api`, `semprec-agents`, `semprec-mailsync@`, `semprec-transcribe`, `semprec-ai-gateway`. Each unit runs from the `current` release symlink, loads `/opt/semprec/shared/.env` via `EnvironmentFile=` (the shared-env contract from #175), and applies the loopback contract from #174 (all internal listeners bind to `127.0.0.1`).
- **Four timer/shell pairs as skeletons**: dead-man switch (body filled by #171), backup (#177), monthly restore test (#178), and trash purge. The skeletons install the timer schedule and a shell entry point; their bodies are filled by the issues named.
- **journald configuration**: `Storage=persistent` plus limits — the provisioning-level application of the retention values #171 defines at the app/logging level.

Rerunning must not duplicate units or timers and must not overwrite configured secrets or releases.

## Scope

### In scope

- Service units, timer/shell skeletons, journald configuration, unit verification, idempotence, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- Host packages, service user, and the `/opt/semprec` tree — delivered by #244.
- Timer bodies — #171, #177, #178.
- `deploy.sh`/CI — #190/#188.

## Acceptance criteria

- Units verify (`systemd-analyze verify` passes) and point to `current` plus the shared env file.
- All five services and four timers are installed after one run; a second run creates no duplicates and overwrites no secrets.
- journald is configured with `Storage=persistent` and the limits after the run.

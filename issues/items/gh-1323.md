## Behavior specification

Global search: item search documents, Czech matching, search operation.

## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions for job global-search-1

#### (a) Decisions adopted from open questions

- Recent searches: device only vs synced -> device only, backend stores nothing -> the recommended option; no new table. -> Decided by the product owner: recent searches stay on the device only and the backend stores nothing (privacy, lean v1).
- Company kind (supplier): add companies.kind vs drop -> no subtitle for it, no schema change. -> Decided by the product owner: the company kind is dropped; no schema change in v1.
- Calendar search (events and tasks by date): scope+sort vs `q` on database.query -> no separate calendar search. -> Decided by the product owner: the calendar uses the global search operation (#1842) with `scope` `event` and `task` (they are its groups); the calendar agenda only lays the returned results out by date.
- Global scope for Library, Health records, Recipes, Recordings and custom collections -> excluded (allow-list of seven database groups) -> the design omits them; custom collections are searched via `q` in the collection. -> Decided by the product owner: global search covers the seven groups; sensitive and module-specific parts stay excluded.
- Page/subpage hits: owner group with excerpt vs new group -> page text is indexed on the owner item, no new group. -> Decided by the product owner: a page hit is reported in its owner item's group with an excerpt; the excerpt is built by #1996 on top of #1658 and #1842.
- Hidden Pages database: omitted by the allow-list in 05 (decided by the product owner together with the page-hit rule above).
- Locale for select labels: tenant's earliest-created active user's `users.locale` (assumption; tenants are single-user today).
- Derived area in the document: builder follows project -> area relation itself instead of depending on the read-time derived area.
- Page text storage: two columns (`page_text`, `body`) instead of one `body`, so a journal-day rebuild after an Inbox change does not need to reconvert the page.
- `stripMarkdown(md, maxChars)`: implemented in 04 (not left to the Markdown serializer issue).
- `item.count` with `q`: applies `q`; `totalCount` of the two query operations ignores `q`.
- `mail`/`chat` scope values accepted in 05 but empty until their contributors land; thread-backed results carry `threadId` instead of item ids.
- Chat group is returned to human actors only (decided by the product owner).
- Backfill call placement: `db/runMigrationsCli.ts` after `grantQueueSchemaPrivileges` instead of `tenantCutoverMigrations.ts` -> the cutover runs before the queue schema exists, so it cannot enqueue.
- Re-shaping existing rows when `czech_simple` arrives: 03's migration deletes non-Emails index rows (derived data) and the per-deploy backfill (`onlyMissing`) rebuilds them; Hunspell activation transition triggers a full non-Emails reindex.

#### (c) User-visible behavior

The overview search finds items across tasks, events, projects, areas, people and companies, the journal and files, ignoring diacritics and capitals, matching inflected forms and words typed only in part, with every word required. Text on pages and in journal days is found too, within about a minute of an edit. Results come grouped in a fixed order, four per group with the real total, or all of one type with paging, and carry structured subtitles (date, time range, project, people, phone and so on) that each client renders in the user's language. A hit in a page's text is reported in its owner item's group with an excerpt. The calendar searches events and tasks through the same operation and lays the results out by date. Recent searches stay on the device. Renames of a project or area, moving a project to another area, removing a person from an event, and deleting or restoring items update the search results shortly afterwards. Trashed items, system projects, archived databases and other tenants never appear, and an agent only sees databases it has been granted. Inside a collection, a search field narrows rows together with the active filter without changing the collection's item count.

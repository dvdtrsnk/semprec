## Behavior specification

Sleep: module, validation, plans, nights, editing, alarm, mute Semp and context.

This batch was planned from the reviewed design of the iOS app: every issue below states the backend behavior the app needs that `develop` does not provide yet. The issues are linked as sub-issues; their order follows the dependency graph across all batches, foundations first.

Status: drafts awaiting the `/define-behavior` Phase 5 audit. No issue in this batch carries `spec:approved` or `agent:ready` yet.

## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions: sleep-1 (module, validation, plans, nights, push)

#### (a) Decisions adopted from open questions

- Owner of the sleep databases (system project "Sleep" or the Semprec project) -> one new system project item "Sleep" in Projects with `systemActive: true`, owning all three databases -> follows the library module pattern and gives the module its own agent text and grants later.
- Default plan names in the seed -> seeded in the tenant user's language like the other seeded names (Czech "Pracovní dny" and "Víkend" by default, English "Weekdays" and "Weekend" for an English user); afterwards they are user data and are not localized -> Decided by the product owner: Czech "Pracovní dny" and "Víkend", created in the user's language (Czech by default) like the rest of the seed, then plain user data without localization (#1776).
- Existing-tenant delivery of the module -> the generic system-module pattern: manifest, seed for new tenants and one per-tenant cutover step in `runTenantCutoverMigrations` for existing ones, as Recipes does (#1707) and as `runTranscriptsCatalogCutoverMigration` already does -> the design note asked to reuse the generic mechanism and justify any cutover.
- Hiding the sleep databases from collections -> no `overviewOrder`/`switcherOrder`, so `clientVisible` is false; no `hidden` column -> as the design note specifies.
- Enforce "at least one plan" and day collision after a restore on the server? -> No in this batch; the resolver takes the lower `position`, ties by lower `id` -> Decided by the product owner: not in this batch; on a collision the lower position wins.
- Plan title versus label derived from days -> `name` stays a stored title -> nothing in the batch derives labels.
- `durationMinutes` by wall clock, real, or both -> wall clock only (night of 24 Oct 2026 is 510 min); real instants are in `bedAt`/`wakeAt` -> Decided by the product owner: as stated, together with the two rules below.
- Bedtime before 12:00 with an earlier wake time, and bed equals wake -> wake day is always night + 1, no 400, duration 0 when wake is not after bed -> Decided by the product owner: as stated.
- Wake time after 12:00 -> allowed, the server has no upper bound -> Decided by the product owner: as stated.
- Time zone while traveling -> the tenant's time zone is used -> Decided by the product owner: the tenant's time zone; device-reported zones and a Profile row are not planned.
- Retention of past night exceptions -> kept as history, no pruning job -> Decided by the product owner: past exceptions are kept as history.
- Reset a night to plan -> PUT with all fields null keeps the item with null fields (`changed` false), nothing is deleted -> consistent with 30-day restore of deleted items.
- If the custom PUT route is rejected in review -> fall back to POST with Idempotency-Key plus PATCH with ifVersion, night read from GET; not built here.
- iPad receives the resync push and rings the alarm too (registrations of platform `ios` are not distinguished) -> Decided by the product owner: yes, the iPad rings too; there is no per-device setting.
- Visible bedtime reminder on the web / server-side kind -> not built (local notifications on iOS only) -> Decided by the product owner: local notifications on iOS only.
- Response of PUT for a night with neither plan nor exception -> `{ night: null }`.

#### (b) Open questions that remain for the product owner

- Is the Sleep card tied to a chosen area (relation `sleepSettings.area`, who sets it, which area type) or does the client keep the fixed rule "Health area"? `#1833` is only to be implemented after this decision; without it no relation or `areaId` should exist.

#### (c) User-visible behavior

Every user gets a Sleep feature backed by three locked system collections that do not appear among the user's collections: one settings record (plan on or off, goal, preparation reminder, mute Semp, alarm sound, show in calendar), sleep plans keyed by the day the user wakes (default "Pracovní dny" Mon to Fri 23:00 to 06:45 with an alarm, and "Víkend" Sat and Sun 00:00 to 08:30 without, named in the user's language, "Weekdays" and "Weekend" in English), and optional exceptions for single nights. Values are checked on every write (goal 7 to 9 hours, reminder 15/30/45/60 minutes, allowed alarm sounds, valid times), there is only one settings record and one exception per night, and a day can belong to only one plan: moving a day into a plan takes it away from the other plan in the same save. The app can read the planned nights for any range of up to 62 days in the user's time zone (including daylight-saving changes) and change or reset a single night with one call. After any change from the web, another device or Semp, the user's iPhone gets a silent wake-up push (at most one per 30 seconds) so it can reschedule its alarm and reminders. Optionally, once the product decides, the settings can point to the area that shows the Sleep card.

### Decisions: sleep-2 (mute Semp in the sleep window, agent tools, context ref)

#### (a) Decisions adopted from open questions

- Hold or send quietly during sleep -> hold the push until wake time (notification row and realtime frame are created at once) -> Decided by the product owner: hold until wake time; no quiet APNs variant.
- Earlier wake time after a hold -> the originally scheduled time stands; a later wake time is honored because the window is re-checked when the deferred job runs -> Decided by the product owner: as stated; already deferred pushes are not rescheduled.
- Exception from muting -> notification category `approvals` from the kind-to-category map of #1440, no second constant -> Decided by the product owner: approvals are delivered during sleep.
- Resolver or read failure -> push is sent immediately and the failure is logged without content -> never lose a push.
- Move local task reminders on the phone? -> yes, as an iOS contract line added to #1838 (not moved: bedtime reminder and alarm) -> Decided by the product owner: yes, they are moved to wake time.
- Time zone while traveling -> tenant time zone (as in sleep-1) -> Decided by the product owner: the tenant's time zone.
- Approval for the agent night tool -> `sleepNightSet` needs no approval (non-destructive write) and is usable only when the user has granted the run's project access to the sleep databases -> Decided by the product owner: no approval, but the tool is available only with a granted access to the sleep data (#1840).
- Capabilities -> `sleep.read` and `sleep.write` declared by the sleep manifest; plans and settings are changed with the generic `item.patch`.
- Context of the area card and overview circle -> client decision; backend only accepts a night date. Plan circle uses a normal `item` ref.
- Access to the virtual `sleepNight` ref -> run grant, else the one-run read access of #1849 extended to this kind, else the ref is rendered as unavailable.
- Agent guidance text of the Sleep project -> not assigned to any issue; out of scope -> Decided by the product owner: not planned.

#### (b) Open questions that remain for the product owner

- Does #1849 allow the virtual `sleepNight` ref as a one-run grant, or is a project grant always required?
- Which night should the area card and the home overview circle attach as context (today's night or the settings)?

#### (c) User-visible behavior

With the sleep plan on and "Mute Semp while sleeping" switched on, notifications that arrive between the bedtime and the wake time of the user's resolved night (including a changed night) still appear in the app, but the phone and browser pushes are held and delivered at wake time; approval requests are always delivered immediately, local task reminders and calendar alerts on the phone move to wake time, and the bedtime reminder and the alarm (scheduled on the phone) are not affected. If the sleep data cannot be read, pushes go out immediately. Semp can read and change nights (for example "move tomorrow's alarm to 7:00") when the project has access to the sleep data; the change changes one night only, leaves the plan unchanged, and refreshes the phone's alarm like any other edit. The user can attach a night (from the calendar strip, the night circle or the preparation marker) to a message to Semp, which then sees that night's times, plan and exception and can edit them.

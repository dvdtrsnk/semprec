## Context

Model tier rationale: Platform authentication session with a custom URL scheme and a state handshake through the backend.

Epic decision: `alldots://oauth` with `ASWebAuthenticationSession`.

## Task

Load `kmp-shared`, `shared-viewmodel`, `apple-swiftui`, `mobile-navigation`, `canonical-keys`, `testing` and `mobile-verify`.

1. Register the URL scheme `alldots` in the app Info.plist (Tuist manifest).
2. Shared: `oauth/start` with `redirectTarget: "app"` and the chosen services; `oauth/complete` with state and code; reauthorize with services.
3. Apple: a DesignSystem-free wrapper in the App target or a feature module presenting `ASWebAuthenticationSession` with callback scheme `alldots`, returning the URL to the shared ViewModel.
4. Account detail: "Sign in again" when status needs reauthorization; "Allow calendar access" when calendar status needs consent.

## Scope

### In scope

- Google and Microsoft connect, reauthorize and consent on iOS.

### Out of scope

- macOS sign-in (follows the iOS flow later).

## Acceptance criteria

- A Google account can be added from the phone and starts syncing mail and calendars.
- A cancelled sign-in leaves nothing behind.
- "Allow calendar access" turns the calendar status to syncing.

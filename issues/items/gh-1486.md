## Context

Model tier rationale: Atomic multi-write confirmation must preserve rollback, stale-state guards, idempotency and acting-user semantics.

Confirm already locks the card and is transactional/idempotent; #1476 adds relations and #1479 validates multi-step envelopes. This issue executes their active steps atomically.

Decision: active steps are executed in dependency order (a step before every step that refers to its `ref`); skipped steps (disabled, or optional and unreferenced) are neither validated again nor written. `create` writes the item through `createItemWithClient` and its relations through `createRelationWithClient`; targets given as `{ ref }` use the id of the item created by that step. `link` writes the declared edges between existing items. `update` moves an existing item's "one"-side relation: `deleteRelationWithClient` of the old edge (`before`) and `createRelationWithClient` of the new one (`after`) in the same transaction. The `properties` of an `update` step are written with `updateItemWithClient` as the acting user, so the choke point applies the same validation, approval and risk rules as to any other item update (decided by the product owner; a project's status is not a special case). Any failure rolls the whole transaction back, leaves the card `proposed` and returns 400 with `details: { ref, field }`. `resultItemId` and `resultLabel` are those of the primary step; the response is the card as before. Per-step results are reported by #1648 and are not stored here.

Decision: confirm now passes the acting user (`actingUserId`) to the item writes so realtime notifications go only to that user, and the writes are made as that user. The route handler (`createConfirmProposalRouteHandler` in `inboxRouteHandlers.ts`) supplies the id from the authenticated identity; callers without a user (tests, internal) pass none, which keeps today's behavior. Product policy: confirmed blocks will be user-authored under #1681 (including legacy pageContent) and #1981 (journalEntry), with #1648 block ids. Those later Tasks/AC own the switch explicitly; retain Task 2 single-step behavior here, including the interim author divergence recorded by the accepted agent-page-write-policy ADR. Acting-user item writes and invalidations remain required now.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a company-plus-new-person proposal is confirmed, then the company, the person and the `People.companies` edge exist after one transaction, and `resultItemId` is the company's.
- When the person step fails, then nothing is created and the response is 400 with `details.ref` naming the person step.
- When the follow-up task step of a health record proposal is disabled, then only the health record is created; when the optional "new" person step is no longer referenced, then no person is created.
- When an `update` step moves a project, then the old `Projects.area` edge is gone and the new one exists.
- When an `update` step changes a project's `status`, then the project has the new status after confirm, and a failure of another step leaves the old status.
- When confirm is repeated, then it returns the confirmed card and creates nothing.
- When the route handler confirms, then realtime invalidation is addressed to the acting user.

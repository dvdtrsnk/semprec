**Blocked by:** #1476, #1479
**Model tier:** high

## Context

Model tier rationale: Confirm becomes a multi-write unit including moves of existing items (delete plus create of an edge) under one lock, with rollback, idempotency and acting-user semantics all in play on the one path that writes user data from a proposal.

`confirmProposalWithClient` (`backend/packages/data/src/inbox/proposalActions.ts`) locks the proposal, is idempotent for an already confirmed card, writes in one transaction, and returns one `resultItemId` and `resultLabel`. #1476 made it write the primary item's relations; #1479 validates multi-step envelopes (`operations` with `create`, `update` and `link` steps, per the ADR #1378). This issue makes confirm execute all active steps atomically.

Decision: active steps are executed in dependency order (a step before every step that refers to its `ref`); skipped steps (disabled, or optional and unreferenced) are neither validated again nor written. `create` writes the item through `createItemWithClient` and its relations through `createRelationWithClient`; targets given as `{ ref }` use the id of the item created by that step. `link` writes the declared edges between existing items. `update` moves an existing item's "one"-side relation: `deleteRelationWithClient` of the old edge (`before`) and `createRelationWithClient` of the new one (`after`) in the same transaction. The `properties` of an `update` step are written with `updateItemWithClient` as the acting user, so the choke point applies the same validation, approval and risk rules as to any other item update (decided by the product owner; a project's status is not a special case). Any failure rolls the whole transaction back, leaves the card `proposed` and returns 400 with `details: { ref, field }`. `resultItemId` and `resultLabel` are those of the primary step; the response is the card as before. Per-step results are reported by #1648 and are not stored here.

Decision: confirm now passes the acting user (`actingUserId`) to the item writes so realtime notifications go only to that user, and the writes are made as that user. The route handler (`createConfirmProposalRouteHandler` in `inboxRouteHandlers.ts`) supplies the id from the authenticated identity; callers without a user (tests, internal) pass none, which keeps today's behavior. Decision (product owner): blocks inserted by a confirmed proposal are authored by the user who confirmed it, and the card records that Semp proposed them; the page-content and journal confirm paths that insert blocks apply this, and the per-step results (#1648) list the inserted block ids.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Add `actingUserId` to `confirmProposalWithClient`'s signature (optional), pass it to `createItemWithClient` options and have `createConfirmProposalRouteHandler` provide the caller's user id.
2. Implement the step executor in `proposalActions.ts` (or a new `backend/packages/data/src/inbox/proposalSteps.ts` imported by it): ordering by refs, skipped-step rule, `create`/`update`/`link` as decided, mapping `ref` to created item ids, error wrapping with `ref` and `field`. A single-step envelope bypasses it and behaves exactly as before, including the credential, transcript-edge and page-content paths.
3. Tests: company plus new person creates company, person and the `People.companies` edge in one transaction; a failing person rolls back the company and the edge and the 400 carries the person's `ref`; with the follow-up task step of a health record proposal disabled only the health record exists; with the "new" person removed (optional and unreferenced) no person exists; an `update` step moves a project from one area to another (old edge gone, new edge present) and rolls back with the rest on failure; an `update` step setting a project's `status` to `done` writes it through the choke point; `resultItemId` is the primary step's item; a repeated confirm creates nothing twice; realtime invalidations carry the acting user.

## Scope

### In scope

- Atomic execution of `create`, `update` and `link` steps in confirm and passing the acting user.

### Out of scope

- Validation of steps (#1479) and of single-step relations (#1426).
- Per-step confirm results and origin of created items (#1648, #1659).
- Moving an attachment into Files on confirm (#1703).

## Acceptance criteria

- When a company-plus-new-person proposal is confirmed, then the company, the person and the `People.companies` edge exist after one transaction, and `resultItemId` is the company's.
- When the person step fails, then nothing is created and the response is 400 with `details.ref` naming the person step.
- When the follow-up task step of a health record proposal is disabled, then only the health record is created; when the optional "new" person step is no longer referenced, then no person is created.
- When an `update` step moves a project, then the old `Projects.area` edge is gone and the new one exists.
- When an `update` step changes a project's `status`, then the project has the new status after confirm, and a failure of another step leaves the old status.
- When confirm is repeated, then it returns the confirmed card and creates nothing.
- When the route handler confirms, then realtime invalidation is addressed to the acting user.

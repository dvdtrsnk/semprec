## Context

Model tier rationale: One mechanism (BackgroundTasks) in the app target over the SDK entry of sp-5440a887-f4a6-4c91-b8d8-64937880de5b; scheduling and completion rules are known, the uncertainty is the system's own timing.

The outbox of the batch's first issue sends captures while the app's process runs. After an Action-button capture made offline the app is suspended, so nothing sends it until the app runs again. iOS offers `BGTaskScheduler`: a processing task with `requiresNetworkConnectivity` runs when the device has a network, and an app-refresh task runs opportunistically; neither time is guaranteed. A background `URLSession` would upload without the app but would bypass the shared data layer (`docs/adr/2026-10-24-mobile-backend-calls-through-shared-data-layer.md`) and is not chosen here.

## Task

1. Add an ADR under `docs/adr/` (load `adr-conventions`) deciding that the Apple app sends waiting captures from `BGTaskScheduler` tasks through the shared SDK, and why a background `URLSession` was rejected for now.
2. Register, at launch on iOS, a processing task `cz.alldots.app.inboxCaptures.send` (`requiresNetworkConnectivity = true`) and an app-refresh task `cz.alldots.app.inboxCaptures.refresh`; both run `InboxCaptureSDK.send()` through the bridge of sp-5440a887-f4a6-4c91-b8d8-64937880de5b, end when it returns or when the system expires the task (cancelling the send), and schedule themselves again when captures still wait.
3. Schedule both after a capture is added by the intent or the diary while captures wait, and when the app moves to the background with captures waiting. Add `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers` and the `fetch` and `processing` background modes to the app's Info.plist in `Project.swift`.
4. When the app becomes active, run a send through the bridge.
5. macOS has no `BGTaskScheduler` for apps; there only item 4 applies.

## Scope

### In scope

- Registering, scheduling and running the two tasks, sending when active, the Info.plist keys and the ADR.

### Out of scope

- A background `URLSession`, silent pushes for captures, Android.

## Acceptance criteria

- With captures waiting, moving the app to the background schedules both tasks; with none waiting, nothing is scheduled.
- A task run sends through the SDK, completes with success when nothing waits and reschedules when something does; an expired task cancels its send and completes.
- Becoming active sends the waiting captures.
- The Info.plist holds both identifiers and the background modes; the macOS build has no BackgroundTasks code path.

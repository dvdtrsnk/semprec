## Context

Model tier rationale: One known mechanism (a transactional diff of relation edges plus commit-coupled job enqueue) across two custom routes with existing route, isolation and hook fixtures, with the job contract and the edge metadata contract defined by its blockers.

#1757 defines the participants of an Event as `Events.people` edges whose metadata holds a role and an optional invitation, and lets the client read them. This issue adds the writes the participants sheet needs: replace the list of people on an Event in one request, optionally inviting the new ones by email, and invite one already linked person. #1774 owns the sending: it exports `enqueueEventInvitation` in `backend/packages/data/src/calendar/eventInvitationJob.ts`, which enqueues a `REQUEST` or `CANCEL` job inside the caller's transaction.

Routes over Events belong to the systemDatabases module (single-writer ownership ADR `docs/adr/2026-09-10-single-writer-ownership-model.md`), so both routes are declared in `backend/packages/data/src/seed/systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts`, like the transcript routes there. A custom route is justified as `transactional-semantics`: the edge changes and the job enqueue must commit together.

Behavior of `PUT /api/events/:id/people` with body `{ people: [{ personId }], sendInvites: boolean, mailboxId?: uuid }`:

- `people` is the full desired list of non-organizer participants. It is diffed against the current edges in one transaction. The organizer edge is never removed by the diff.
- A newly added person who has an email address (the first line of `People.emails`) and `sendInvites` true gets an edge whose metadata carries `invitation: { status: "pending", email, mailboxId }` (written with the system writer kind) and a `REQUEST` job. A newly added person without an address, or with `sendInvites` false, is only linked: an edge with no invitation.
- A person who stays in the list keeps their edge and metadata unchanged and is not invited again.
- A removed person whose edge carries an invitation produces a `CANCEL` job; their edge is deleted in the same transaction. A removed person without an invitation is only unlinked.
- If an invitation is sent and the Event has no organizer edge yet, the organizer edge (role `organizer`, to the person #1509 links to the tenant's user) is created in the same transaction.
- The response is `{ people: [...same items as the read endpoint...], invited: n, linkedOnly: n }`, where `invited` counts new pending invitations and `linkedOnly` counts newly linked people without one.

Behavior of `POST /api/events/:id/people/:personId/invite` with body `{ mailboxId }`: the person must already be linked to the Event, must have an email address, and must not have an invitation; it then writes the pending invitation and enqueues the `REQUEST`.

Decision: the mailbox is chosen per request. `mailboxId` is required whenever an invitation is to be sent (otherwise 400 `validation_failed`, field `mailboxId`), and it must be a live Mailboxes item of the tenant. No default mailbox setting is built.

Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for #1780, #1786 because both edit the same registry, manifest, cutover, route matrix or hook files listed in Touches.

## Scope

### In scope

- The two routes, the transactional diff of participant edges, the pending invitation metadata, and enqueueing the `REQUEST` and `CANCEL` jobs.

### Out of scope

- The edge metadata validation and the read endpoint: #1757.
- Building and sending the messages, retries, and recording `sentAt`: #1774.
- Recording replies: #1950.
- A default mailbox for invitations in settings: not in v1 (product owner decision); the mailbox is chosen on each send.
- Sending a cancellation when an edge is removed through the generic relation endpoint rather than this route: not built.

## Acceptance criteria

- When two new people (one without an email) are saved with `sendInvites` true, then the response has `invited` 1 and `linkedOnly` 1 and exactly one `REQUEST` job is queued.
- When `sendInvites` is false, then both people are only linked, no invitation is stored and no job is queued.
- When an invited person is removed, then in one transaction their edge is deleted and one `CANCEL` job with the same `UID` is queued.
- When any step fails, then no edge changes and no job is queued.
- When an invitation is to be sent and `mailboxId` is missing or not a mailbox of the tenant, then the response is 400 `validation_failed`.
- The invite endpoint on a linked person with an email stores a pending invitation and queues one `REQUEST`; on a person who is already invited it is 409 `conflict`.

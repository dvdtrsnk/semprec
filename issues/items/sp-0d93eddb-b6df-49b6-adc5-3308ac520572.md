## Context

Model tier rationale: Changes the calendar list grouping, editing rules and the new-event calendar picker.

Calendars of internet accounts must appear grouped by account with their status, colour override and read-only state.

## Task

Load `kmp-shared`, `shared-viewmodel`, `apple-swiftui`, `mobile-navigation`, `canonical-keys`, `testing` and `mobile-verify`.

1. Shared: `CalendarKind.ACCOUNT`, `accountId`, `readOnly`, `remoteName`, `remoteColor` from the calendars query; colour resolves to the local override or the remote colour.
2. Calendar list grouped by account (account name and provider icon as the section header, sync status line); account calendars can be renamed and recoloured locally, not deleted; read-only ones show a lock.
3. New item: writable account calendars are choosable.
4. Pull to refresh in the calendar triggers `POST /api/accounts/:id/calendars/sync` for accounts with calendars, and the app does it when it becomes active (debounced).

## Scope

### In scope

- Account calendars in the list, colours, read-only and the picker.

### Out of scope

- Event editing of recurrence exceptions beyond the existing editor.

## Acceptance criteria

- A synced Google calendar appears under its account with the provider colour.
- A read-only calendar cannot be chosen for a new event.
- Recolouring keeps the local colour after the next sync.

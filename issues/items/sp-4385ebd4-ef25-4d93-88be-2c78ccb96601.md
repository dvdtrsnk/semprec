## Context

Model tier rationale: Replaces a time throttle in a shared domain use case with a coalescing of concurrent calls, including cancellation of the caller, and keeps every calendar ViewModel's request semantics.

Follow-up of the 15 s server debounce: `SyncAccountCalendars` (`mobile/shared/domain`) drops a call made within 60 s of the previous one. Every calendar ViewModel of one `CalendarSDK` calls it: opening the calendar, the app coming back to the front, and pulling the calendars sheet to refresh. So within a minute a pull to refresh after an external edit sends nothing, and nothing says so. The server coalesces requests close together on its own (one pass per 15 s, later requests deferred to the end of the window and never dropped), so a client-side pause only loses requests.

Decisions:

- **Every call is sent.** The time pause is removed.
- **Concurrent calls coalesce, none is dropped.** A call made while a round of requests is under way waits for that round. All calls that waited are then served by one more round, so the request that serves them leaves after they were made. Suppressing a call while one is in flight would lose a request whose answer was already on its way when an external edit happened, so the waiting call is sent rather than suppressed.
- **Cancellation.** A round whose caller is cancelled, or whose accounts could not be read, serves nobody, so the next waiting call sends its own.
- **User-visible state.** The use case is a nudge whose outcome is the account's calendar status. The screens show nothing new: the account screen's explicit "Sync now" already shows `syncRequested` while its request is under way (unchanged), and the calendars sheet reads its list again on every pull. No exported type changes.

## Scope

### In scope

- When the shared layer sends the accounts' calendar sync requests.

### Out of scope

- The server's debounce, the account screen's "Sync now", any new screen state, Android and Apple UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three calls one after another send three rounds.
- Calls made while a round is under way wait, and all of them are served by exactly one more round.
- When the caller of a running round is cancelled, a waiting call still sends its own round.
- A refused or missing sync of one account does not stop the others or the next call; accounts that could not be read let the next call through.
- Every pull to refresh of the calendars sheet, and each refresh of the calendar, asks for the accounts to sync; a server change never does.

## Context

Model tier rationale: v2 validation beside legacy pageContent with existing fixtures. The real schema/type/stored parser owner is inbox/proposalEnvelope.ts; inboxTickAction re-exports it. Legacy properties {flavour, fields?, children?} continue to validate and confirm unchanged on any live item. V2 is discriminated by markdown and uses {markdown, placement, heading?}: nonempty page-Markdown from #1608, no page block; end/start or an existing h1-h3 afterHeading on the target. afterHeadingText is tick-input only (#1649), never stored/revise. V2 targets only projects, areas, Journal days or Pages subpages; absence of a document is valid. Use the existing noncreating page summary (#1615); do not create or compact. Confirm stays 409 until #1681 implements placement, and disappeared headings then conflict. #1959 supplies manual options later.

## Task

Load `error-handling`, `state-writes` and `testing`.

1. Extend the `pageContent` schema in `inboxTickAction.ts` with the v2 shape and its types; keep the old shape typed and parsed unchanged. `parseStoredProposalEnvelope` accepts both.
2. In `assertValidProposalEnvelope`, branch on the shape: for v2 validate `markdown` (non-empty, parser from #1608, no `page` blocks), `placement` (known values only), `heading` (string up to a sensible length, no newline), `afterHeading` against the target's headings, and the page-host rule above. Throw `ValidationError` (400) with `field: "properties"` or `"target"`.
3. In `assertValidEnvelopeForCard` (`proposalActions.ts`) accept v2 on a non-transcript card and keep rejecting `pageContent` on a transcript card.
4. Revise (`reviseProposalWithClient`) accepts a v2 envelope and may switch between the old and the new shape.
5. Until #1681 lands, confirm of a v2 card answers 409 (explicit branch) rather than treating it as the old shape.
6. Tests in `proposalActions.test.ts` and `inboxTickProposal.test.ts`: v2 valid; markdown with a `page` block gives 400; `afterHeading` of a non-heading or foreign block gives 400; target that is not a page host gives 400; an old-shape envelope still validates and confirms as before.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a v2 envelope has an empty `markdown`, a `page` block or invalid Markdown, then writing it by the tick and by revise gives 400.
- When `placement.afterHeading` is not an `h1`-`h3` block of the target page, then 400.
- When the target is not a project, area, Journal day or Pages sub-page, then a v2 envelope gives 400.
- When the target has no page document yet, then a valid v2 envelope is accepted.
- When an envelope in the old shape is written, revised or confirmed, then the behavior is identical to today.
- When a v2 card is confirmed before the write issue lands, then the answer is 409 and nothing is written.

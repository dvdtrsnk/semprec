- Empty/invalid `markdown` or a `page` block: direct validation and revise throw ValidationError (400); an eligible background tick stores needsClarification without the invalid envelope.
- Invalid target-page h1-h3 `placement.afterHeading`: the same validator/revise 400 and background clarification outcomes apply.
- A target outside projects, areas, Journal days or Pages subpages has those same outcomes. Existing stale, locked and unchanged-fingerprint guards remain in force.
- When the target has no page document yet, then a valid v2 envelope is accepted.
- When an envelope in the old shape is written, revised or confirmed, then the behavior is identical to today.
- When a v2 card is confirmed before the write issue lands, then the answer is 409 and nothing is written.

## Context

Model tier rationale: One shared ViewModel over a status flow the queue already provides, its CoreKit bridge and one banner in the Apple app with copy; existing patterns throughout.

Since `[offline-first 03/07]` a change made without a connection waits in the queue and a change the server refuses is dropped and recorded as a failure (`OfflineLayer.status`: the queued count and the failures, with a call that forgets them). The person sees neither: an offline app looks like an online one, and a dropped change disappears silently. The Apple app shows its signed-in tabs inside `SessionGate`; copy lives in String Catalogs and the copy policy (`mobile/scripts/architecture-policy.json`).

## Task

Load `kmp-shared`, `shared-viewmodel`, `apple-swiftui`, `canonical-keys`, `testing` and `mobile-verify`.

1. `:shared:domain` declares the port `OfflineStatusSource` (a flow of `OfflineStatus(offline, waiting, failed)` and `forgetFailures()`); `OfflineLayer` implements it (offline is the layer's offline state).
2. `SyncStatusViewModel` in `:shared:presentation` (`SyncStatusState(offline, waiting, failed)`, action `DismissFailures`, no effects) follows the source in `viewModelScope`; `SyncSDK.statusViewModel()` creates one per call. Without a data directory the status is always online with nothing waiting.
3. Apple: CoreKit's `SyncStatusScreenModel`, its bridge, `ScriptedSyncStatusScreenModel` and mapping tests; the app shows above the signed-in tabs of a scene a banner while offline ("Offline · showing saved data", or "Offline · 3 changes waiting" with the count) and one while failures exist ("2 changes could not be saved" with a dismiss button); nothing when online with no failures. Copy in en and cs, plural forms, keys in the copy policy as Apple-only; identifiers in `AppIdentifiers`.
4. Tests: the ViewModel's states and the dismissal (commonTest); the CoreKit mappings; the banner states in `AlldotsTests`.

## Scope

### In scope

- Showing the offline state, the waiting count and the failures, and dismissing the failures.

### Out of scope

- Retrying or editing a failed change; Android.

## Acceptance criteria

- Without a connection the app shows that it is offline and how many changes wait; once they are sent the banner goes away.
- A change the server refused shows as a failure until dismissed.
- Online with nothing waiting nothing is shown.

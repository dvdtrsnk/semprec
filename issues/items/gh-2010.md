## Context

Model tier rationale: One new rule kind added to an existing rule schema, its date computations and one preset of an existing route, following the patterns of the other fixed rule kinds with existing fixtures; no new lifecycle, locking or persisted-data contract.

The new-item menu offers "yearly" recurrence, but the task recurrence rules in `backend/packages/data/src/tasks/taskRecurrenceRule.ts` know only fixed `weekdays`, `monthDates`, `nthWeekday` and floating `{unit, n}`; there is no rule for one date per year. #1665 adds `PUT /api/tasks/:id/recurrence` with the presets `daily`, `weekdays`, `weekly` and `monthly`, or `{mode, rule}` in the rule schema. #1670 adds the date functions `firstOccurrenceAfter` and `latestOccurrenceOnOrBefore` in `backend/packages/data/src/tasks/nextDueDate.ts`, which the advance (#1670), the projection of virtual occurrences (#1672), single occurrences (#1720) and the midnight close (#1677) use.

Decision (product owner): yearly recurrence is one fixed rule kind `yearDates` with a month and a day, because it is one general rule and yearly recurrence is common (anniversaries, fees). A date that does not exist in a given year (29 February) is skipped in that year, the same way `monthDates` skips months without the requested day.

## Task

Load `canonical-keys`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. In `taskRecurrenceRule.ts` add the fixed rule kind `{ kind: "yearDates", month, day }` (`month` integer 1 to 12, `day` integer 1 to 31) to `fixedRecurrenceRuleSchema`; a day that never exists in that month (for example 31 April or 30 February) is a validation error.
2. In `nextDueDate.ts` handle `yearDates` in every computation over fixed rules (the fixed-date step used by `computeNextDueDate` if it still exists, and `firstOccurrenceAfter` and `latestOccurrenceOnOrBefore` from #1670): the dates are the given month and day of each year, and a year without that date (29 February outside a leap year) has no occurrence.
3. In the PUT route of #1665 add the preset `yearly` = `yearDates` with the month and day of the task's `date`; with a `{date}` in the body the month and day are recomputed from the new date, as for `weekly` and `monthly`. The `recurrence` property of the task reports `preset: "yearly"` for such a rule.
4. Tests: the rule schema accepts `{month: 3, day: 15}` and `{month: 2, day: 29}` and rejects month 13, day 0 and 31 April; `firstOccurrenceAfter` of a 15 March rule from occurrence 15 March 2026 gives 15 March 2027; a 29 February rule from 29 February 2028 gives 29 February 2032; `latestOccurrenceOnOrBefore` of a 15 March rule anchored 15 March 2025 with today 16 October 2026 gives 15 March 2026; PUT `{preset: "yearly"}` on a task dated 22 October 2026 stores `yearDates` month 10 day 22; PUT `{preset: "yearly", date: "2026-12-24"}` stores month 12 day 24.

### Out of scope

- Client menus and labels for the yearly option.

## Acceptance criteria

- When PUT `{preset: "yearly"}` is sent for a task dated 22 October 2026, then the stored rule is `yearDates` with month 10 and day 22 and the response's `recurrence` has `preset: "yearly"`.
- When the occurrence of 22 October 2026 of that series is completed, then the next occurrence is dated 22 October 2027.
- When a yearly rule is for 29 February, then the next occurrence after 29 February 2028 is 29 February 2032.
- When a rule `{kind: "yearDates", month: 4, day: 31}` or a month outside 1 to 12 is sent, then the response is 400 and nothing changes.

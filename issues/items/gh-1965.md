**Model tier:** high

## Context

Model tier rationale: It adds a new background job in the media-processing process that runs ffmpeg over untrusted uploads, new blob references and a re-trigger of the Inbox tick, so resource limits, cleanup ownership and tenant scope all matter.

An Inbox card and the day summary show an attachment as a small preview with facts: "JPG - 2.1 MB", the image size, the audio length "23:14", and when a voice recording was made. The assistant also needs a reduced copy of a photo to read. The bytes and mime type are known after upload (#1963), but nothing computes dimensions, a preview or a duration: `blobs` holds only `mime_type`, `byte_size` and `content_hash`. `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` may run only in `semprec-transcribe` (`docs/adr/2026-09-22-ffmpeg-confined-to-semprec-transcribe.md`; the only spawn site is `backend/services/semprec-transcribe/src/mediaNormalization.ts`), whose queue lane is `transcribe` (`backend/packages/queue/src/index.ts`).

This issue adds a job there that runs after an attachment is attached and records its metadata. It is the one place that classifies an audio attachment: a short audio note is a voice note, a long one a recording.

What builds on it: #1970 reads the reduced image, #1980 transcribes voice notes, #1985 routes recordings, #1969 returns metadata in the day summary.

Decisions:
- Images (JPEG, PNG) and audio (M4A, MP3) only. PDF page counts and PDF previews need a renderer and are not computed here; a PDF attachment gets mime type and size only, with `pages` null. Reason: the product decided that PDF attachments get no preview and no page count, consistent with Files, which has no previews.
- Image previews are 128 px on the long side (shown at 64 pt on retina screens) as JPEG; the reduced copy for the assistant is a JPEG with the long side at most 1568 px, never enlarged. Both are stored as ordinary blobs and are counted references.
- The audio length comes from `ffprobe`; the recording time is the media `creation_time` tag when present. The device-supplied values of a client are not accepted; the probe is authoritative.
- `audioKind` is `voiceNote` when the duration is less than 120 seconds and `recording` at 120 seconds or longer, as required by #1978. The boundary is the constant `INBOX_RECORDING_MIN_SECONDS = 120` in this issue's module and is the only definition.
- When the job finishes it re-triggers the Inbox item's `onItemEvent` heartbeat (`triggerOnItemEventHeartbeats`) so a card computed earlier is recomputed with the facts.

## Task

Load `io-hardening`, `state-writes`, `db-migrations`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Migration `backend/packages/data/src/db/migrations/NNNN_inbox_attachments_metadata.sql (next free ordinal at commit time)`: add to `inbox_attachments` the columns `metadata jsonb`, `thumbnail_blob_id uuid` and `reduced_blob_id uuid` (nullable, composite tenant foreign keys to `blobs`, indexes on `(tenant_id, thumbnail_blob_id)` and `(tenant_id, reduced_blob_id)`). Additive only.
2. Extend `deleteBlobIfUnreferenced` to count `thumbnail_blob_id` and `reduced_blob_id`, and make `deleteInboxAttachment` and the permanent-delete path release a row's derived blobs too (storage keys returned for after-commit removal).
3. In `packages/queue/src/index.ts` add the core task `inboxAttachmentMetadata` (affinity `transcribe`, tenant lane), enqueue it from the capture transaction of #1963 once per attachment (job key `inbox-attachment-metadata:<attachmentId>`), and register its handler in the transcribe runtime (`backend/services/semprec-transcribe/src/queueRuntime.ts`).
4. Implement the handler in `backend/services/semprec-transcribe/src/inboxAttachmentMetadata.ts`, calling `ffprobe`/`ffmpeg` only through `mediaNormalization.ts` helpers (add `probeImage` and `renderImageJpeg` there, with the same timeout, partial-output cleanup and output validation as the existing helpers). For images: width, height, preview and reduced JPEG as new blobs. For audio: duration, `recordedAt`, `audioKind`. Write `metadata` once (`{ width?, height?, durationSeconds?, recordedAt?, audioKind?, pages: null }`) and the blob ids; a rerun changes nothing. A file that cannot be read is recorded as `metadata.error` and never fails the capture.
5. Export `INBOX_RECORDING_MIN_SECONDS` and add `metadata`, `thumbnailBlobId` and `reducedBlobId` to the attachment reader of #1564.
6. After the metadata is stored, call `triggerOnItemEventHeartbeats` (event `update`) for the source Inbox item in the same transaction.
7. Tests with real media fixtures: a JPEG gets dimensions, a 128 px preview and a reduced copy no larger than 1568 px; a small image is not enlarged; an M4A of 40 s is `voiceNote` and one of 23 min is `recording`; a corrupt file records `metadata.error`; a rerun is a no-op; a foreign tenant's attachment is not processed; deleting the attachment frees both derived blobs; the job runs only in the transcribe runtime.

## Scope

### In scope

- The metadata job for images and audio, derived blob references, the audio classification and the re-trigger of the Inbox tick.

### Out of scope

- PDF page counts and previews: not planned (decided; Files has no previews either).
- HEIC conversion: not planned (the client converts HEIC to JPEG before upload).
- Reading the image with the assistant: #1970; transcribing voice notes: #1980; routing recordings: #1985.
- Accepting client-supplied duration or recording time.
- Returning metadata in cards and the day summary: #1969.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a photo is attached, then after the job its metadata has width and height, a preview blob of at most 128 px and a reduced copy of at most 1568 px on the long side.
- When a 40-second voice note and a 23-minute recording are attached, then `audioKind` is `voiceNote` and `recording`, and the duration is recorded.
- At the boundary, a 119-second attachment is `voiceNote`, while 120-second and 121-second attachments are `recording`; exercise all three durations in the metadata job's tests.
- When the file is corrupt, then `metadata.error` is set and the Inbox item is unaffected.
- When the attachment is deleted, then its preview and reduced blobs are deleted if nothing else uses them.
- When the job runs twice, then the second run changes nothing.

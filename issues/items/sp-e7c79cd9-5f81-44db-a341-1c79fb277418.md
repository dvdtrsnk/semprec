## Context

Model tier rationale: Adds a calendar kind with ownership and editing rules other modules rely on, plus tenant tables holding remote identity.

Calendars have `kind` own, system or subscription; only own calendars are written by users. A calendar synced from an internet account is a new kind that belongs to an account, may be read-only at the provider, and needs remote identity and sync tokens.

Decision: kind `account`; a relation `Calendars.account` ↔ `Mailboxes.calendars` (one-to-many, system-owned by the calendar module).
Decision: the provider's name and colour are system-owned `remoteName` and `remoteColor`; the user-owned `color` and `name` remain and override them locally when set (`name` and `color` null mean the remote value). `readOnly` is system-owned.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `db-migrations`, `error-handling`, `io-hardening`, `canonical-keys` and `testing`.

1. Seed: add kind `account`, the `account` relation, system-owned `remoteName`, `remoteColor`, `readOnly` on Calendars; allow `name` and `color` null for kind `account` only.
2. Rules in `calendarRowHooks.ts`/`calendarTrashHooks.ts`: an account calendar cannot be made default, deleted or trashed by a user (it goes when the provider or the account removes it); its local `name`, `color`, `notify` and `hidden` are user-editable.
3. Event creation (`defaultCalendarCreateHook.ts`, `createEventInCalendar`): a writable account calendar is a valid chosen calendar; a read-only one is refused with `calendar_read_only`. Event writes (update, trash, move) in a read-only account calendar are refused with `calendar_read_only` unless the writer is `calendarSync`.
4. Side tables `calendar_remote_state` (calendar item id PK, `remote_id`, `remote_url`, `sync_token`, `ctag`, `last_synced_at`, `last_error_code`) and `event_remote_state` (event item id PK, calendar item id, `remote_id`, `remote_href`, `etag`, `base_snapshot` jsonb of the last synced field values) with tenancy, RLS, FKs to items with cascade and grants; unique `(tenant_id, calendar_item_id, remote_id)`.
5. A system writer identity `calendarSync` (`chokePoint/writerKind.ts` process list) that may write the system-owned fields above and events in read-only account calendars.
6. Timeline: entries from writable account calendars are not `readOnly`; read-only ones are. The calendars list exposes `kind`, `accountId`, `readOnly`, `remoteName`, `remoteColor`.
7. Update `docs/architecture/domain-calendar-tasks-and-media.md` (Tenancy, Calendars, Event creation, Timeline): external calendars also arrive through internet accounts and sync both ways.

## Acceptance criteria

- A user cannot delete, trash or make default an account calendar; renaming or recolouring it changes only the local override.
- Creating, changing or trashing an event in a read-only account calendar answers `calendar_read_only` for users and agents and succeeds for `calendarSync`.
- An event can be created in a writable account calendar.
- The side tables are tenant-isolated (shared isolation harness) and removed with their items.

## Context

Model tier rationale: Ref graphs, skipped steps and typed updates span proposal parsing, validation and confirmation.

#1378 defines the multi-step contract; #1426 covers existing-item relations and #1486 confirms steps.

Decision: relation targets inside a step may be an item id (string) or `{ ref: "<step ref>" }`; the id-array shape of #1426 stays valid. Decision (product owner): an `update` step is a general write of an existing item: it may change relations ("a project moves from one area to another") and properties (for example a project's `status`; it is not a special case). Its `properties` are checked with the same rules as an item update of that item through the choke point (writable, user-owned properties of the item's database, values valid for their type), and `before` must hold the current value of every property and relation the step changes (including Task 6 deferred-allocation preconditions). Required-field and hidden-field rules for steps come with the editor schema work, not here.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a proposal for a company and a new person is stored, then both steps survive parsing and validate.
- When the person step fails validation, then the response is 400 and `details` carries that step's `ref` and the failing `field`.
- When two steps refer to each other, then validation fails.
- When a step is disabled or an unreferenced optional step, then its invalid payload does not fail validation.
- When an `update` step changes a project's `status`, then the envelope validates; when an `update` step writes a system-owned property, then validation fails and `details` carry its `ref` and `field`.
- A single-step envelope is accepted and serialized exactly as before.

- Graph, validation and execution cover the same effective after.relations changes; invalid after-only targets, missing before coverage and conflicting representations fail with the step ref and field.

- Disabled/unreferenced optional payloads are skipped, but empty/duplicate refs and multiple primaries are globally rejected.

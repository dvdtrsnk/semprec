**Blocked by:** #1378, #1426
**Model tier:** high

## Context

Model tier rationale: Adds a graph-shaped envelope (refs, cycles, skipped steps) to the schema that every proposal writer and reader parses, where a parsing mistake silently drops steps or lets a malformed graph reach confirm.

#1378 records the multi-step envelope: an optional `operations` array of steps (`ref`, `op` of `create`/`update`/`link`, `databaseKey` or `itemId`, `properties`, `relations`, `role`, `optional`, `enabled`; `update` steps carry `before` and `after` and may change properties and relations), with the top-level `entityKind`, `target` and `properties` equal to the primary step so single-step readers keep working. `proposalEnvelopeSchema` (`backend/packages/data/src/inbox/inboxTickAction.ts`) is a `z.object`, so today it silently drops an `operations` key on every parse. #1426 added `relations` and the target-side link check for one step. This issue makes the schema carry `operations` and makes `assertValidProposalEnvelope` validate every active step as a single-step envelope would be, plus the graph rules. Writing the steps on confirm is #1486.

Decision: relation targets inside a step may be an item id (string) or `{ ref: "<step ref>" }`; the id-array shape of #1426 stays valid. Decision (product owner): an `update` step is a general write of an existing item: it may change relations ("a project moves from one area to another") and properties (for example a project's `status`; it is not a special case). Its `properties` are checked with the same rules as an item update of that item through the choke point (writable, user-owned properties of the item's database, values valid for their type), and `before` must hold the current value of every property and relation the step changes. Required-field and hidden-field rules for steps come with the editor schema work, not here.

## Task

Load `error-handling`, `io-hardening` and `testing`.

1. Extend `proposalEnvelopeSchema` / `ProposalEnvelope` with the optional `operations` array as recorded in the ADR, with the relation target union above. Existing single-step envelopes parse and serialize unchanged.
2. In `assertValidProposalEnvelope` validate, when `operations` is present: refs unique and non-empty; exactly one step with `role: "primary"` whose `op`, database and `properties` equal the top-level envelope; every `{ ref }` target names another step of the same proposal; the ref graph has no cycle; a `create` step names an existing, writable database by `databaseKey` and its `properties` and `relations` pass the same checks as a single-step `database` envelope (including the relations check from #1426, with `{ ref }` targets skipped as not yet existing); an `update` or `link` step names an existing item and passes `assertRelationTargetLinkableWithClient` for its relations; `update` carries `before` and `after` covering every changed key, and its `properties` pass the same checks as an item update of that item.
3. Steps with `enabled: false`, and `optional` steps that no active step refers to any more, are skipped: not validated, and not counted when checking refs of other steps (a ref to a skipped step makes the referring step skipped only if it is itself optional, otherwise a `ValidationError`).
4. Errors carry `details: { ref, field }` of the failing step.
5. Tests: a company-plus-new-person envelope validates; a person step failing validation reports its `ref` and `field`; a cycle is rejected; a ref to a missing step is rejected; two primaries are rejected; a disabled step with an invalid payload is ignored; an optional step nobody refers to is ignored; an `update` step changing a project's `status` validates; an `update` step writing a system-owned property, or changing a key that is missing from `before`, is rejected with its `ref` and `field`; a single-step envelope is byte-for-byte unchanged by parse and stringify; an envelope with `operations` survives parse.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `operations` schema and its validation including graph rules and skipped steps.

### Out of scope

- Confirming the steps (#1486).
- Computing steps in the tick (#1492) and editing step switches (#1920).
- Required and hidden fields per step.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a proposal for a company and a new person is stored, then both steps survive parsing and validate.
- When the person step fails validation, then the response is 400 and `details` carries that step's `ref` and the failing `field`.
- When two steps refer to each other, then validation fails.
- When a step is disabled or an unreferenced optional step, then its invalid payload does not fail validation.
- When an `update` step changes a project's `status`, then the envelope validates; when an `update` step writes a system-owned property, then validation fails and `details` carry its `ref` and `field`.
- A single-step envelope is accepted and serialized exactly as before.

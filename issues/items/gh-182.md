**Blocked by:** #246, #181

## Context

Last of three parts of the recording-preparation slice of the `batch-transcription` batch (approved behavior in #179). #245 delivered the `semprec-transcribe` process, step 0, and the checkpointing step runner; #246 delivered the normalized 16 kHz mono Opus audio with duration and creation time. This part adds the two paid stages — diarization and ASR — using the gateway functions from #181, and makes their results resumable. #183 consumes the checkpointed diarization turns and raw ASR chunks for the pure merge step.

## Task

- Add step 2 (`diarize`): call `gateway.diarize()` (pyannoteAI, #181) over the **entire** normalized recording in one call; diarization is never chunked. Persist the returned speaker turns `[{ speaker: 'SPEAKER_00', start, end }]` immediately into the declared computed checkpoint for this stage.
- Add step 3 (`ASR`): call `gateway.transcribe()` (DeepInfra Whisper large-v3, #181) over the normalized audio split into 20-minute chunks with a 30-second overlap. Language is detected on the first chunk and passed explicitly to every later chunk; the detected language is kept with the ASR checkpoint so #183 can store `computed.language`. Persist each chunk's raw result immediately after that chunk finishes into the declared computed checkpoint for this stage (per-chunk granularity, so a crash mid-stage repeats only the unfinished chunks).
- Resume semantics: on a repeated run, the step runner from #245 skips diarization if its checkpoint exists and skips every ASR chunk whose checkpoint exists; an already-paid gateway call is never repeated.
- Overlap deduplication and merging are not performed here — the raw chunks are the input for #183's pure merge function.

## Scope

### In scope

- Steps 2 and 3, whole-recording diarization, chunked ASR with explicit language propagation, immediate per-stage/per-chunk checkpoints, resume without repeated calls, tests, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- Process bootstrap and step 0 — #245; ffmpeg/ffprobe preparation — #246.
- Merge, overlap dedup, summary, `done` — #183; Event matching — #184; retry, rerun, notifications — #186.

## Acceptance criteria

- Diarization runs exactly once over the whole recording and its turns are checkpointed before ASR starts.
- ASR chunks are 20 minutes long with a 30-second overlap; every chunk after the first receives the language detected on the first chunk; each chunk result is checkpointed right after it completes.
- A crash after diarization (or after some ASR chunks) resumes after the last checkpoint: the repeated run makes no repeated gateway call for any stage or chunk already checkpointed (verified with a fake gateway counting calls).
- Every gateway call of these stages is metered as an audio call per #181 (`audio_seconds`/`cost_usd` set, token columns and `agent_run_id` NULL).

**Blocked by:** #140

## Context

Login works through the common session model from #140, but nothing yet limits password guessing. #139 created the `login_attempts` table; this issue makes the login choke-point record into it and enforce an exponential lockout before any password is verified. #141 then adds platform-specific token delivery and the native bridge on top of the same login endpoint.

## Task

Record every login attempt with normalized email and request IP. Before password verification, apply an exponential lockout window to failed attempts for the same email-plus-IP pair; above the configured threshold reject even a correct password, and reset the active failure sequence after success.

## Scope

### In scope

- Attempt recording, exponential lockout, success reset.

### Out of scope

- Cookie/Bearer delivery contracts, platform mismatch rejection, and the WKWebView bridge contract — delivered by #141.
- Public signup, CAPTCHA, proxy-level rate limiting.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every successful and failed login attempt is persisted in `login_attempts` with the normalized email and the request IP.
- Threshold tests prove lockout is scoped to email plus IP, grows exponentially, rejects a correct password while active, and resets after success.

## Context

Harvest issue #934 collected three reports of one defect in `backend/packages/agent-runtime/src/__tests__/lifecycleAdapter.test.ts` (PR #920 line 261, PR #920 line 267, PR #920 line 270): `failEventsFor(...statuses: string[])` interpolates its arguments, wrapped in hand-written single quotes, into a `CREATE TRIGGER … EXECUTE FUNCTION` statement. DDL cannot take bind parameters, and every caller passes the literals `"done"` or `"error"`, but the signature accepts any string, so a value containing a quote would break or inject into the SQL.

## Task

In `lifecycleAdapter.test.ts`, change the parameter type of `failEventsFor` from `string[]` to an array of a literal union of the terminal statuses it is called with (`"done" | "error"`), so only those values can reach the interpolated SQL.

## Touches

- backend/packages/agent-runtime/src/__tests__/lifecycleAdapter.test.ts

## Scope

### In scope

The signature change; call sites keep passing `"done"` and `"error"` unchanged.

### Out of scope

Every other finding of harvest issue #934 and anything adjacent to the lines named in the Task, even in the same file.

## Acceptance criteria

- `failEventsFor("x'y")` does not type-check (`tsc` reports it), while the existing calls with `"done"` and `"error"` still compile and the file's tests pass.

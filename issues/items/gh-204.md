## Task

Use concurrency-safe arbiter for partial unique non-null provider_message_id equivalent to ON CONFLICT(provider_message_id) WHERE provider_message_id IS NOT NULL DO NOTHING while retaining message_id upsert. On conflict, skip the colliding insert without a raw PostgreSQL error, preserve the first existing item/message identity unchanged, and continue synchronization.

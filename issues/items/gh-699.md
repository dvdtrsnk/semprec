## Context

The four long-running units — `deploy/systemd/semprec-api.service`, `semprec-agents.service`, `semprec-transcribe.service`, `semprec-ai-gateway.service` — all carry, in their `[Unit]` section (lines 5-6), `StartLimitIntervalSec=5min` and `StartLimitBurst=3`, with `Restart=on-failure` / `RestartSec=5s` in `[Service]` (lines 18-19). Every one of these processes exits non-zero the moment Postgres is unreachable at startup (`ensureQueueSchema` / `createDispatcher` / `resolveStartupConfig` throw into `installFatalHandlers`), and until sibling issue F2a of this batch lands, also the moment an idle pool connection is dropped. Three such exits inside five minutes — roughly fifteen seconds of a Postgres restart or a container recreate — move the unit to `failed`, systemd stops restarting it, `semprec-failping@%n` fires, and nothing brings the service back after the database returns until a human runs `systemctl reset-failed`. `deploy/README.md` line 107 documents the current "up to three times in five minutes".

`provision.sh` installs these files verbatim (`install_systemd_units`) and `deploy/provision.test.sh` already asserts individual unit lines with `grep -qx` (lines 109-120), so the new values can be pinned there.

Issue #624 adds a `TimeoutStopSec=` line to the `[Service]` section of `semprec-ai-gateway.service`; this issue edits only the two `StartLimit*` lines in `[Unit]` of each file and leaves `RestartSec` alone so the two edits cannot collide.

## Task

1. In each of `deploy/systemd/semprec-api.service`, `semprec-agents.service`, `semprec-transcribe.service` and `semprec-ai-gateway.service`, replace lines 5-6 with `StartLimitIntervalSec=15min` and `StartLimitBurst=180`. With `RestartSec=5s` unchanged this means a crash loop shorter than fifteen minutes never parks the unit (180 restarts × 5 s = 15 min), while a permanently broken unit still stops restarting — and still triggers `OnFailure=semprec-failping@%n.service` — after fifteen minutes. Do not touch `RestartSec`, `Restart` or anything in `[Service]`.
2. In `deploy/README.md`, rewrite the sentence on line 107 ("The four long-running services restart up to three times in five minutes.") to state the new bound: restarts every 5 s for up to 15 minutes (180 attempts) before the limit is exhausted and `/fail` is pinged.
3. In `deploy/provision.test.sh`, immediately before the final `echo 'provision.sh idempotence test passed'` line (after the `missing-asset` case, line 150), add a loop over `semprec-api semprec-agents semprec-transcribe semprec-ai-gateway` asserting `grep -qx 'StartLimitIntervalSec=15min'` and `grep -qx 'StartLimitBurst=180'` on the installed `$TEST_ROOT/systemd/system/$unit.service`. Place it there, not next to the existing per-unit greps at lines 109-120, because sibling issues edit those lines.

## Acceptance criteria

- When one of the four services crash-loops for ten minutes and then starts successfully, systemd never reports it `failed` and no `semprec-failping@` run is triggered.
- When a service crash-loops for more than fifteen minutes, the unit reaches `failed` and `semprec-failping@%n.service` runs once, as today.
- `bash deploy/provision.test.sh` passes and fails if any of the four installed units lacks `StartLimitIntervalSec=15min` or `StartLimitBurst=180`.

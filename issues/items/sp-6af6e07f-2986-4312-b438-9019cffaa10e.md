## Context

Model tier rationale: One more erase step in the tenant purge, on a private table of the queue library that the purge does not own, guarded by a lock on the queue library's hot job table and raced against a third-party function (`add_job`). A wrong predicate deletes another tenant's queue or a queue that a running job still holds, a missing lock leaves a job that no worker ever selects, and a lock held too long stalls every enqueue of every tenant.

Finding RH-1 (low) of the gh-1086 rehearsal on a scratch `postgres:16-alpine` 16.15 cluster: after `handleTenantPurgeTask` completed for two tenants, `graphile_worker._private_job_queues` still held one row per lane the tenant had used, named `<lane>:<tenantId>` (`agent-run:<id>`, `value-links:<id>`, `mail-thread-project:<id>`). A forensic grep of `pg_dump --data-only` found the deleted tenant id four times: the tombstone and these three rows; no email, file, mail, subject or secret marker. The checklist of gh-1086 and `docs/adr/2026-10-18-tenant-purge-erases-outside-the-choke-point.md` say only a tombstone remains.

Verified for this source at `e6387779b` (identical tree to the integration tip): queue names come from `tenantLane(lane)` (`backend/packages/data/src/tenancy/tenantLane.ts`), which returns `` `${lane}:${tenantId}` `` inside a tenant scope. `purgeQueuedJobs` in `backend/packages/data/src/tenancy/tenantPurgeJob.ts` completes the tenant's unlocked jobs through `graphile_worker.complete_jobs` and never touches the queue rows; `finalise` asserts that no tenant table holds a row and then erases the identity rows, writes the tombstone and deletes the `tenants` row in one transaction. graphile-worker 0.17.3 creates a queue row on `add_job` (`insert ... on conflict do nothing`) and refers to it from `_private_jobs.job_queue_id`; it never deletes a queue row. The purge runs as `alldots_data`, which holds DML on every `graphile_worker` table through `alldots_side` (`grantQueueSchemaPrivileges`).

`add_job` takes no lock on a queue row and `_private_jobs.job_queue_id` has no foreign key (graphile-worker 0.17.3 `sql/000011.sql`: `job_queue_id int null, -- not adding references`), so a job that a still-open transaction has enqueued into the lane is invisible to a `NOT EXISTS` check, and deleting its queue row leaves a job whose `job_queue_id` points nowhere; `getJobs` selects only jobs whose queue is null or available, so that job never runs. Measured by the challenge of this source's decision on two connections: the row-locking `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE OF q SKIP LOCKED` plus `DELETE` removed the queue row while the enqueue was open, and after both commits the job had `job_queue_id = 1`, no queue row and was not selectable. With `LOCK TABLE graphile_worker._private_jobs IN SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE MODE` under a lock timeout the purge failed with `55P03` while the enqueue was open, deleted nothing once it had committed, and an `add_job` that started while the purge held the lock waited and then re-created the queue row with a selectable job.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `error-handling`, `testing` and `adr-conventions`.

1. In `finalise` (`tenancy/tenantPurgeJob.ts`), inside its transaction, delete the tenant's idle queue rows: `DELETE FROM graphile_worker._private_job_queues AS q WHERE right(q.queue_name, 37) = ':' || $1::text AND q.locked_at IS NULL AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM graphile_worker._private_jobs AS j WHERE j.job_queue_id = q.id)`. Run this as the last statements of the finalise transaction (after the `tenants` delete), and first take `SET LOCAL lock_timeout = '2s'` and `LOCK TABLE graphile_worker._private_jobs IN SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE MODE`: add_job takes no lock on a queue row and `job_queue_id` has no foreign key, so without the table lock a job enqueued by a still-open transaction is invisible to `NOT EXISTS`, and deleting its queue row leaves a job no worker ever selects. The lock waits for such a transaction (or times out, which fails the run and is retried by the next sweep) and is held only until the commit that follows. Log the count with step `queues` as the other steps do (count only, no queue name).
2. Right after that DELETE, in the same transaction, check the remaining rows: any remaining row of `graphile_worker._private_job_queues` whose name ends with `':' || tenantId` fails the run with `Tenant purge left rows behind in: graphile_worker._private_job_queues`, naming the table and never a queue name or row. A failure rolls the whole finalisation back, including the identity erase, the tombstone and the `tenants` delete. The tenant then stays `deleting` and the next sweep retries, as for any other remaining row; a queue held by a running job is erased on a later run once the job has finished.
3. Tests in `backend/packages/data/src/__tests__/tenantPurge.test.ts`:
   - In "erases a deleting tenant completely, leaves a tombstone and leaves another tenant untouched", enqueue jobs for both tenants through `tenantLane` queues before the purge; afterwards no `_private_job_queues` row ends with the purged tenant's id, and the other tenant's queue rows are unchanged.
   - New: a purged tenant whose lane queue is locked (`locked_at` set, with a locked job in it) keeps `status = 'deleting'`, has no tombstone, and the run reports the table name; after the lock is cleared and the job removed, the next run completes and leaves no queue row of the tenant.
   - New: a queue of another tenant whose name merely contains the purged tenant's id somewhere other than the `:<tenantId>` suffix is not deleted.
   - New: while another connection holds an open transaction that enqueued a job into the tenant's lane, finalisation fails (lock timeout or remaining queue row), the tenant stays `deleting`; after that transaction commits and the job is removed, the next run completes, and no `_private_jobs` row has a `job_queue_id` without a queue row.
4. Run `node .github/scripts/adr.mjs --platform backend` and add `docs/adr/<date>-tenant-purge-erases-the-tenants-queue-rows.md` (`status: accepted`, `area: [backend]`, `supersedes: []`) that extends `[[2026-10-18-tenant-purge-erases-outside-the-choke-point]]` and `[[2026-10-08-per-tenant-queue-lanes]]` without editing them: the purge is the one writer outside graphile-worker that deletes `_private_job_queues` rows, only idle rows of the `:<tenantId>` suffix with no job, as the last statements of the finalise transaction under `LOCK TABLE graphile_worker._private_jobs IN SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE MODE` with `lock_timeout = '2s'`; the bounded stall this lock causes (every `add_job` of every tenant waits at most until that commit, and the purge gives up after the lock timeout and retries on the next sweep); why this is safe against graphile-worker's `add_job` and job selection (no lock on a queue row, no foreign key on `job_queue_id`); and that a held queue defers finalisation.

## Scope

### In scope

- Erasing the deleted tenant's idle `graphile_worker._private_job_queues` rows in the purge, and refusing to finalise while one remains.

### Out of scope

- Any write to another `graphile_worker` table (the purge only takes the table lock on `_private_jobs`), queue naming, `tenantLane`, the jobs step, and graphile-worker's own schema or version.
- The tombstone itself (`tenant_deletions` keeps the tenant id by design), backups and restored snapshots.
- The restore tooling of `[security-gate 03/06]`.
- Any production deployment or purge run, any change to gh-1086 itself, any change under `web/` or `mobile/`, and web or mobile verification.

## Acceptance criteria

- When a deleting tenant whose lanes left idle queue rows is purged, then no `graphile_worker._private_job_queues` row ends with `:<its id>` and only the tombstone in `tenant_deletions` carries its id.
- When another tenant has queue rows, then the purge leaves every one of them unchanged, including a name that contains the purged id elsewhere.
- When one of the tenant's queues is locked or still holds a job at finalisation, then the tenant stays `deleting`, no tombstone is written, the failure names only the table, and a later run completes once the queue is idle.
- When another connection holds an open transaction that enqueued a job into the tenant's lane during finalisation, then finalisation fails and the tenant stays `deleting`; after that transaction commits and the job is removed, the next run completes, and no `_private_jobs` row has a `job_queue_id` without a queue row.

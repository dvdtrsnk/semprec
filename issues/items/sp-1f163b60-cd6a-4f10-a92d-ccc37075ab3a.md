## Context

Model tier rationale: One mechanism (presenting the system document scanner from an intent that continues in the foreground) across the intent, an app-level presenter and the CoreKit bridge of sp-5440a887-f4a6-4c91-b8d8-64937880de5b; it needs a recorded exception to the no-UIKit rule.

sp-5440a887-f4a6-4c91-b8d8-64937880de5b added `AddToInboxIntent`, which captures a text. The owner also wants to capture a scanned document from the same Action-button action. The Shortcuts dialog cannot show a camera, so the intent must bring the app to the foreground (`continueInForeground`, iOS 26) and show the system document scanner (`VNDocumentCameraViewController` of VisionKit, a UIKit view controller). The capture then goes into the outbox of the batch's first issue like a text one, so it is never lost and reaches the Inbox when online.

## Task

1. Add an ADR under `docs/adr/` (load `adr-conventions`) recording the exception to the no-UIKit rule of the Apple app for the system document scanner only: one `UIViewControllerRepresentable` wrapping `VNDocumentCameraViewController`, iOS only.
2. Give `AddToInboxIntent` a content parameter, an `AppEnum` with `text` and `scan`, asked for after the type; on macOS only `text` is offered. For `scan` the intent calls `continueInForeground`, asks the app to show the scanner and waits for its outcome.
3. In the app target add a process-wide scan presenter that the app's scene observes and that shows the scanner full screen; when the person finishes, the pages become one PDF (PDFKit) named `Scan YYYY-MM-DD HH-mm.pdf` with media type `application/pdf` and source camera, captured through the bridge of sp-5440a887-f4a6-4c91-b8d8-64937880de5b with the chosen type, day and time and an optional text the intent asked for; when they cancel, nothing is captured. The capture is written by the app side, so it is kept even if the intent's run ends first.
4. The intent answers a dialog that the scan is saved, that it was cancelled, or that the scanner is not available on this device (`VNDocumentCameraViewController.isSupported`).
5. Localize every new string in English and Czech.

## Scope

### In scope

- The content choice, the foreground scanner, the PDF capture and the ADR.

### Out of scope

- Picking photos or files in the intent, recognizing text of the scan.
- Background sending (sp-d6d6351a-9488-4b7d-89c4-b1f6157c0182).

## Acceptance criteria

- Choosing "Scan" opens Alldots on the scanner; finishing captures one PDF with all pages into the outbox with source camera, type, day and time; cancelling captures nothing.
- A scan made offline reaches the Inbox once online, through the outbox.
- On macOS, and on a device without the scanner, the intent offers or answers accordingly without crashing.
- Tests cover building the PDF from pages (page count, name, media type) and the presenter's finish and cancel outcomes.

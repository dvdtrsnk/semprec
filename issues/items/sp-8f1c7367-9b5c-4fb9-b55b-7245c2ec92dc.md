## Context

Model tier rationale: A grant change on the identity plane and on the tables that turn a database row into an API-accepted actor. A missing revoke leaves the side role able to mint a bearer credential in any tenant; a revoke too many breaks login, the MCP run credential, the purge or a side process in production.

Finding R2-DB-2 (medium) of the gh-1086 external review: `docs/operations/database-roles.md` and migration `0057_identity_tables_api_role_only.sql` state that a compromised side-role process (`alldots-ai-gateway`, `alldots-restore-test`) must not be able to mint a session. But `alldots_side` holds full DML on `agent_runs` (`0040_least_privilege_roles.sql`), `agent_run_mcp_credentials` (`0055_grant_late_side_tables.sql`) and `approval_requests` (0040). As `alldots_side`, after `set_config('app.tenant_id', <tenant B>)`, the review inserted a running `agent_runs` row for a tenant-B user and an `agent_run_mcp_credentials` row with the SHA-256 of a token it chose and capabilities `{items.write,items.delete}`; the API's own two lookups in `mcp/mcpRunCredentialsStore.ts` then resolved that token to tenant B and the tenant-B actor. The side role thereby gets an actor whose writes `alldots_data` executes, which bypasses the "side role cannot write items" tier.

Finding R2-DB-3 (low), the side-role part: the global tables have no row-level security, and `alldots_side` can read `sessions`, `password_reset_tokens`, `login_attempts`, `invites`, `admin_audit_log`, `tenant_deletions` and `agent_run_mcp_credentials` of every tenant, and holds full DML on `push_subscriptions` (0040), so it could register an attacker endpoint for any user's push notifications.

Verified for this source at `e6387779b` (identical tree to the integration tip), by reading every reader and writer:

- The side-role processes are `alldots-ai-gateway` (`services/alldots-ai-gateway/src`, `packages/ai-gateway/src`) and the restore-test recorder (`observability/restoreTestResult.ts` through `observability/operatorAlerts.ts` and `notifications/notify.ts`). None of them writes `agent_runs` or `approval_requests`, and none reads or writes `agent_run_mcp_credentials`, `push_subscriptions`, `sessions`, `password_reset_tokens`, `login_attempts`, `invites`, `admin_audit_log` or `tenant_deletions`. The gateway reads `agent_runs` (`reserveGatewayCall` checks that the attributed run exists), so the side role's `SELECT` on `agent_runs` must stay. They read `users` (`getUserById`, `listAdminRecipients`), `tenants` and `tenant_ai_budgets`, which keep their `SELECT`.
- Every other process that touches those tables (`alldots-api`, `alldots-agents`, `alldots-transcribe`, their queue workers, the tenant purge and the restore ledger validator) connects as `alldots_data`. `alldots_data` reaches `agent_runs`, `agent_run_mcp_credentials`, `approval_requests` and `push_subscriptions` only through its membership in `alldots_side`, and `SELECT` on `invites`, `admin_audit_log` and `tenant_deletions` only through it as well (0074, 0077, 0079 grant `alldots_data` the writes directly). So each revoke from `alldots_side` must be preceded by a direct grant to `alldots_data`, as 0057 did for the identity tables.
- The previous release's side processes use none of the revoked privileges and its `alldots_data` processes receive them directly, so the migration is rollback compatible.

Kept on purpose, recorded in `issues/decisions/gh-1086.md`: `alldots_side` keeps `SELECT` on `users` (the operator alert reads recipients across tenants, and `getUserById` selects `password_hash`, so a column revoke is a contract step for a later release), `tenants` and `tenant_ai_budgets`; it keeps full DML on the side tables and on `graphile_worker` (the gateway enqueues jobs). `alldots_data`'s global read of the identity plane (R2-APP-1) is accepted in writing there too. The side tables it keeps include `external_credentials`, `tenant_keys`, `mail_oauth_states`, `mail_account_server_settings`, `agent_database_grants`, `project_mcp_grants`, `agent_run_read_allowances`, `mcp_tool_registrations`, `project_heartbeats` and the document and mail content tables; that remainder is accepted there as the residual risk of R2-DB-2, not closed by this source.

Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for sp-3d4170a4-0620-4343-83c6-02badea81bd7 because both edit `docs/operations/database-roles.md` and add the next migration ordinal.

## Scope

### In scope

- Removing every `alldots_side` write on the tables that create or retarget an actor (`agent_runs`, `agent_run_mcp_credentials`, `approval_requests`) and on `push_subscriptions`.
- Removing `alldots_side`'s read of credentials, tokens and identity-plane rows that no side process needs.
- Tests that prove the refusals and that the side processes and the `alldots_data` flows still work.

### Out of scope

- Row-level security or a `tenant_id` column on any global table, and any change of `alldots_data`'s privileges beyond the direct grants of step 1.
- A column-level grant on `users` for `alldots_side`, and any change of `getUserById`, `writeNotification` or `listAdminRecipients`.
- `graphile_worker` privileges of `alldots_side`, and authentication of job envelopes.
- The `search_path` fix of `[security-gate 01/06]`.
- Any production deployment or password change, any change to gh-1086 itself, any change under `web/` or `mobile/`, and web or mobile verification.

## Acceptance criteria

- When the side role, inside any tenant's scope, inserts an `agent_runs` row, updates the actor of a run, inserts an `agent_run_mcp_credentials` row, inserts an `approval_requests` row or inserts a `push_subscriptions` row, then each statement is refused with `permission denied` and nothing is stored, so the minting sequence of R2-DB-2 no longer produces a credential.
- When the side role selects from `agent_run_mcp_credentials`, `push_subscriptions`, `sessions`, `password_reset_tokens`, `login_attempts`, `invites`, `admin_audit_log` or `tenant_deletions`, then it is refused with `permission denied`; its `SELECT` of `users`, `tenants`, `tenant_ai_budgets`, `agent_runs` and `approval_requests` still works.
- When `alldots_data` mints and resolves an MCP run credential, logs in, verifies and revokes a session, resets a password, accepts an invitation, writes an admin audit row, purges a deleting tenant and validates a restore ledger, then each works as before (the existing integration tests of those flows pass unchanged).
- When the restore-test recorder records a failed run on the side role, then the check is alerting and the admin gets one `backup_restore_failed` notification; the AI gateway tests that run on the side role pass unchanged.
- When the previous release runs against the migrated schema, then none of its processes hits a `permission denied`.

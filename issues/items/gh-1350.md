#### (b) Open questions for the product owner
- None remaining.

#### (c) User-visible behavior
The Overview screen gets all of its badges and counts from one request: pending Inbox cards, unread mail, open tasks for today (tasks completed today are not counted), active Semp problems, the item count of every collection, the active project count of every area, and the id of today's Journal entry (empty until that entry exists). Each number is exactly the one the owning screen shows, and a tenant sees only its own numbers.

**Blocked by:** #1978
**Model tier:** medium

## Context

Model tier rationale: One routing rule in the existing tick that hands a case to a producer built elsewhere, with a clear classification input and existing tick fixtures, but it must not start the model call for the wrong case.

A long audio file shared into the Inbox (23 minutes, "shared from Voice Memos") is a recording to transcribe, not a note to read: the card should be "New recording". #1965 classifies each audio attachment as `voiceNote` or `recording` at 120 seconds. #1978 adds the producer of a proposal to transcribe an audio attachment from the Inbox (the "recording" card). The tick (`backend/packages/data/src/inbox/inboxTickAction.ts`, computation from #1434) today would send every Inbox item to the model.

Decisions:
- If an Inbox item has at least one audio attachment with `audioKind` `recording`, the tick produces the recording proposal of #1978 and makes no model call, even when the item also has text or other attachments. Reason: the long recording is the subject; the rest of the item is handled within the recording card and gets no proposal of its own.
- If the metadata of an audio attachment is not ready yet, the tick does nothing; the metadata job re-triggers it (#1965).
- A short voice note continues through the normal computation with its transcript (#1980).

## Task

Load `state-writes` and `testing`.

1. In the tick's entry (`inboxTickAction.ts`), before the model computation, load the item's attachments (`listInboxAttachments`) and decide: not ready (an audio attachment without `metadata`) returns without writing; any `recording` calls the producer of #1978 for that attachment and returns; otherwise continue as before.
2. Keep the decision in one small pure function `routeInboxAttachments(attachments)` returning `"notReady" | { recording: attachmentId } | "compute"`, exported for tests.
3. Tests: a 23-minute M4A produces the recording proposal and no gateway call is made; a 40-second note continues to the computation; metadata not ready writes no card; an item with a recording and a photo routes to the recording; an item with no audio is unchanged.

### Out of scope

## Acceptance criteria

- When an Inbox item has a 23-minute audio attachment, then the card is the recording proposal and no model call is made.
- At the shared boundary from #1965, 119 seconds continues to proposal computation, while 120 and 121 seconds route to the recording card without a model call; test all three values.
- When the audio is 40 seconds, then the item goes through the normal computation.
- When the audio metadata is not ready, then no card is written until the metadata job re-triggers the tick.
- When an item has a recording and a photo, then the recording proposal is produced.

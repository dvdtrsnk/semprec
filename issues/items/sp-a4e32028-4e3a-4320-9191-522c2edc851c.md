## Context

Model tier rationale: One background job that calls existing repository reads through the layer `[offline-first 01/07]` built, with a rate limit and one helper moved from presentation to domain; no new persistence or protocol.

The offline set is kept answer by answer as the screens read it (`[offline-first 01/07]`), so a screen the person has not opened since the last connection (the next month of the calendar, a diary day, a project) has nothing to show offline. Each answer is kept under its exact request, so a warm-up must send the same requests the screens send: the calendar reads the window of a month (`windowOf` in `:shared:presentation` `CalendarMapping.kt`: the six weeks of the month grid with a week on each side), the Tasks screen the lists of `TaskScope`, the pickers the catalogs, the diary the day periods and their pages (`[offline-first 05/07]` keeps pages).

## Task

Load `kmp-shared`, `testing`, `error-handling` and `mobile-verify`.

1. Move `windowOf` to `:shared:domain` as `CalendarDay.calendarWindow()` (same result); the calendar ViewModels call it.
2. `OfflineWarmup` in `:shared:data` reads, through the repositories of a window: the schema and the databases, the task lists of every `TaskScope`, the areas, projects and people, the calendars, the invitations, the calendar windows of the current month, the one before and the two after, the agenda's first page from today, the diary day periods from 7 days back to 7 days ahead with their pages, the overview, the sleep overview of today and the Inbox cards and types (no screen reads the Inbox count). A read that fails is left as it is; cancellation stops it, and so does the process turning offline.
3. It runs when a window starts with a session and whenever `OfflineLayer` reports the connection back or the socket connected, at most once every 30 minutes per account for the whole process, never while offline and never without a session; it runs after the queue replay finished, so it reads what the replay wrote.
4. Tests (commonTest): the requests one warm-up sends (each repository called once, the four windows, the fifteen days); a second trigger within 30 minutes sends nothing; offline and signed out send nothing; the windows of `calendarWindow()` equal the former `windowOf` for the presentation tests.

## Scope

### In scope

- Reading the offline set ahead of the screens.

### Out of scope

- Collections other than tasks, areas, projects and people (they are kept once opened).
- Mail, files, recordings, Semp and search.

## Acceptance criteria

- After a connection, without opening them, the next month of the calendar, a diary day of the past week, the task lists, the overview and the project picker open without a connection.
- A warm-up runs at most once every 30 minutes and never offline or signed out.
- The calendar shows the same windows as before.

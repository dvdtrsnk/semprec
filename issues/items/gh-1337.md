## Behavior specification

Transcripts: detail, summary, action items, event matching, cards, speakers and Semp.

This batch was planned from the reviewed design of the iOS app: every issue below states the backend behavior the app needs that `develop` does not provide yet. The issues are linked as sub-issues; their order follows the dependency graph across all batches, foundations first.

Status: drafts awaiting the `/define-behavior` Phase 5 audit. No issue in this batch carries `spec:approved` or `agent:ready` yet.

## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions: transcripts-1

#### (a) Decisions adopted from open questions

- Speaker names in generated meeting notes (a speaker who is not yet confirmed shows as a suggestion on a card): question "use the proposed mapping or not" -> only confirmed `speakers` edges count; an unmapped speaker shows as "Speaker N" -> a suggestion is not a fact and the notes must not name someone the user has not confirmed -> Decided by the product owner: only confirmed speakers are named, the others are "Speaker N"; a proposed mapping could distort the notes. (#1695)
- Brief for the unknown speaker token in generated text: question not asked in the notes -> unknown token reads as the generic localized word "Speaker"/"Mluvčí"; unmapped known speaker reads "Speaker N". (#1695)
- Which action items the Event's proposals list offers: question "my + unowned + participants except clients, or fix the design" -> conservative: only open items that are mine or have no owner; other people's items are never offered; the detail endpoint and the list share one function -> Decided by the product owner: only open items that are mine or have no owner are offered; other people's items belong to them, so the design is to be corrected, not the rule. (#1767)
- Undo of an added action item: question "soft-delete the task or only unlink" -> soft-delete the task (30-day trash) and clear the state -> Decided by the product owner: the task goes to the 30-day trash, so it stays restorable. (#1767)
- What Semp does with followed (noted) items -> Decided by the product owner: in v1 the followed state is only stored; reminders and checks come later. (#1754)
- Adding a followed item: adding replaces the followed state; following an already added item is refused. (#1754, #1767)
- Nothing overlapped the recording: question "no card (a) or card proposing a new meeting (b)" -> Decided by the product owner: (b), the card with a new-meeting proposal, which the user can reject; the variant without a card is not built. (#1813, #1818)
- Later speaker mappings into Events.people of a meeting created from a recording: question "propagate or let the user add by hand" -> Decided by the product owner: yes, by adding only: a confirmed speaker assignment adds the person to the Event's participants (as the speaker card confirm does); changing or clearing an assignment never removes a participant. Participants are read at confirm (#1818); later confirmed assignments add them (#1770 for the manual assignment, #1671 for the card).
- Revise by choosing another Event or person: question "revise with a relation envelope or PATCH values" -> revise with a relation envelope (this route); PATCH values stays for single fields. (#1870)
- Overlap boundaries: strict overlap; an Event without an end time counts as a point at its start; the 30-minute lead is dropped. (#1813)
- Language names that are not in the Whisper map become `null`. (#1475)
- Recording quote on the recommendation card is chosen deterministically (first segment of at least five words, 120 characters), not by the model. The reason is written by the model in the user's UI language. (#1848)
- Backfill of old transcripts selects only `done` transcripts with segments and no notes, whose automation is not error/locked/pending, in bounded batches; a failed backfill re-run leaves the finished transcript's `status` and notifications alone. (#1712)
- `ifVersion` on revise: included now (card `updatedAt`, existing 409 `version_conflict`); locked-card refusals keep the current 400. (#1870)
- Message-only revise: allowed for transcript cards only; this issue records the message in the history, acting on it is the recompute issue. (#1870)

#### (b) Open questions remaining for the product owner

- Operation name for the Event recommendation call: the notes for the recording-matching topic call it `transcript_event_match` (adopted here), while the shared Inbox prerequisites topic lists a `transcript_event_pick` registration. One of the two names must be dropped; if the Inbox prerequisites issue registers `transcript_event_pick`, change the name in the recommendation issue.
- Whether the Event's proposals list and the transcript's action items should be generated from one source (done in code: one shared function) or the design should be corrected.
- Whether a transcript without an Event can be assigned to one later by hand.
- Which locked-card error code revise should use once the dedicated 409 code exists.
- Design inconsistencies recorded for design: the shared meeting summary names Jana before Speaker 3 is mapped (adopted behaviour: not named); the demo data of the recordings do not match the calendar (14 Oct entries, "yesterday 14:41").

#### (c) User-visible behaviour of this chunk (for the epic body)

A transcript now has one detail screen that is served by a single call: header (name, date, duration, language), all spoken segments with speaker names that update as soon as a speaker is mapped to a person, notes, the linked Event, the playable file, processing state and how many "who is this speaker" questions are waiting. The detected language is stored as a proper language code (for example `cs`). When a recording is processed, Semp now writes structured meeting notes: a title, a summary, a short summary, the decisions and the action items with owner, due date (relative dates such as "tomorrow" are resolved against the date of the recording in the user's time zone) and the moment they were said; older recordings get the same notes in the background. The user can follow other people's action items and turn their own into tasks of the meeting's Event with the project and people inherited from the Event, undo that (the task goes to the trash), and see the open items the Event could still turn into tasks. Recordings are matched to calendar meetings by real time overlap in the user's time zone; when several meetings overlap, Semp reads the notes and recommends the likely one on the Inbox card with the reason. When no meeting fits, the proposed new meeting has a proper name, the real start and end of the recording, the likely project and the participants recognised from the speakers. Cards for recordings in the Inbox can now be revised from the app: pick another meeting or person, or give Semp an instruction in words.


### Decisions: transcripts-2

#### (a) Decisions adopted from open questions

- Which Events the recording card offers (#1978): question "meeting and standup only, or also focus events; what type is lunch" -> Decided by the product owner: Events of the recording's local day of type `meeting` or `standup` and Events with no type (calendar Events have no type and could not be chosen otherwise), plus "no Event"; focus work is not offered.
- Project of a chosen Event vs the card's project field (#1978): -> Decided by the product owner: the project is taken over from the chosen Event until the first change (Event to recording/transcript inheritance); a project the user chose explicitly on the card stays the recording's own value. The confirm writes only an explicitly different project into the recording request; otherwise the transcript inherits it from the Event (#1696, #1697).
- How Semp guesses the name and project before transcription (#1978): from the usual Inbox proposal computation over the item's text when there is text, otherwise a localized default name; no project, no language and no speaker count are proposed -> Decided by the product owner: as drafted.
- Which audio gets the recording card (#1978): -> Decided by the product owner: only an audio attachment of at least 120 seconds; a shorter one is a voice note without a recording card (the threshold decided for Inbox attachments, routed by #1985).
- Candidate rule for speakers in the menu and on the card (#1770, #1640): the manual-assignment endpoint returns all participants with invitation status and mapped key and applies no invitation filter (the client hides mapped people); the card's candidates are all named Event participants plus the tenant's own person, excluding persons already mapped to another key -> Decided by the product owner: named participants and the user's own person are offered; an already mapped person is not offered.
- Mapped persons on the card (#1640): excluded from suggestion and alternatives (not only from the menu) -> the model cannot map one person to two keys.
- One person under two speaker keys (#1640): not supported; such a person is not offered -> Decided by the product owner: as drafted.
- Source of the person's title (#1770, #1640): no property exists; `title` is always `null`, and `relationship` is returned additionally by the candidates endpoint -> Decided by the product owner: the title stays empty; no new property in v1.
- Recompute instruction scope (#1940): the model may only choose among the card's own candidates/alternatives or edit the fields of the current new-item proposal; a result that switches card role is discarded.
- Re-asking when the Event changes (#1942): untouched `needsClarification` cards are refreshed by the producer; cards in any other status or touched by the user are never rewritten; the checkpoint is bound to the Event and candidate set -> Decided by the product owner: as drafted.
- Company of a transcript after its Event is deleted (#1697): -> Decided by the product owner: the company stays as the transcript's own value, like the project and the people (values taken over from a deleted source stay as own); the company link that came through the project ends with the Event's removal or deletion.
- Self person in transcript people (#1697): left out through a `valueFilter` added to the link-rule schema -> Decided by the product owner: the user's own person is not shown among a transcript's people.
- Semp writes over transcripts (#1832): no write tools or approval exceptions added -> Decided by the product owner: read tools only.
- Segment ref limits (#1832, #1834): requested range at most 200 segments or 15 minutes, context of 3 segments on each side on top.
- Cardinality of Transcripts relations (#1470): project and company single-valued, people many-to-many -> Decided by the product owner: as drafted.

#### (b) Open questions remaining for the product owner

- Name of the gateway operation for the Event recommendation versus the Inbox prerequisites topic (see transcripts-1 decisions).
- Design/design items: the speaker menu in the design offers only invited unmapped people; re-assigning or un-assigning a speaker does not exist in the design; the relationship catalog differs between two design screens.

#### (c) User-visible behaviour of this chunk (for the epic body)

Recordings of at least two minutes shared into the Inbox are no longer transcribed automatically: a card shows the length and time of the recording and lets the user set the name, language, number of speakers, meeting and project, and only confirming starts the transcription. Every unmapped speaker in a transcript gets a question card ("Who is Speaker 3?") with two or three quotes, when they first speak, why Semp suggests someone, and alternatives, including the user's own person and a "new person" choice; the user can also assign a speaker from the transcript screen in one step, and the card closes. Suggestions are asked again when the transcript is linked to a meeting later or the meeting gains participants. Transcripts get their own project, company and participants, which they take over from the meeting (and the company through the project) while the user has not changed them; changing the meeting's project or participants carries over, and deleting the meeting keeps the values. The user can give Semp a plain-language instruction on a recording or speaker card ("it was the accountant call") and Semp recomputes the proposal without overwriting hand-edited fields. Semp can read a transcript with real speaker names and read any stretch of it with surrounding context, and a chat message can point at a stretch of a transcript.

## Context

This issue builds on #170 and completes the `observability` batch.

## Task

Set distinct SyslogIdentifier values on Semprec units and the journald Compose driver. Provision Storage=persistent, SystemMaxUse=2G, MaxRetentionSec=90day and exclude journals from backups. Add a five-minute dead-man timer that calls public HTTPS /healthz and only then HEALTHCHECKS_PING_URL. Add OnFailure=semprec-failping@%n.service after restart exhaustion, including the unit name.

## Scope

### In scope

- Task deliverables, migrations, integration, automated tests, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- Monitor account creation is manual.
- No monitor hosted on the Semprec server.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit/tag filters isolate process logs and Compose joins journald.
- Retention provisioning is idempotent.
- Health failure withholds success ping.
- Transient recovered crashes do not /fail; restart exhaustion does.
- External monitoring shares no Semprec notification dependency.

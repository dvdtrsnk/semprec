## Behavior specification

People and companies: last contact, phones, mail sender and the user person.

This batch was planned from the reviewed design of the iOS app: every issue below states the backend behavior the app needs that `develop` does not provide yet. The issues are linked as sub-issues; their order follows the dependency graph across all batches, foundations first.

Status: drafts awaiting the `/define-behavior` Phase 5 audit. No issue in this batch carries `spec:approved` or `agent:ready` yet.

## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions: people-1

#### (a) Adopted decisions
- Last contact derived or manual? -> derived from past events, e-mails and transcripts (recomputed through the queue, plus daily sweep and backfill) -> recommended by the notes; a manual date would drift and could not be kept correct by the mail and transcription pipelines.
- Stored form of lastContact -> calendar date in the tenant timezone -> matches a date-typed property and the "older than 30 days" filter.
- Phone number storage -> stored verbatim, one per line (longText, like emails); normalization only at search time ((not planned yet)).
- Is the user's own person hidden? -> ordinary People item exposed as user.personId; the client filters it out -> no data change. -> Decided by the product owner: hidden from the People collection, as the design shows (not shown as "Me"); it is reachable through the Profile and is still used elsewhere, for example as a speaker candidate.
- Self-person auto-creation -> none; cutover only links an existing person matched by e-mail, else null.
- Foreign vs nonexistent person id on PUT /api/auth/self-person -> both 404, identical.
- People searchable fields and phone normalization -> left to (not planned yet) and (not planned yet).
- People subtitle with area or role -> Decided by the product owner: fix the design; a person's subtitle shows their company (the existing People `companies` relation). No text role and no area derived from the person's projects in v1, so no backend issue. The detail screen's Phone, E-mails and Last contact rows are client work.

#### (b) Open questions for the product owner
- None remaining.

#### (c) User-visible behavior
People gain a Phone field (several numbers, one per line) and a read-only Last contact date that the system keeps up to date from the latest past meeting, e-mail or recording the person took part in; it updates when participants, dates or trash state change, and a daily check covers meetings that have just passed. The tenant owner can link their account to their own person in People (the link is pre-filled from the account e-mail when it matches), and the session tells the app which person is "me"; the app hides that person from the People collection and from people pickers, and shows it through the Profile.

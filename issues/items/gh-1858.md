**Model tier:** medium

## Context

Model tier rationale: One read-only route that reuses the fire-time function and existing item query helpers across defined seams, with tenant scoping covered by the existing route-isolation fixtures.

Event and Task reminders are scheduled on the phone, not pushed by the server. The iPhone keeps a sliding window of local notifications (the system allows at most 64 pending) and refreshes it when the feed is invalidated, when the app opens and when a silent push arrives (#1879). The server keeps no reminder state; it only answers "what fires in the next hours".

Today nothing like this exists. Notifications are a closed catalog (`backend/packages/data/src/notifications/notificationKinds.ts`) delivered by Web Push and APNs after a server-side event; Events and Tasks have no reminder surface. #1719 provides `computeReminderFireAt` (`backend/packages/data/src/calendar/reminderFireTime.ts`). #1843 materializes an Event occurrence on its first exception or link, so a moved, cancelled or edited occurrence is a real row with its own date and time.

Custom routes over Tasks and Events belong to the `systemDatabases` module (single-writer ownership, `docs/adr/2026-09-10-single-writer-ownership-model.md`), so this route is declared in `backend/packages/data/src/seed/systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts` like the existing `single-consumer-read` route `listTranscriptSpeakers`, even though its path is under `/api/calendar`.

Decisions:
- Only rows that exist are listed (every Event row, every Task row, and materialized occurrences). Not-yet-materialized virtual occurrences of a recurring series are not listed here. Reason: expansion is delivered by #1877 and #1672; wiring them in is a follow-up so this route does not duplicate recurrence logic.
- `hours` defaults to 48 and must be an integer from 1 to 168; otherwise 400 `validation_failed`. `from` is an ISO-8601 instant and is required. The response holds at most 200 entries, ordered by `fireAt` then `key`.
- The muted-sleep adjustment (shifting reminders that fall inside a sleep window) is applied by the client, not the server.

Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for #1856 because both edit the same registry, manifest, cutover, route matrix or hook files listed in Touches.

## Task

Load `io-hardening`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Add `GET /api/calendar/reminders?from=<instant>&hours=48` returning `[{ key, itemId, kind, title, fireAt, startsAt }]`. `kind` is `"event"` or `"task"`; `key` is `<itemId>:<fireAt>` or `<itemId>@<date>:<fireAt>` for an occurrence row; `title` is the item name; `fireAt` and `startsAt` are ISO instants (`startsAt` of an all-day or untimed item is 09:00 of its date).
2. Implement the handler in a new file `backend/packages/data/src/calendar/remindersFeedRouteHandler.ts` exporting `createRemindersFeedRouteHandler`. It reads the tenant zone with `getCalendarClock` (#1418), selects Events and Tasks whose date could produce a fire time in the window (the window widened by the longest possible offset: one day plus one hour of alert and travel), excludes trashed rows, reads each Event's calendar `notify` (#1600), calls `computeReminderFireAt` and keeps entries whose `fireAt` is in `[from, from + hours)`.
3. Declare the route in the `systemDatabases` manifest `customRoutes` with `justification: "single-consumer-read"` and a comment naming the consumer (the phone's local scheduler), and add it to `backend/services/semprec-api/src/routeMatrix.ts` and `backend/services/semprec-api/src/__tests__/tenantIsolationRoutes.test.ts` so it is covered by the route and tenant-isolation suites.
4. Tests: a meeting at 10:00 with `alertMinutes` 15 and `travelMinutes` 30 appears with `fireAt` 09:15 Europe/Prague; a muted calendar's events are absent while a merely hidden one's are present; a trashed Event is absent; a moved materialized occurrence shows its new `fireAt`; a Task with notifications and `timeFrom` 14:00 appears; `hours` 0 or 200 gives 400; another tenant's items never appear.

## Scope

### In scope

- The read-only reminders feed route, its manifest declaration, route-matrix entries and tests.

### Out of scope

- The fire-time rules: #1719.
- Expanding virtual occurrences of recurring series: #1877 and #1672.
- Notifying the phone that the feed changed: #1879.
- Birthday and holiday entries; they are generated entries and join the feed with #1800 and #1804.
- Server-side reminder pushes and a reminders table: deferred, not in v1; in v1 the phone schedules reminders from this feed.
- Shifting reminders around sleep windows (#1838 and #1794 own the sleep contract; the client applies it).

## Acceptance criteria

- When a meeting 10:00 has `alertMinutes` 15 and `travelMinutes` 30, then the feed contains it with `fireAt` 09:15 in Europe/Prague.
- When a calendar has `notify` false, then its events are absent; when it is only hidden, they are present.
- When an Event is trashed, then it is absent; when an occurrence was moved, then its entry has the new `fireAt`.
- When a Task has notifications on and `timeFrom` 14:00, then it is listed with `fireAt` 14:00 local.
- When `hours` is outside 1 to 168 or `from` is missing or not an instant, then the response is 400 `validation_failed`.
- When two tenants have reminders, then each caller sees only their own.

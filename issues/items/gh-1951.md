## Context

Model tier rationale: One more id-less tenant-wide scope on the established realtime catalog plus a single publish call at the end of a nightly per-tenant action, following the preceding scope issues, with the tenant isolation of the call being the part that needs careful tests.

The Overview screen shows live counts (tasks for today and the badges). Almost all of them change through events that already reach the client: item invalidations for the Tasks, Processing proposals, Emails, Projects and Areas databases (the client maps the database id to the number), the `sempStatus` scope for the problem count (#1868), and the `inboxCards` scope (#1811); archiving a database arrives as a `schema` invalidation. A task whose last host event ends is revealed by a write of `hiddenUntil`, which is an ordinary item invalidation (#1782). The one change that no write announces is the turn of the day: at midnight in the tenant's time zone the nightly action `core.tasksDayRollover` rolls unfinished one-off tasks over to the new day and closes missed occurrences of recurring tasks, and the number of tasks for today changes even when no task moved.

This issue adds the realtime scope `overview`, without payload fields, delivered to every socket of the tenant, following exactly the shape and wiring of the earlier scopes in this chain (#1946 and before): the `invalidation` message type with a `scope` discriminator in `backend/packages/realtime/src/pgNotifyPublisher.ts`, the `OutboundFrame` variant in `backend/packages/realtime/src/protocolV1.ts`, the `InvalidationEvent` variant in `backend/packages/data/src/realtimeHook.ts`, the mapping in `backend/packages/realtime/src/syncServer.ts` and the parser in `backend/packages/realtime-client/src/serverFrame.ts`. Rules of `docs/adr/2026-10-16-tenant-scoped-realtime-fan-out.md` apply: thin payload, tenant from Postgres, no replay, publish only after commit.

Its only producer is the end of `core.tasksDayRollover` for one tenant. The action is scheduled per tenant at 00:00 in that tenant's time zone (not by the global `CORE_CRONTAB` in `backend/packages/data/src/worker.ts`, which has no per-tenant time zone) and is delivered by the Tasks rollover issues, which run before this one in the program. It must run inside the tenant's scope so the envelope carries that tenant, and it publishes after the rollover and the closing of missed occurrences are committed, whether or not any task moved. After a reconnect the client loads the whole overview over REST (#1837); the frame only says the numbers changed.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `canonical-keys`, `io-hardening` and `testing`.

1. Add the `overview` variant to `InvalidationEvent`, `RealtimeMessage` (+ `parseRealtimeMessage`), `OutboundFrame` (`{ type: "invalidate", scope: "overview" }`), the `invalidation` case of `syncServer.ts` (no `userId`, so `sendToTenant(tenantId, frame)`), and `parseServerFrame`.
2. Create `backend/packages/data/src/overview/overviewInvalidation.ts` exporting `publishOverviewInvalidation(client)` that defers `notifyInvalidation({ scope: "overview" })` with `runAfterCommit`.
3. Call it at the end of the per-tenant run of `core.tasksDayRollover`, after its writes are committed or in the transaction that holds them, including when the tenant had nothing to roll over.
4. Tests: the frame reaches every socket of the tenant whose rollover ran and no socket of another tenant; it is sent even when no task moved; a rolled-back rollover sends nothing; a client-parser round trip.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `overview` scope end to end and its single publication after the nightly day rollover.

### Out of scope

- Any publication from a cron in the global `CORE_CRONTAB`.
- Event end: it is an item invalidation of `hiddenUntil` (#1782); problem count: `sempStatus` (#1868); inbox cards: #1811.
- The overview data route: #1837.
- Scheduling or implementing the rollover itself.

## Acceptance criteria

- When `core.tasksDayRollover` finishes for a tenant, then every socket of that tenant receives `invalidate` with scope `overview` and no other field, even when no task was moved.
- When another tenant's sockets are connected, then they do not receive it.
- When the rollover transaction rolls back, then no frame is sent.
- A malformed overview message is dropped by the listener and by the client parser.

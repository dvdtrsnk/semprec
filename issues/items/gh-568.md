## Context

The review follow-ups pipeline (epic #565; decision recorded in the ADR that #567 added, `docs/adr/*-merged-review-findings-become-proposed-follow-up-issues.md`) introduces five labels and a new way for an issue to become `spec:approved`:
- a daily harvest collects open review findings into a harvest issue;
- Relay's triage workflow turns that issue into proposed follow-up issues;
- a human approves each proposed issue.

`.github/ISSUE_FORMAT.md`'s `## Labels` table is where every agent and human looks up what a label means and who sets it. The table does not know these labels yet, and its `spec:approved` and `agent:ready` rows name `/define-behavior` Phase 5 as their only setter.

The approved semantics of the new labels and of a human's decisions on a proposed issue (epic #565, L4 and L5):

- **Approval.** A human swaps `spec:proposed` for `spec:approved` + `agent:ready`. An issue touching a protected path gets `spec:approved` only and is maintainer-implemented.
- **Reshaping.** A human edits the issue, then approves it.
- **Rejection.** Closing a proposed issue retires its findings permanently, because their markers stay in the ledger whatever the issue's state. Reopening it is the undo.
- **Epic.** Closing an epic decides nothing: each child issue carries its own findings and its own decision.

## Task

1. In `.github/ISSUE_FORMAT.md`'s `## Labels` table, add one row per label:

   | Label | Set by | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | `followups:harvested` | the harvest | on a merged pull request whose open findings were harvested, or that had none |
   | `followups:harvest` | the harvest | on every harvest issue; kept forever |
   | `followups:ready` | the harvest | on a harvest issue awaiting triage; Relay's trigger; removed by triage when it publishes |
   | `followups:issue` | triage | on every issue and epic it creates; kept forever |
   | `spec:proposed` | triage | on every issue it creates; awaiting human approval; never dispatched |

2. Add "or a human approving a `spec:proposed` follow-up issue" to the "Set by" cell of the `spec:approved` row and of the `agent:ready` row.
3. Directly under the table's closing paragraph about `agent:ready` and `dequeue`, add one paragraph on follow-up issues. It states approval, reshaping, retirement on close, reopening as the undo, and that closing an epic decides nothing about its child issues, as listed in Context. It links the ADR from #567 by its path.

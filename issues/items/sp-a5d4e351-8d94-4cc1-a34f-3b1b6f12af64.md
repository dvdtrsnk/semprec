**Model tier:** high

## Context

Model tier rationale: Introduces a new job family, guard and reconcile transaction that every adapter plugs into; it must follow no-network-in-transaction and echo suppression.

With the model in place, an account with Calendars on needs a job that fetches changes through a provider adapter and applies them, mirroring `mailAccountSync`.

Decision: the adapter interface is `listCalendars()`, `fetchChanges(calendar, syncState)` returning upserts, deletions and a new sync state, `putEvent`, `deleteEvent` (used by the write-back issue). Adapters live under `calendar/sync/adapters/`.
Decision: the first sync of an account fetches events from 1 year back to 2 years ahead; later passes are incremental.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `db-migrations`, `error-handling`, `io-hardening`, `canonical-keys` and `testing`.

1. Job `calendarAccountSync` (key `calendar-account-sync:<accountId>`, maxAttempts 3, affinity api), enqueued when `calendarsEnabled` turns on, by a sweep `calendarAccountSyncSweep` every 5 minutes for due accounts, and by `POST /api/accounts/:id/calendars/sync` (debounced 60 s per account).
2. The pass takes the account guard (advisory lock), reads credentials outside any transaction, calls the adapter, then applies results in one transaction through the choke point as writer `calendarSync`: create, update or remove account calendars; upsert events matched by `event_remote_state.remote_id`; trash events the provider deleted; store `base_snapshot`, etag and sync tokens.
3. Echo suppression: a fetched event whose fields equal `base_snapshot` writes nothing.
4. Status: `calendar_account_sync_state` (account item id PK, `status` ok|error|never|needsReauthorization|needsConsent, `last_successful_sync_at`, `failing_since`, `last_error_code`, `initial_sync_completed_at`) exposed as `calendarStatus` in the mailbox rows; a Semp problem `calendarSyncError` like `mailSyncError`.
5. Switching Calendars off stops the job and removes the account's calendars, their events and remote state in a chunked cleanup; removing the account does the same.
6. An adapter registry selected by sync mode/provider; a fake adapter for tests.
7. Update `docs/architecture/domain-calendar-tasks-and-media.md` with the sync owner and lifecycle.

## Scope

### In scope

- The job, sweep, manual trigger, apply, echo suppression, status and cleanup with a fake adapter.

### Out of scope

- Real provider adapters (next three issues).
- Pushing local changes (write-back issue).
- Notifications (notification issue).

## Acceptance criteria

- With the fake adapter, switching Calendars on creates the account calendars and their events within one pass.
- A second pass with unchanged data writes nothing.
- An event deleted at the provider goes to the trash on the next pass.
- Switching Calendars off removes the account calendars and their events and stops further passes.
- An adapter failure leaves data unchanged, sets status `error` and raises a Semp problem; a 401 sets `needsReauthorization`.

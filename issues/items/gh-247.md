**Blocked by:** #183, #82, #89

## Context

First of two parts of the Event-matching slice of the `batch-transcription` batch (approved behavior in #179). It builds on #183, which finalizes `computed.segments` and the `done` status, and on the relation-integrity guarantees of #82 and the generic operations of #89. This part adds the deterministic matching step (step 6 of the pipeline) and the Processing Suggestion card it emits on ambiguity. #184 builds on it by adding the `entityKind: 'relation'` proposal shape and the transactional `confirm`/`revise` behavior for those cards.

## Task

- Add the match step to the `semprec-transcribe` pipeline: find candidate Event rows with `type` in {`standup`, `meeting`} whose `date` (with time) lies inside the window `[recordingStart - 30 minutes, recordingEnd]`, where the recording start is the transcription `date` and the end is start plus the checkpointed duration. The step is mechanical — no LLM candidate selection.
- Exactly one candidate: write the existing 1:1 Transcription<->Event edge directly and finish.
- Zero or more than one candidates: create no edge and no guess; create exactly one Processing Suggestion card with `kind = 'transcript'` and `sourceTranscript` pointing at the transcription. In this part the card's `proposal` carries the existing create-Event shape (`entityKind: 'database'`, `target` = Events, `properties` estimated from the transcript); #184 adds the link-existing shape.
- Deterministic card identity: re-running the match step for the same transcription (retry, crash, rerun) must converge on the same single card, never a second one, and must never create a second edge when one already exists.
- Confirming the create-Event card is not wired here beyond what `confirm` already does; the transactional Transcription<->Event edge on confirm is #184.

## Scope

### In scope

- Time-window candidate query, exactly-one auto-link, zero/many suggestion emission with the create-Event proposal shape, idempotent re-execution, tests, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- `entityKind: 'relation'`, the link-existing proposal shape, transactional edge creation in `confirm`, and full-replace `revise` — #184.
- LLM-based candidate selection — never.
- Speaker-to-People mapping — #185.

## Acceptance criteria

- One candidate inside the window links automatically through the existing 1:1 relation; no card is created.
- Zero candidates or several candidates never guess: no edge, exactly one deterministic `kind = 'transcript'` card with `sourceTranscript` is created.
- Retries are idempotent: re-running the step on an already-linked transcription creates no card and no second edge; re-running on an ambiguous one does not create a second card.
- Events outside the window, or of other `type` values, are never considered.

## Context

Model tier rationale: One read-only aggregate route over existing item data with a defined output shape, but the month boundaries in the tenant time zone, rounding and the budget states need careful tests.

The Finance overview shows "Spent in October" against a monthly budget, a state label (in budget, near the limit, over), the share of the budget used, the days left in the month, and, per category, the spent part of that category's monthly budget. No endpoint produces these numbers. A rollup (`backend/packages/data/src/rollup/config.ts`) can sum over one relation but has no filter or period, and the generic query (`POST /api/databases/:id/query`) returns items without relations, so computing the summary on the client would need many calls. #1511 creates `transactions` and `financeCategories` (with `monthlyBudget` and the `category` relation) and #1353 the Finance manifest. The aggregate-route pattern is `/api/ai-usage` (`backend/packages/data/src/aiGateway/aiUsageRouteHandler.ts`, a handler factory taking the pool, declared in `schemaCoreModuleManifest.ts`). Month and day boundaries use the tenant time zone from `getSystemTimezone` (`backend/packages/data/src/systemSettings.ts`, default Europe/Prague).

#1646 exposes the same numbers to the assistant by calling the function built here.

Decisions:
- "Spent" counts expense transactions by `date`, not `dueDate`, and counts unpaid ones too. Reason: the figure answers "what did this month commit me to", and an unpaid invoice of the month is a spend.
- A transaction in the trash is ignored. A transaction without a category counts in the total `spent` but appears in no category row.
- `budget` is the sum of `monthlyBudget` of non-trashed categories (empty counts as 0). `percent` is `round(spent / budget * 100)` half up, or `null` when the budget is 0. A category without a budget has `percent: null`.
- `state` is `inBudget` when `percent` is null or at most 89, `nearLimit` from 90 up to and including 100, and `over` above 100. The threshold constants live in one place and can change without touching the route. The label texts for `nearLimit` and `over` are a client concern.
- `daysLeft` is the number of days from tomorrow to the end of the month in the tenant zone (16 October gives 15); for any month other than the current one it is 0.
- Currency is `CZK`; the response states it.


Shared-file ordering: this issue also waits for #1574, #1583 because both edit the same files listed in Touches.

## Task

Load `io-hardening`, `error-handling` and `testing`.

1. Add `backend/packages/data/src/finance/financeSummary.ts` exporting `getFinanceSummary(client, { month? })` returning `{ month, currency, spent, income, budget, percent, state, daysLeft, categories: [{ categoryId, name, icon, color, spent, budget, percent }] }`, with `categories` ordered by `sortOrder`. `month` is `YYYY-MM`; when absent it is the current month in the tenant zone. Month bounds are computed in the tenant zone with luxon, so a transaction at 00:30 on 1 October and one at 23:30 on 31 October (Europe/Prague) both belong to October. A malformed month throws `ValidationError`.
2. Add the handler `backend/packages/data/src/finance/financeSummaryRouteHandler.ts` exporting `createFinanceSummaryRouteHandler(pool)` for `GET /api/finance/summary?month=YYYY-MM`, and declare it in `financeModuleManifest.ts` (#1353) under `customRoutes` with `justification: "single-consumer-read"` and a comment naming the consumer (the Finance overview).
3. Add the route to `backend/services/semprec-api/src/routeMatrix.ts` and `backend/services/semprec-api/src/__tests__/tenantIsolationRoutes.test.ts`, and export the handler from the data package barrel.
4. Tests: a transaction at 1 October 00:30 and one at 31 October 23:30 count in October and a trashed one does not; spent 6 200 against budget 10 000 gives `percent` 62 and `inBudget`; on 16 October `daysLeft` is 15; a category with budget 5 000 and spent 4 750 has `percent` 95; no category with a budget gives `percent: null` and `inBudget`; 90 % gives `nearLimit` and 101 % gives `over`; an uncategorized expense counts only in the total; a malformed month gives 400; another tenant's transactions are never counted.

## Scope

### In scope

- The summary function, the `GET /api/finance/summary` route, its manifest declaration and route-matrix entries, and tests.

### Out of scope

- The assistant tool that returns the same numbers: #1646.
- Restricting what the assistant may write to Finance: #1815.
- Listing the latest transactions: the generic query, relation expansion (#1518) and value links (#1403) already cover it.
- Setting budgets, managing categories and marking expenses paid: they are ordinary item writes.
- Label texts for the `nearLimit` and `over` states.

## Acceptance criteria

- When transactions exist at 1 October 00:30 and 31 October 23:30 (Europe/Prague), then both are in the October summary; a trashed transaction is not.
- When spent is 6 200 and the total budget is 10 000, then `percent` is 62 and `state` is `inBudget`; on 16 October `daysLeft` is 15.
- When a category has a budget of 5 000 and spending of 4 750, then its `percent` is 95.
- When no category has a budget, then `percent` is `null` and `state` is `inBudget`.
- When `month` is malformed, then the response is 400 `validation_failed`.
- When two tenants have transactions, then each caller sees only their own.

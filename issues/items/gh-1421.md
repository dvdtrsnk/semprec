## Context

Model tier rationale: A destructive change to the purge path that must only remove blobs owned one-to-one by the purged item, with the bytes deleted after commit as the existing ADR requires.

When a Files item is purged after 30 days (`trash/purgeExpiredTrash.ts` calling `deleteItemDependents` in `chokePoint/itemTrash.ts`), only the blob named by `properties.file.blobId` is removed, and only when nothing else references it (`deleteBlobIfUnreferenced`). The normalized audio blob that the prepare step creates with `createBlob` (`computed.prepare.normalizedBlobId`, one per Files item) is never referenced by any property and stays orphaned forever. The playable rendition added by #1577 (`computed.prepare.playbackBlobId`) is the same kind of derived blob.

This issue makes the purge of a Files item also delete the derived blobs named by its `computed.prepare` checkpoint, unconditionally because they are one-to-one with the item, except that a `playbackBlobId` equal to the original blob is not deleted separately. Rows are deleted in the purge transaction and the bytes after the commit (`docs/adr/2026-09-28-blob-bytes-deleted-best-effort-after-the-commit.md`). #1577 and #1642 build on it.

## Task

Load `state-writes`, `io-hardening` and `testing`.

1. Add `deleteDerivedBlob(client, blobId)` to `blobs/blobsStore.ts` that deletes the blob row and returns its `storage_key`, or null when absent.
2. In `deleteItemDependents` read the purged item's `computed.prepare` (tolerating a missing or older checkpoint), delete `normalizedBlobId` and `playbackBlobId` through `deleteDerivedBlob` (skipping a playback id equal to the original blob id), and return all storage keys; update `purgeExpiredTrashSubtree` and its caller to remove every returned key after the commit.
3. Tests: purge removes original, normalized and playback rows and, after commit, their bytes; an item without a checkpoint behaves as before; a playback blob equal to the original is deleted once; a blob shared by another item is not touched.

### Out of scope

## Acceptance criteria

- When a recording's Files item is purged, then the original and the derived blobs are removed, and their bytes after the commit.
- When the purge is rolled back, then no blob row or bytes are removed.
- When the playback blob is the original, then it is deleted exactly once.

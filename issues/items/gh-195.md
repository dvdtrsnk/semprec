## Context

This issue establishes the first implementation slice of the `mail-live-sync` batch; #196 builds on it.

## Task

Build mailsync composition root: discover active accounts; exactly one provider lifecycle each; stop on deactivation; restore cursors/expiry on restart; isolate failures. Signals enter existing idempotent reconcile, never parallel ingestion. Maintain next_expected_activity_at and periodic safety-net reconcile.

## Scope

### In scope

- Task deliverables, migrations, integration, tests, and necessary documentation.

### Out of scope

- Provider transports/writeback follow.

## Acceptance criteria

- Activation/deactivation starts/stops one lifecycle.
- Restart avoids duplicate watchers.
- Signals converge through reconcile.
- One account failure does not stop others.

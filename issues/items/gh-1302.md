## Decisions

Decisions adopted while drafting, and questions still open for the product owner (per chunk of the batch):

### Decisions: tasks-recurrence-1

#### Adopted decisions
- Recurrence model (a related issue question) -> option A: an occurrence with state is a Tasks item, completing creates the next instance, future occurrences are only projected; Tasks.recurrence owner-user variant rejected -> design note recommends A, keeps generic queries, counts and search. Consequence: #1665 is kept (it would have dropped out under option B).
- Already materialized future occurrences when a series is edited -> series PATCH updates template, current instance and open (notDone) materialized occurrences dated on or after the current one; done, wontDo, missed untouched; per-occurrence fields never part of a series change -> conservative, matches the design (edit applies to future occurrences).
  -> Decided by the product owner: editing a series does not move occurrences that were already moved on their own; their dates are their own values and stay unchanged (#1667).
- Yearly recurrence -> Decided by the product owner: add a fixed rule kind `yearDates` {month, day} and a `yearly` preset, because it is one general rule and yearly recurrence is common (anniversaries, fees). Delivered by #2010 (yearly rule and preset).
- Removing the date of a recurring task -> rejected with 400; user turns recurrence off with DELETE -> conservative.
- Date shown in occurrence detail -> series start (anchor_date), as in the design; occurrenceDate kept as a separate field in the response.
- Source of truth for missed (nightly job vs read model) -> nightly job writes stored missed (#1677 kept), readers do not compute it; materialized future occurrences are kept.
  -> Decided by the product owner: the nightly job, consistent with the day rollover rule (#1668, #1677).
- Occurrences finished before the backfill -> stay without seriesId.
- Backfill implemented as a per-tenant SQL cutover (registered in tenantCutoverMigrations.ts) because the data-migration converter cannot see task_recurrence.
- O7 missed rule on done to notDone uses existing rule helpers from nextDueDate.ts, not the O3 functions, since O7 is not blocked by O3.
- Semp access to recurrence -> Decided by the product owner: Semp sets, changes and ends a task's recurrence through the generic item write with the same risk rules as other properties; changing or ending an existing series changes future occurrences and therefore always requires approval. Delivered by #2011 (Semp recurrence write); no module agent tool and no proposal card.

#### User-visible behavior (epic paragraph)
A recurring task becomes a series: it has a stable identity, a rule (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly) set and cleared from the task detail or by Semp (a change or end of a series by Semp waits for the user's approval), and one date per occurrence. Editing time, name or other fields from an occurrence changes the whole series and its open future occurrences, while ticking or dragging one occurrence affects only that day. Future occurrences are shown in the calendar even though only the current one is stored, and a future day can be ticked in advance. Completing an occurrence creates the next one once, on the right date, even when completed early or late. An occurrence nobody completed is marked "Nesplněno" (missed) at midnight when the next one arrives, the open status is now called "Nehotovo", and the series continues with the latest occurrence.

## Context

Model tier rationale: A final grouped breakdown with a new i18n label set and a share calculation, in a file already in this chain and on data shaped by the earlier issues.

Every recorded AI call carries an operation (`ai_gateway_calls.operation`, migration 0034): `agent_turn` (Semp chat and automatic tasks, `services/semprec-ai-gateway/src/piMessagesHandler.ts`), `transcription_transcribe`, `transcription_diarize` (`audioHandler.ts`), `transcript_summary`, `transcript_speaker_suggestion` and `agent_guidance_drift` (`completeHandler.ts`). The AI usage report (`backend/packages/data/src/aiGateway/aiUsageReport.ts`) does not group by it, so a usage screen cannot show where the money went by kind of activity.

This issue adds `byOperation` to `GET /api/ai-usage`, built on #1472 (which in turn follows #1467) and the same counting rules. The catalog of display names for operations lives next to the model names from #1442.

Decisions: (1) an operation the catalog does not know gets its raw key as label, so a later operation (for example one registered by another issue) is never hidden, and its own issue adds a label when it is introduced. (2) A NULL operation is reported as the operation `other`, labeled "Ostatní" / "Other". (3) The per-trigger and per-project split is not part of this issue; all chat and automatic agent turns are one activity. (4) The two voice operations `voice_transcribe_stream` (streaming speech-to-text of the live voice mode) and `voice_synthesize` (text-to-speech of spoken answers and read-aloud) are one activity, reported under the merged key `voice` with the label "Hlas" / "Voice", the same way the drift operations are merged under `agent_guidance_drift`; they are introduced by the semp-voice issues of epic #1307, and until they exist the mapping has nothing to merge.

## Task

Load `canonical-keys` and `testing`.

1. Add operation labels to the catalogs `backend/packages/data/src/aiGateway/i18n/cs.json` and `en.json` (created by #1442), keyed by the English canonical operation key: `operation.agent_turn` (cs "Semp – chat a automatické úlohy", en "Semp - chat and automatic tasks"), `operation.transcription_transcribe` ("Přepis nahrávek" / "Recording transcription"), `operation.transcription_diarize` ("Rozpoznání mluvčích" / "Speaker recognition"), `operation.transcript_summary` ("Shrnutí schůzek" / "Meeting summaries"), `operation.transcript_speaker_suggestion` ("Návrhy mluvčích" / "Speaker suggestions"), `operation.agent_guidance_drift` ("Kontrola instrukcí AGENT.md" / "AGENT.md instruction check"), `operation.other` ("Ostatní" / "Other"). Also map the future behavioral drift operation `agent_behavior_drift` to the same label as `agent_guidance_drift`, so both are one activity: add the key `operation.agent_behavior_drift` with the same text and merge both under the key `agent_guidance_drift` in the result. Add the keys `operation.voice` (cs "Hlas", en "Voice"), `operation.voice_transcribe_stream` and `operation.voice_synthesize` (same texts), and merge both voice operations under the key `voice` in the result.
2. Add a pure `resolveOperationLabel(catalogs, locale, operation)` to `backend/packages/data/src/aiGateway/modelDisplayNames.ts` (or a sibling file) with the fallback order: requested locale, en, raw operation key.
3. In `aiUsageReport.ts`, add `byOperation: [{operation, label, costUsd, share, callCount}]`, one entry per merged operation over `[from, to)`, failed calls excluded from `callCount`, ordered by `costUsd` descending then `operation`. `share` is `costUsd / totalCostUsd` as a fraction between 0 and 1 (0 for every entry when the total is 0).
4. Tests: the sum of `byOperation[].costUsd` equals `totalCostUsd`; the drift operations merge; the two voice operations merge into one `voice` entry labeled Hlas / Voice; a NULL operation becomes `other`; an unknown operation key keeps its raw key as label; labels follow the user's locale; shares sum to 1 (within rounding) when the total is above 0.

## Scope

### In scope

- The `byOperation` array with localized labels and shares.

### Out of scope

- A breakdown by trigger (user, heartbeat, supervisor) or by project.
- Labels for operations of work that is not yet recorded (mail triage, embeddings, inbox suggestions, transcript event recommendation); each such change adds its own label when its operation is introduced.
- The behavioral drift operation itself: this issue only prepares its label; recording it belongs to the drift detection work in #1629.

## Acceptance criteria

- When the range has calls from several operations, then `byOperation` has one entry per operation and the costs sum to `totalCostUsd`.
- When the user's locale is `cs`, then `agent_turn` is labeled "Semp – chat a automatické úlohy"; for `en` the English text.
- When an operation is unknown, then its label is the raw key.
- When both guidance drift operations occur, then they appear as one entry.
- When the period contains `voice_transcribe_stream` and `voice_synthesize` calls, then `byOperation` has one entry `voice` labeled "Hlas" (cs) / "Voice" (en) whose `costUsd` is the sum of both.
- When `totalCostUsd` is 0, then every `share` is 0.

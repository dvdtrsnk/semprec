## Context

Model tier rationale: A final grouped breakdown with a new i18n label set and a share calculation, in a file already in this chain and on data shaped by the earlier issues.

Every recorded AI call carries an operation (`ai_gateway_calls.operation`, migration 0034): `agent_turn` (Semp chat and automatic tasks, `services/semprec-ai-gateway/src/piMessagesHandler.ts`), `transcription_transcribe`, `transcription_diarize` (`audioHandler.ts`), `transcript_summary`, `transcript_speaker_suggestion` and `agent_guidance_drift` (`completeHandler.ts`). The AI usage report (`backend/packages/data/src/aiGateway/aiUsageReport.ts`) does not group by it, so a usage screen cannot show where the money went by kind of activity.

This issue adds `byOperation` to `GET /api/ai-usage`, built on #1472 (which in turn follows #1467) and the same counting rules. The catalog of display names for operations lives next to the model names from #1442.

Decisions: (1) an operation the catalog does not know gets its raw key as label, so a later operation (for example one registered by another issue) is never hidden, and its own issue adds a label when it is introduced. (2) A NULL operation is reported as the operation `other`, labeled "Ostatní" / "Other". (3) The per-trigger and per-project split is not part of this issue; all chat and automatic agent turns are one activity. (4) The two voice operations `voice_transcribe_stream` (streaming speech-to-text of the live voice mode) and `voice_synthesize` (text-to-speech of spoken answers and read-aloud) are one activity, reported under the merged key `voice` with the label "Hlas" / "Voice", the same way the drift operations are merged under `agent_guidance_drift`; they are introduced by the semp-voice issues of epic #1307, and until they exist the mapping has nothing to merge.

### Out of scope

## Acceptance criteria

- When the range has calls from several operations, then `byOperation` has one entry per operation and the costs sum to `totalCostUsd`.
- When the user's locale is `cs`, then `agent_turn` is labeled "Semp – chat a automatické úlohy"; for `en` the English text.
- When an operation is unknown, then its label is the raw key.
- When both guidance drift operations occur, then they appear as one entry.
- When the period contains `voice_transcribe_stream` and `voice_synthesize` calls, then `byOperation` has one entry `voice` labeled "Hlas" (cs) / "Voice" (en) whose `costUsd` is the sum of both.
- When `totalCostUsd` is 0, then every `share` is 0.

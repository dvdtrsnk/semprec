Spec amendment (boundary-rule exemption). `chokePoint/itemTrash.ts` on develop imports `../blobs/blobsStore.js`, `../docs/docsStore.js` and `../library/itemAutomationStore.js` for the cascade delete/restore/purge path. All three folders are in the rule's `to.path` list, and hooks for `itemDeleteWithClient` are out of scope here, so a `from.pathNot` that exempts only `schemaCoreModuleManifest.ts` cannot satisfy "on the merged tree it passes". The Task and Acceptance criteria now exempt `itemTrash.ts` as well, which is what PR #840 already implements and its ADR already documents.

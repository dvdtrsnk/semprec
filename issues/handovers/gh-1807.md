## What was delivered

- Route `GET /api/inbox/cards` (manifest route `listInboxCards`, justification single-consumer-read, handler `createInboxCardsRouteHandler` in `inbox/inboxRouteHandlers.ts`) over the new read model `inbox/inboxCardsStore.ts`. Tenant and user come only from `ctx.identity.user`; the route reads no query parameters; the store issues only SELECTs inside `withTransaction`.
- The stack: pending approvals of tick and MCP runs (the same resolver as `GET /api/approval-requests?status=pending&source=tick,mcp`, `listApprovalRequestsQueuePage`, oldest first, delegated runs resolved to their root run), then the open Processing proposal cards of kind inbox and transcript (statuses proposed, computing, computeFailed, needsClarification, invalid) in the groups proposed (computing and computeFailed included), invalid, needsClarification, transcript. Inside a group: capture date and time of the Inbox item (the instant of the transcript date for a transcript card), then id.
- At most 200 cards; `total` counts every open card except computing and computeFailed ones, approvals past the first page included.
- Card fields: `cardId`, `source`, `cardKind`, `baseKind` (`inboxCardKind.ts`), `status`, `version` (item `updatedAt`, null for an approval), `resolvedSource` (union tagged by `kind`: `inboxItem` with id/text/date/time/type {id,name,emoji}, `transcript` with id/name/completedAt, or `run`), `context`, `targets` [{databaseKey,label}] localized through the Inbox catalogs entries `card.target.<baseKind>` (11 base kinds, en and cs), and the effective envelope (the stored proposal).
- Route matrix: slice `routeMatrix/inboxCards.ts` (`INBOX_CARDS_ROUTES`), the ROUTE_CASES isolation case `inbox cards` and the ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES mapping were added together.
- `inbox/inboxCatalogs.ts` holds the cached Inbox catalog loader that `invalidReason.ts` had privately; `invalidReason.ts` imports it.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The Task does not name the field that holds the resolved source: it is `resolvedSource`.
2. `version` is null for an approval card.
3. The group of a transcript-kind card is always the last group, whatever its status.
4. Targets carry the label `card.target.<baseKind>`; a card has no separate kind label field because the Task's field list has none. A database without a key is left out of `targets`.
5. Ordering and the cut at 200 are done in memory over a light query of all open cards; only the page is then loaded. A card trashed between the two reads is left out (tested).
6. Route matrix slice file added, with its ROUTE_CASES case and ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES mapping (spec seam 1). At landing the slice import and spread conflicted with `LIBRARY_ROUTES` (gh-1793) in `routeMatrix.ts` and both were kept.
7. A stored envelope that does not parse still throws and fails the whole read, as `journalInboxCompute` does.

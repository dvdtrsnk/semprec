## What was delivered

- `convertEventDateToWallClock` (`calendar/eventDateMigration.ts`, barrel export of `@semprec/data`) as the Events data migration 1.0.0 to 1.1.0, declared as the last `dataMigrations` entry of the systemDatabases manifest, after the three Tasks migrations. An instant or wall-clock `date` is split into the calendar date, `timeFrom`, `timeTo` (start plus one hour, clipped to 24:00) and `time` through `deriveEventTimeProperties`/`deriveTaskTime` in the tenant zone; a date-only or missing date is returned as the same object; stored `timeFrom`, `timeTo` and `endDate` are never overwritten; converting its own output is the identity (a second run changes no row and leaves `updated_at` alone).
- An unreadable date is returned unchanged and logged with the item id as the whole reason (`context.log(context.itemId)`).
- Review fixes (`f014d207e`): an instant date for an invalid tenant zone is refused with a `ValidationError` (`assertValidTimezone`, new export `eventDateIsInstant` in `eventTime.ts`, the predicate of the write hook), so the batch rolls back and the transition stays unrecorded and is retried once the zone is fixed (without it luxon wrote "Invalid DateTime" over the stored instant); a split whose date is not a calendar date or whose start is not HH:MM is logged and returned unchanged; an Event with a stored `timeFrom` and no `timeTo` now gets the end derived from that start.

# Handover for gh-1431

Journal period keys can now be parsed, validated and described by pure functions in the new `backend/packages/data/src/journal/journalPeriods.ts`, exported from the `@semprec/data` barrel. `computeJournalPeriodKey` is unchanged.

- **`parseJournalPeriodKey(type, key, timezone = "UTC")`** returns `{ type, period, reference }`, where `reference` is the first instant of the period at 00:00 in `timezone`. It throws `ValidationError` with `details: { field: "period" }` for a bad format, a non-canonical spelling or a non-existent period (`2027-W53`, `2026-W54`, `2026-02-30`, `2026-Q5`, `2026-1`, `2026-W5`). Validity comes from luxon, and a final `computeJournalPeriodKey(type, reference) === key` round trip rejects anything non-canonical. An unknown `type` fails with `field: "type"` and an invalid zone with `field: "timezone"`.
- **`describeJournalPeriods(date: Date, timezone)`** returns `{ year, quarter, month, week, day }`, each `{ period, from, to }` with `YYYY-MM-DD` inclusive bounds. The week runs Monday to Sunday and may cross a month or year boundary.
- **`weeksOfMonth(monthKey)`** returns every ISO week overlapping the month, in order.
- **Tests:** `journal/__tests__/journalPeriods.unit.test.ts` has 31 unit tests and needs no database. They cover `computeJournalPeriodKey` for all five types and the year turn, the 53-week edge cases, the Prague-midnight case and `weeksOfMonth('2026-10')`. I also added the three new exports to `publicSurface.test.ts`.
- **Notes for dependants (#1537, #1541, #1837):** call `parseJournalPeriodKey` on any client-supplied key and pass the tenant or user timezone when you need `reference` in that zone. `weeksOfMonth` takes only a month key and returns calendar dates, which do not depend on a zone.

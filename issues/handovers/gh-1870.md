## What was delivered

- `POST /api/proposals/:id/revise`, answering `200` with the updated card item. The body is parsed strictly: only the keys `entityKind`, `target`, `properties`, `message` and `ifVersion`; `entityKind`, `target` and `properties` come all together or not at all (a partial envelope is `400 validation_failed` naming the missing key); an empty body, or one with neither envelope nor message, is `400`; `entityKind` must be one of `pageContent`, `database`, `relation`; `message` is at most 2000 UTF-16 code units; every string field refuses U+0000 and `properties` is walked iteratively for U+0000 in strings and keys (400 naming the field, instead of a Postgres jsonb 500).
- With an envelope the revise behaves as before (validation through `assertValidEnvelopeForCard`, status `proposed`, one history entry; the text is `Revised by user.` when no `message` is sent). The target of a relation envelope may be any live item that passes the validation, not only a listed candidate.
- A `message` alone is accepted only on a `kind = transcript` card: one user history entry is appended and nothing else changes (status, proposal, aiProposal untouched, no Journal recompute). On any other card it is `400 validation_failed` with `details.field = "message"`.
- `ifVersion` is the card's `updatedAt`; it is handed to the card update, so a mismatch is the existing `409 version_conflict` carrying the current card and nothing is written. The envelope is validated before the stale check (an invalid envelope on a stale card is refused as invalid).
- A locked (`confirmed` or `rejected`) card, a card whose `invalidReason` is `sourceDeleted` and a card with an unsupported stored `entityKind` are refused before the message-only branch. A speaker-mapping card cannot be revised into an Event proposal (400 on `entityKind`).
- A foreign tenant's card id, an unknown id, an id of another database and a trashed card all answer `404 not_found`.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The route matrix entry lives in the slice module `routeMatrix/proposals.ts`, not `routeMatrix.ts` (the slice that already holds confirm and reject).
2. The Task's `proposalActions.test.ts` is `backend/packages/data/src/__tests__/proposalActions.test.ts`.
3. Seam with gh-1845: the chain was cut before the reject route existed and placed the manifest entry after confirm; at the merge the reject entry, handler, matrix entry, isolation case and mapping of the tip were kept and the revise ones placed after them, as the Task's ordering requires.
4. Choices where the Task is silent: the optional `actingUserId` fifth parameter (the confirm route's pattern); the unsupported-stored-`entityKind` refusal (409) also applies to a message alone; a message alone does not enqueue the Journal inbox recompute because no status changes; the route does not take `relations` (unknown key); the 409 is asserted at handler level as `ConflictError` with `details.current`, while the `details.currentItem` projection is the existing adapter contract and is not re-tested over HTTP.
5. The revise body parser has its own `ifVersion` parsing (non-empty string, no NUL) and does not reuse the reject route's `parseIfVersion` helper; the two accept the same shape. A later cleanup could unify them.

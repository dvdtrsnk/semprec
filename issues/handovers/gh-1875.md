## What was delivered

- Migration `0164_databases_purge_at.sql`: nullable `databases.purge_at timestamptz` and the partial index `databases_purge_at_idx` (`WHERE purge_at IS NOT NULL`). Additive, `IF NOT EXISTS`.
- `archiveDatabase` stamps `purge_at = now() + RETENTION_DAYS * 24 hours`; `restoreDatabase` clears it. `DatabaseRow.purgeAt` (`Date | null`).
- `purgeExpiredArchivedDatabases(pool, storage, alternateStorage?)` in `trash/purgeArchivedDatabases.ts`: assigns a deadline to archived user databases that have none or a stale one (system databases excluded), selects the expired ones, and purges each in separate transactions: chunks of at most 500 items (inline databases owned by the chunk's items first, depth first, with a cycle guard) through `hardDeleteItemsOfDatabase`, `deleteItemDependents` and the item purge hooks; blob bytes removed after each commit, best effort, from both storage roots; a last transaction removes the dependent rollup properties, the paired relation properties of other databases and the one-way relation properties of other databases that target the purged database, then `dropDatabaseWithPartition`; schema invalidations are announced after the commit. Every deleting transaction takes `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` on the expired archived root and stops when a restore (or a re-archive with an earlier deadline) committed first. An interrupted purge resumes on the next run.
- `handleItemTrashPurgeSweepTask` runs the archived-database purge after `purgeExpiredTrash` per tenant; a failure of one stage or one database is logged with tenant and database ids and the error class, does not skip the rest, and fails the task at the end.
- `GET /api/trash/databases` (gh-1873) returns the stored `purgeAt` and `daysLeft` (calendar days to the day of `purge_at` in the tenant zone, floor 0).
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-13-archived-databases-are-purged-after-the-trash-retention-period.md` (accepted) records the lock, the order, what is accepted (no tenant advisory lock, no search reindex of other databases, a restore after the deadline between two chunks is not protected, computed values of deleted rollups stay).

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. `RETENTION_DAYS` moved to `trashRetention.ts` because `databasesStore.ts` may not import `trash/` (chokepoint-knows-no-domain).
2. The deadline is 24-hour days (`make_interval` hours), like an item's `purgeAt`, so it does not move with daylight saving; the Task says days. `daysLeft` counts calendar days to the day of `purge_at`, which can differ by one from the item rule near a DST change; documented in the route, `daysUntil` and the ADR.
3. `purgeExpiredArchivedDatabases` takes an optional third argument `alternateStorage` so blob bytes go from both roots (ADR 2026-11-01).
4. Item purge hooks run for each deleted item beyond the Task's `deleteItemDependents` (the library cover-candidate hook applies to items of any database).
5. New store functions live in the owning stores, not in one file; `rollup/purgeDependencies.ts` is new because `rollup/dependencies.ts` is re-exported whole from the barrel.
6. The write-safety baseline was edited by hand for the changed archive and restore UPDATE texts and the two bulk DELETEs.
7. No rollup-of-rollup closure (the validation forbids it). The purge takes no tenant advisory lock and queues no search reindex of other databases; the ADR states both.
8. The stale-deadline predicate is wider than the security reviewer suggested (`purge_at < archived_at + retention` instead of `purge_at < archived_at`) so that a stale deadline that is still ahead is also restamped.

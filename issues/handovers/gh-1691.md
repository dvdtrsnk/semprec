# Handover for gh-1691

Purge mail rows when an email item is purged. Candidate commit `6edf9858dbfef1ce315de5ab54cf007dfacd921e` landed on `feature/backend-parallel` with merge `364bd6f17a6b28f9b5e966e0bf57dee615771e31` (chain `e1-027-mail`, its only member). The source is one commit, the candidate. The acceptance receipt is `issues/batches/backend/acceptance/sources/gh-1691.md`.

The declared blocker gh-2031 (refuse a generic delete of a Mailboxes item) was still open at this landing. The text of gh-1691 marks it as `Shared-file ordering`, so `docs/adr/2026-11-11-a-reviewed-chain-may-land-ahead-of-an-open-shared-file-ordering-blocker.md` applies. Nothing of gh-2031 was needed. gh-1697, which names gh-1691 as a shared-file-ordering blocker, landed before this source.

## What was delivered

- The mail module's item purge hook `mailItemPurgeHook` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailPurgeHook.ts`), registered in `backend/packages/data/src/domainWriteHooks.ts` with `registerItemPurgeHook`. It runs inside the transaction of the permanent purge (`purgeExpiredTrashSubtree`), once per hard-deleted row, after that row's built-in dependents were deleted.
- Scope: the hook acts only on an item of the tenant's system Emails database or system Mailboxes database (`databases.system` is set and `owner_module_id` is `emails` or `mailboxes`). For any other item it returns before its first statement. A non-system database that carries the emails module id is another database.
- Emails item, in this order: the `mail_message_meta` row and, when it was the last row naming its thread, the thread; the `mail_attachments` rows; the `mail_message_flag_sync_state` rows.
- An emptied thread: no `mail_message_meta` row names it. The row of a message in the Trash counts as naming it. The hook soft-deletes the Threads item the `mail_threads` row is bound to through `itemDeleteWithClient` as the system writer and then deletes the row; the `mail_thread_aliases` rows that point at it go by their foreign key cascade. A Threads item already in the Trash is left as it is. A Threads item that is already purged, or a row bound to no item, does not fail the purge: the row is deleted.
- Mailboxes item, in the removal's order: the `external_credentials` row, the `mail_account_sync_state` row, the `mail_folder_sync_state` rows of every Folders item of the tenant that has no `Folders.mailbox` edge, and the `mail_account_server_settings` row.
- The meta delete takes the tenant's mail-thread mutation guard (`lockMailThreadMutation`) before its first statement on a mail table. The hook is a participating writer of that guard and takes it after the purge's own locks.
- Every delete goes through the store that owns the table and may match no row. Blob rows, blob bytes and the Files items created from attachments are not deleted.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-11-mail-purge-hook-takes-the-thread-guard-after-the-purge-locks.md` (accepted, standalone): the lock order, the handling of an emptied thread and how a purged mailbox's folders are found.

  - `deleteMailAttachmentsOfMessage(client: PoolClient, messageItemId: string): Promise<number>` in `mail/attachments.ts`.

- Behaviour later sources and tests meet:
  - After the purge of an Email no `mail_message_meta`, `mail_attachments` or `mail_message_flag_sync_state` row names it. A test that purges an Email and expects one of these rows to survive now fails. `inbox/__tests__/inboxAttachmentsReferences.test.ts` (issue #1564) pinned that and was adapted: its scenario "retains the surviving mail reference" keeps the Email out of the purge.
  - The purge of an Email can trash a Threads item (the emptied thread's) in the same transaction. That item stays in the Trash for the retention and is purged by a later sweep.
  - The purge of an Email waits at the mail-thread guard while another transaction of the tenant holds it, with the tenant's purge lock and the item's row lock held. The pool's 60-second statement timeout bounds the wait; then the purge call fails with nothing of that item purged and the sweep tries it again at its next run.
  - Lock order of an Email purge: the tenant's purge lock, the item's row lock, the rows of the built-in dependents, the mail-thread guard, the meta row, the thread row, the Threads item's row. Ingest and the reconcilers take the guard first. A new holder of the guard that then locks an Emails or Mailboxes item can deadlock with a purge; PostgreSQL aborts one side.
  - The purge of a Mailboxes item deletes the folder sync state of every folder of the tenant without a mailbox edge, not only of the purged mailbox's folders.
- The hook does not delete: `mail_drafts` and `mail_message_move_sync_state` rows of a purged Email or Mailbox, `credential_access_log` rows, the `mail_folder_sync_state` row of a purged Folders item, the `mail_threads` row of a purged Threads item, `blobs` rows, blob bytes and Files items.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. Emptied thread. The issue names only the `mail_threads` row. Since gh-1573 a row is bound to a Threads item, so the hook also soft-deletes that item, as the existing failed-claim cleanup does. Unlike that cleanup it does not fail on a Threads item that is already purged or on a row bound to no item, because a purge hook that throws keeps the Email in the Trash at every sweep.
2. Folders of a purged mailbox. The purge deletes every relation edge of the purged Mailboxes item before the hooks run, so the folders cannot be found through `Folders.mailbox`. The hook deletes the `mail_folder_sync_state` row of every Folders item of the tenant that has no mailbox edge. This is wider than the issue's "rows of its folders": it also matches a folder that had no mailbox before (one the Gmail pseudo-folder cleanup detached and trashed, one of a mailbox purged before this hook existed, one created without a mailbox). The reviewers found no row a sync pass can still use among them. The owner may want to confirm the width.
3. Lock order. The hook takes the mail-thread guard after the purge's locks, the reverse of ingest and the reconcilers. `docs/adr/2026-11-01-mail-thread-mutations-use-a-tenant-transaction-guard.md` asks for an explicit contract for a new lock order; the new ADR is that contract. The wait bounded by the statement timeout and the possible deadlock with a holder of the guard are reasoned from the code and were not reproduced by a test.
4. Files beyond `Touches`: the four store files (one delete function each, so that each table is deleted through its owning store), the ADR and the adapted test `inboxAttachmentsReferences.test.ts`. `backend/dependency-cruiser.rules.json` needed no entry.
5. Acceptance criterion 4 is tested literally: a purged Folders item keeps its own `mail_folder_sync_state` row and a purged Threads item keeps its `mail_threads` row.
6. The worker implemented while gh-2031 was open, and it was still open at landing. Until gh-2031 lands, the generic delete still trashes a Mailboxes item without the removal, which is the case the Mailboxes branch of the hook cleans up.

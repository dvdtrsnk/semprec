## What was delivered

- The inbox no longer targets or proposes areas: the `areas` inbox type target (`INBOX_TYPE_TARGET_DATABASES`, the
  recognition enum), the `area` card kind with its card target label and its `systemDatabases:area` editor schema, the
  `area` proposal option field, areas as page sources and hosts, and the `areas` group and project `areaId` of the
  proposal workspace context (and its budget drop order) are removed.
- A `journalEntry` proposal has no `areaId` in its envelope, confirm path, tick snapshot, AI prompt or response
  schema; `JournalInboxProposalSummary` has no `areaItemId`/`areaName`, and `journalInboxCompute` reads no Areas
  database.
- Search has no `area` group and no `area`/`count` subtitle kinds, indexes no area titles (neither an item's own
  area nor its project's), and no longer queues the `projectItems` fan-out.
- The Areas database is still seeded; its inbox target-database option label stays until the seed removes it.

## Contracts dependents build on

- `SEARCH_GROUP_TYPES` is `task, event, project, contact, journal, file, mail, chat`; `SEARCH_SUBTITLE_KINDS` has no
  `area` or `count`.
- `InboxCardKind` has no `area`; proposal option fields are `project, people, page, company, mcpProjects`.

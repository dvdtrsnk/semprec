## What was delivered

The catalog operation `relation.related` (capability `core.item.read`, non-destructive) and `GET /api/items/:id/related`. For a record it returns a bare array of `{ databaseKey, databaseId, count, via, filter }`: the collections of other databases that hold records related to the viewed record, each with the count and the `relation_contains` filter that selects exactly those records.

- `packages/data/src/chokePoint/relatedOps.ts` counts in one SQL statement over `properties`, `relation_definitions` and `item_relations` the distinct live records of every database whose relation property points at the viewed record's database (both sides of a definition; a one-way relation is read from its target side). `relatedRoutes.ts` replaces the direct row of a database with a counted filter route: for an area, the manifest area filters of #1597 (Tasks, Files, Health records and Events through the project, own area only without a project; Projects and Companies by own area); for a company, Tasks, Events, Files and Health records through its projects, the own company only for an event or file without a project; for a project, the recordings set on it or on its events; for an area, the Threads database through the thread's project.
- Rows are ordered by the record type's `relatedOrder` (keys in the omit list skipped), then other system databases by display order, then user-created databases by name. Archived, empty and internal databases (system, not client-visible, other than Threads) are left out.
- Authorization: the application service passes `readerOf(actor)` (a `DatabaseReader`: an agent's project id or `{ externalClient: true }` for the `/mcp` human token) through the choke point facade into `relatedOps.ts`; `assertReaderDatabaseAccess` refuses a read of a record in a database the reader may not read, and `listUnreadableDatabaseIds(reader)` removes the rows of such databases (sensitive databases are refused to an external client on this path too, as gh-1998 requires). Unknown, trashed and other-tenant ids answer `null`, mapped to the item not-found error (the same answer for all three).

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The route entry is in `routeMatrix/items.ts` (slice seam), with its isolation case and handler-file mapping in the same merge.
2. The resolver gained two optional methods, and `chokePoint.ts`, `publicSurface.test.ts`, `relatedRoutes.ts`, `relatedContributors.ts`, `rows.ts`, `jsonSchema.unit.test.ts`, `itemsHandler.test.ts` and `collectionMetadata.unit.test.ts` changed although outside Touches.
3. Internal and system-process databases are those whose resolved metadata is not client-visible, with Threads exempt (the mail manifest does not make Threads client-visible but the related orders and Task step 5 require its row). Its area row uses the fixed path thread.project -> project.area.
4. A relation held by the viewed record's database with no property on the other database (a one-way relation read from its origin side, such as an Email's sender person) gives no row, because no filter could select the counted records.
5. For a company the own-company-only-without-project rule also applies to Files; a person's recordings count `Transcripts.speakers` and `Transcripts.people`; the recordings of a project are the transcripts with the project set or whose event has the project.
6. The area filters are expanded and counted at read time without the extra probe compile of `resolveDatabaseAreaFilters`; a broken declaration surfaces as the 400 of the query use.
7. The catalog outputs are TypeScript types in `rows.ts`/`port.ts`, so there is no zod output schema (the Task says `schemas.ts`).
8. Direct rows of a database named in a route are replaced by the route's count; a route with zero records removes the direct row. Contributor rows replace the generic row.
9. The ADR for the contributor registry was missing in the first version (review finding) and is added; the worker's deviation 14 (no ADR) is superseded.

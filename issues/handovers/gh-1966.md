## What was delivered

A recurring event of an ICS feed now appears in the calendar timeline once per occurrence, expanded on read from stored rows; nothing of an occurrence is stored.

- Three system-owned text properties on the `subscribedEvents` database: `recurrenceRule` (the RRULE value as ical.js writes it back, with every part ical.js does not model, such as RSCALE, SKIP or an X- part, appended as the feed gave it so the rule is judged unsupported), `exceptionDates` (a JSON array text of the original starts that EXDATE excludes, sorted) and `recurrenceId` (override rows only). In the seed (`seed/seedSubscribedEvents.ts`) and, for tenants that already have the database, in an idempotent cutover step `runSubscribedEventsRecurrenceCutoverMigration` (`calendar/subscribedEventsRecurrenceCutoverMigration.ts`), registered after the gh-1962 step and after the gh-1964 entry, before the gh-1971 entry. cs and en labels in `calendar/i18n`, manifest `1.1.0` to `1.2.0`.
- Import (`calendar/subscribedEventsImport.ts`): a master keeps its RRULE and EXDATEs; a VEVENT with RECURRENCE-ID is imported as an override row identified by `(calendar, uid, recurrenceId)`; unchanged, disappeared and unreadable handling is per key. A rule outside the supported subset is stored, shown once at its start, and counted in the new `unsupportedRules` field of `SubscribedEventsImportResult` and in the import log line (counts only, no feed content). An EXDATE, RECURRENCE-ID or RRULE value that ical.js cannot read, or that names an unknown zone, makes the VEVENT unreadable (counted, the other events import, a master keeps its stored row by key; an override with no readable RECURRENCE-ID has no key and is only counted). Re-importing an unchanged feed writes nothing (updated_at included).
- Expansion (`calendar/timelineSubscribedEventSource.ts` with the new pure modules `subscribedEventRecurrence.ts` and `subscribedEventOccurrences.ts`): supported rule is FREQ DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY, INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL, BYDAY, BYMONTHDAY, BYMONTH (combinations the expansion does not define are unsupported). Occurrences are computed in the event's own zone and converted to the tenant wall clock, so the local start time is kept across daylight-saving changes. EXDATE filtering, override substitution within the same calendar and UID, the requested range limit and a cap of 100 entries per series per read. Entries stay `readOnly`.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-a-subscribed-event-series-is-expanded-from-its-stored-rows-on-read.md`.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- Properties `recurrenceRule`, `exceptionDates`, `recurrenceId` of the `subscribedEvents` database (key list `SUBSCRIBED_EVENT_RECURRENCE_KEYS`, shared by the seed and the cutover). An original start is the tenant wall-clock text `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`.
- Occurrence ids of an expanded series are `<rowId>@<date>` (`formatOccurrenceId`); override rows, rule-less rows and rows with an unsupported rule keep the row id. Timeline entries are plain `CalendarTimelineItem` with `readOnly: true` and the `subscribedEvents` database id.
- `parseRecurrenceRule(text)` returns `null` for an unsupported rule; `recurrenceDates` lists the days; `expandSubscribedSeries` places them in time (modules under `packages/data/src/calendar/`, not exported from the data barrel).
- `SubscribedEventsImportResult.unsupportedRules`.
- Cutover entry order in `tenantCutoverMigrations.ts`: gh-1962, gh-1964, gh-1966, gh-1971, gh-1973. gh-1971 names gh-1966 as a shared-file ordering blocker and already stands after it.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. gh-1877 has no extension point for subscribed events and the Events expansion takes the JSON fixed-rule vocabulary of Tasks, which cannot express RRULE; the expansion is in the gh-2005 timeline source, in two new modules.
2. The expansion is own code, not ical.js iteration (ical.js rolls 29 February to 1 March and 31 February to 3 March); recorded in the new ADR, which the Task did not name.
3. `recurrenceRule` is the RRULE as ical.js writes it back (default WKST=MO and unknown parts are not kept), plus every jCal part ical.js does not write back, appended so the rule is judged unsupported (review fix); a non-default WKST makes the rule unsupported.
4. i18n keys are in `calendar/i18n/{cs,en}.json` (as in gh-1962), not `seed/i18n`.
5. Occurrence ids are `<rowId>@<date>`.
6. Beyond the Task list, these are treated as unsupported and shown once: COUNT with UNTIL, ordinal BYDAY outside monthly or yearly-with-BYMONTH, BYMONTHDAY in weekly, yearly BYDAY/BYMONTHDAY without BYMONTH. DTSTART always counts as the first occurrence; a day a month lacks is no occurrence.
7. An EXDATE or RECURRENCE-ID naming an unknown zone makes the VEVENT unreadable.
8. A series is capped at 100 entries per read and the iteration looks at no more than 20000 periods.
9. `subscribedEventsImport.test.ts` assertions were extended for `unsupportedRules`; its 'recurrence' case and the cost test were changed because the behaviour changed.
10. The cutover entry stands after gh-1962 and, at landing, after the gh-1964 entry (moved by the queue runner), before gh-1971.
11. Web, mobile and the whole-tier runs were not touched or run (backend-only scope).

## Follow-ups from review

Reviews at `c7fe1ce21`: requirements one high finding (RSCALE, SKIP and X- parts dropped before the supported-part check, so the rule was expanded instead of shown once), rules one high finding (a malformed EXDATE or RECURRENCE-ID threw out of `parseFeed` and failed the whole feed). Both fixed in `902415eb1` and confirmed by the focused recheck (pass, no new finding). Low observations from the recheck, recorded and not fixed: a rule part with an empty name (`RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY;=x`) is filtered out and the rule is expanded as `FREQ=WEEKLY`; a few comment lines are 121-122 characters long. Not in this source and not reopened: `ICAL.parse` itself throws for RRULE values such as `COUNT=garbage` or lowercase BYDAY, which fails the whole feed with `CalendarFeedParseError` (gh-2004 behaviour, unchanged). Known limits in the ADR: a tenant zone change leaves stored original starts in the old zone until the feed is imported again; RDATE and RANGE=THISANDFUTURE are not interpreted.

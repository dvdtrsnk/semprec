# Handover for sp-c12f129d-e765-4ef1-967c-a1e4070e162b

Implementation commit: bad7ebdca.

The writes of the queued routes (`QueuedRoute`: item create, patch and delete, relations, value links, stored
occurrences, task repetition and host event, invitation answers, Inbox captures, the diary's page ensure) are sent
directly while nothing waits and the process is online. Without an answer, a `5xx` or a `429`, or while offline, they
wait in a durable per-account queue (`<account>/queue.json`) and answer what `QueuedAnswers` makes up, so the
repositories decode them as the server's; a write queued behind others while online waits up to 10 s for its own answer.

- The replay sends one write at a time in order through the window's sender; ids made on the device are replaced by the
  server's, writes naming a dropped create are dropped, a stale `ifVersion` is retried once on the current version,
  `5xx`/`429` retry with backoff (1 s doubling to 60 s with jitter) and drop after 8 attempts, `401` stops, `DELETE` +
  `404` counts as done; a create carries an `Idempotency-Key`. Every success and drop makes kept answers stale and tells
  every window; failures record only the route, the kind and the time.
- Decision: `docs/adr/2026-11-23-mobile-writes-wait-in-an-ordered-queue-without-a-connection.md`; decision record
  `issues/decisions/sp-c12f129d-e765-4ef1-967c-a1e4070e162b.md` (an offline write does not wait).

Tests: `QueueReplayTest` and `QueuedWritesRepositoryTest` (every outcome, the order, id mapping, the waits, the
idempotency key, restart persistence).

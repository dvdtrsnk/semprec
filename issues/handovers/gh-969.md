> Tenancy: `bootstrapFirstAccount` (`packages/data/src/auth/authActions.ts`) is the only path to `createUser` outside tests, and it throws `NotFoundError` — before even checking the provided token — as soon as `anyUserExists` is true, holding a Postgres advisory lock across that check to close the race for concurrent callers. The authorization boundary that *is* real and load-bearing here is actor type, not per-user ownership: `Actor.type` is `'user' | 'ai_agent' | 'system'` (e.g. `createdBy` on a view, `packages/data/src/chokePoint/authorization.ts`'s `assertViewWritable`), and it gates what an AI agent may read, write, or adopt, per `docs/adr/2026-09-10-single-writer-ownership-model.md` and `docs/adr/2026-09-10-agent-writes-are-proposals-not-direct-writes.md`. A missing or bypassable `actor.type === 'ai_agent'` check is the real privilege-escalation shape to flag here — a human-vs-human check is not.

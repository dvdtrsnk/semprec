Implements issue #180: extends the Transcripts catalog with reserved computed keys, and adds the two ways a transcription job can be enqueued.

- Registers `segments` and `summaryByInstruction` as reserved `items.computed` keys for Transcripts, guarded against future property-key collisions via `ComputedKeyRegistry`.

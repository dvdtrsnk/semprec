**What changed**

  - The `input` chain is unchanged, and there is still no `flush ruleset`.
  - The two blocked interval sets, `semprec_blocked_v4` and `semprec_blocked_v6`, hold the ranges listed in the issue.

  - `semprec_egress` runs in the order the issue gave: established/related accept, DNS on tcp and udp port 53, the loopback exception, then log and reject for the blocked sets.

  - Blocked destinations get a rate-limited log (10 per minute, prefix `semprec-egress-deny `) as a separate rule, then `reject with icmpx type admin-prohibited`.
  - The v4 and v6 sets each get their own log and reject rule pair.

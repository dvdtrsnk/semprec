## What was delivered

A read tool `calendar.timeline` (capability `calendar.read`, approval `none`, risk class `read`) that a Semp agent session and an MCP client (a run credential whose list names `calendar.read`, or a human session) call with a calendar-date range `from`/`to` of at most 93 days (strict object, `to` not before `from`, the same rules and the same exported `MAX_RANGE_DAYS` as the route). It answers the items `GET /api/calendar/timeline` answers in the full shape, inside the caller's tenant scope, including the items of hidden calendars, with one exception decided in the fix round: sleep nights. The tool leaves out the derived sleep entries (kind `sleep`) unless the caller may read them (see the ADR below). Without the capability the tool is absent and a call is answered exactly as an unknown tool.

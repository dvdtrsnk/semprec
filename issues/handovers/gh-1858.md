## What was delivered

- `GET /api/calendar/reminders?from=<instant>&hours=<1..168, default 48>`: the upcoming reminders of the caller for the phone's local scheduler, `createRemindersFeedRouteHandler(pool)` in `data/src/calendar/remindersFeedRouteHandler.ts`.
- `from` must be an ISO 8601 instant with `Z` or an offset (a `+` in an offset must be percent-encoded) and `hours` an integer 1 to 168; anything else is `400 validation_failed` with `details.field`, answered before the database is touched.
- In one repeatable-read tenant transaction: the tenant time zone via `getCalendarClock`; live Events and Tasks whose date lies in the window widened by 25 hours; mapping through `toTimelineItems`; each Event's calendar `notify` (only an explicit `false` mutes; a missing or trashed calendar mutes nothing); `computeReminderFireAt`; entries with `fireAt` in `[from, from + hours)`, ordered by `fireAt` then key, capped at 200.
- Events: a recurring series row is not listed (a not yet materialized occurrence is not listed, per the issue decision); a materialized occurrence is its own row, listed under the key `<itemId>@<occurrenceDate>:<fireAt>`; a cancelled (trashed) occurrence is absent; a moved one appears once at its new `fireAt`. Tasks are listed when notifications are on and unfinished (not done, not wontDo), at the `timeFrom` instant.
- A stored row with an unreadable schedule, or one for which `computeReminderFireAt` raises `ValidationError`, is skipped with a log of its id and does not fail the feed.

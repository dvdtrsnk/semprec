## What was delivered

- `countOpenInboxCards(client, { ownerUserId })` in `inbox/inboxCardsStore.ts` is the single definition of open Inbox items: the pending tick and MCP approval requests of the actor (`countPendingApprovalRequests`) plus the open Processing proposals cards of kind `inbox` and `transcript` in any open status except `computing` and `computeFailed`, counted with one SQL count. The list's WHERE (`listCandidates`) and the count share the predicate `openProposalCardsPredicate`, so they cannot drift.
- `listInboxCards` takes its `total` from `countOpenInboxCards`; its in-list approval paging loop (`APPROVAL_COUNT_PAGE_SIZE`, `listApprovalRequestsPage`) and the in-memory count were removed.
- `GET /api/inbox/cards/count` answers `{ total }` for the session's tenant and user (custom route `countInboxCards`, justification `single-consumer-read`, handler `createInboxCardsCountRouteHandler` in `inboxRouteHandlers.ts`, declared in `inboxModuleManifest.ts`). Tenant and user come from the session only; no query or body is read.
- Route matrix entry `inbox cards count` in `routeMatrix/inboxCards.ts`, with its `ROUTE_CASES` isolation case and `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping.

## Deviations and spec seams

1. The Task names `countOpenInboxCards(client)`; approvals are per actor, so the signature takes `{ ownerUserId }`.
2. The route matrix entry lives in the slice `routeMatrix/inboxCards.ts` (`INBOX_CARDS_ROUTES`), not in `routeMatrix.ts`.
3. The list's approval paging loop became dead once the total calls `countOpenInboxCards` and was removed.
4. `openProposalCardsPredicate` builds the WHERE of `listCandidates` too (adds a `::uuid` cast on the database id placeholder).
5. The count is read after the page is built, as a separate statement, so `total` and `cards` can differ under a concurrent write (benign read skew, not covered by a criterion).
6. At the merge with gh-2028 (same train) the import block of `inboxCardsStore.test.ts` was reconciled by keeping both sides.

## What changed
- **Migration `0068_semprec_router_graph_subscription.sql`** is additive only:
  - It creates `semprec_router` as `NOLOGIN`, `BYPASSRLS`, with `SELECT` on only `graph_subscription_id` and `tenant_id`.
  - `route_graph_subscription(text)` is `SECURITY DEFINER`, owned by `semprec_router`, with a pinned `search_path`.
  - `EXECUTE` is revoked from `PUBLIC` and granted to `semprec_data` only.
- **Store:** `routeGraphSubscription(client, subscriptionId)` returns the tenant id or `null`. The docstring of `getMailAccountSyncStateByGraphSubscriptionId` now says it is the in-tenant re-read.
- **`handleGraphChangeNotification`** keeps its signature and outcomes:
  - It routes in its own transaction, in the caller's scope. A `null` result returns `unknownSubscription`.
  - Otherwise, inside `runInTenant`, it re-reads the row under RLS, checks `clientState` and enqueues the sync job, so the job envelope carries that tenant.
  - Every path makes exactly one `clientStateMatches` call: a module-level random dummy secret when the subscription is unknown, the row vanished or no state is stored, `""` when the notification has none. Only a real match on a stored state is accepted.
- **Handler:** `graphWebhookHandler.ts` is unchanged.
- **Docs:**
  - A new ADR, `2026-10-07-cross-tenant-router-functions.md`, records the decision and rejected alternatives, and supersedes nothing.
  - `docs/operations/database-roles.md` gains a "Cross-tenant router functions (semprec_router)" section.
- **Tests:**
  - The new `graphSubscriptionRouting.test.ts` covers the catalog shape and privileges, the RLS bypass under an unrelated tenant, the `semprec_side` `42501`, and a second tenant routed through `semprec_data`.
  - The `handleGraphChangeNotification` block in `graphWebhookLifecycle.test.ts` now runs in `runAsSystem` and checks the envelope `tenantId`. A new case counts `timingSafeEqual` calls through a `vi.mock` wrapper and expects exactly one on each of the five paths.

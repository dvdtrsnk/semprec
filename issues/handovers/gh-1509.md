# gh-1509 — Link the account to its own People item

Repository-Issue: gh-1509
Implementation commit: `b2e79fad9782fd36734e4dbc25cd5a20e25c8295`.

## Delivered behavior

- Fresh tenants receive the one-way, user-owned, locked `System settings.selfPerson` relation to People with one-to-one cardinality. The users table and People items are unchanged.
- PUT /api/auth/self-person authenticates first, validates the bounded JSON body, enters the verified tenant scope and replaces or removes the edge through the generic relation writes in one transaction. The singleton row locks concurrent replacements; the selected live People target is also locked. Unknown, foreign, trashed and wrong-collection ids return the same 404 without replacing the old link.
- GET /api/auth/session adds user.personId, resolving only live People targets inside that tenant's transaction. An unset link, absent seed and a trashed or purged person yield null.
- The registered per-tenant cutover adds the missing relation schema with direct code-level migration writes under the existing properties-table lock convention. It preserves a live chosen person and otherwise fills from the scoped user's normalized address in person_email_index. Missing matches, missing users, deleted targets and unseeded tenants are left unset. No person is created.
- The route is registered in the route matrix, handler boundary map and tenant isolation harness. Existing budget-removal tests now account for the additional settings property; the frozen public export list includes the two transaction-scoped helpers.

# Handover: serve a mail sync trigger that arrives during an IMAP pass in that pass's next round

Commit on `fix/followup-mail-latency`: `6e170b50b`. Nothing was run against a real server.

**Finding check.** The follow-up was already enqueued in the release transaction, not on a later schedule. What a message delivered during a pass waited for was the rest of that pass plus a whole new job: queue pickup behind earlier jobs (ingest fan-out shares the worker's concurrency of 4 at priority 0), prologue, claim, credential decrypt and move baseline transactions, and a new IMAP connect and login.

**Changes.**
- `mail/mailAccountSyncStateStore.ts`: `takeMailSyncRerunRequest` clears `pass_rerun_requested` for the lease holder and says whether it was set.
- `mail/mailSyncJob.ts`: the IMAP branch runs in rounds inside one limiter slot and one lease. A completed round takes the rerun request in its final transaction and runs again: moves and move baseline read anew, the move phase (handed the open connection, which it owns and closes), then the reconcile on the open or a new connection. At most `IMAP_PASS_MAX_ROUNDS` (3) rounds; a request during the last round, or an unfinished backfill, goes to the release's follow-up job as before. `syncStatus` is written only when it changes. Gmail and Graph are unchanged.
- `docs/architecture/domain-mail-and-inbox.md`: Sync pass and the mailbox sync guard.

**Measured with the fake IMAP account** (80 ms per command, 500 ms connect, worker concurrency 4): message visible after the running pass resumed in ~1.2 s with 2 connections before, ~0.74 s with 1 connection after. On the real server the saving also includes the queue pickup and the job prologue.

**Tests** (`__tests__/mailSyncSingleFlight.test.ts`): the worker test now asserts rounds 2 and 3 on one connection and one keyed follow-up job after the third round; `answers a trigger during a pass without a provider call, serves it in the pass's next round and enqueues one keyed job only after the last round`; `executes a move asked for during a pass in that pass's next round` (never two connections at once); the IDLE test with a 500 ms connect asserts one connection (two before). `__tests__/mailSync.test.ts`: the overtaken-fetch test expects no queued job, since the running pass serves the busy trigger itself.

**Behaviour to know.** A job can now run up to three rounds, so one `mailAccountSync` job lasts longer while triggers keep coming; the IDLE signal from the pass's own flag STOREs costs one cheap extra round instead of a job and a connection.

**Review round** (commit `0c4e07ad1`). The IDLE test's comment no longer claims its `< 2 s` bound proves the absence of a reconnect (eight 80 ms calls plus a 500 ms connect fit under it); the one-connection assertion is what shows it.

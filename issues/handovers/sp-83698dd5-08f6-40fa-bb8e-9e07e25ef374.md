## What was delivered

- A new tenant has no `areas` database: it left `TEN_DATABASE_MODULE_IDS` (and so the inbox target-database options),
  and the seed creates no `area` relation on Projects, Events, Companies, Health records, Files or Journal, no
  `sleepSettings.area` and no "(unplanned)" area. `AREAS_MODULE_ID` is gone. No migration: the baseline had no area
  DDL.
- `data/src/areas/` (the area write hook and `GET /api/areas/:id/overview`) is deleted with its hook registrations,
  manifest route and route matrix entry; the overview path now answers like an unknown route.
- The seed, sleep and inbox i18n keys of areas are removed.
- Tests that used Areas as a fixture use other databases, area-only tests are deleted and
  `testSupport/tenantScopeWarnFiles.json` follows.
- docs/architecture (data model and writes, mail and inbox) describes derived values, mail and the inbox without
  areas.

## Contracts dependents build on

- The system databases are the nine core databases without `areas`; no database has a relation property keyed `area`
  (the text-area editor control is unrelated and stays).
- No backend source, route or schema publication refers to Areas.

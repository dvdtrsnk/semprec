The daily core task `sempProblemsPrune` now deletes `semp_problems` rows whose `resolved_at` is older than thirty days. Active problems (`resolved_at IS NULL`) are never touched. Committed locally on `wf/gh-1558-n2`; not pushed.

- **Handler:** `backend/packages/data/src/semp/sempProblemsPrune.ts` exports `SEMP_PROBLEMS_RETENTION_DAYS = 30` and `handleSempProblemsPruneTask(pool)`, which returns `{ deleted }` summed over tenants.

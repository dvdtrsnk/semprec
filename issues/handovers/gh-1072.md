## What changed

- **`sendJson`** (`adapter/http.ts`): exports `NO_STORE = "no-store"` and writes `Cache-Control` last, so a caller's own `Cache-Control` cannot weaken it. The docstring is updated.
- **Blob downloads** (`blobsHandler.ts`): the 304, 416, 206 and 200 heads all include `no-store`. The ETag, Range and Content-Disposition logic is unchanged.
- **Graph webhook** (`graphWebhookHandler.ts`): the 200 validation echo and the 202 acknowledgement include `no-store`. The validation body is still the token itself.
- **Unit tests** (`adapter/__tests__/http.unit.test.ts`): the exact-headers assertion now includes `Cache-Control`. New cases cover the default, and a caller-supplied `max-age=60` being overridden.
- **Integration tests** (`__tests__/cacheControl.test.ts`, new):
  - Every `surface: "api"` route in `ROUTE_MATRIX` is requested without credentials and must answer `no-store`.
  - With an authenticated session and one uploaded file, `GET /api/blobs/:id` is checked for 200, 206, 304 (ETag still present), 416 and 404.
  - Both Graph webhook cases (200 and 202) are checked.

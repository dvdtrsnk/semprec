## What was delivered

A recording shared into the Inbox gets a card that proposes transcribing it; nothing is transcribed until the person confirms.

- A new `transcription` proposal envelope kind (`recordingCardEnvelope.ts`, `proposalEnvelope.ts`): the target is the audio attachment id, strict properties `name`, `language`, `numSpeakersExact`, `numSpeakersMin`, `eventId`, `projectId` (null clears). Both speaker counts are bounded by `MAX_RECORDING_SPEAKERS = 2147483647`, the top of the integer columns. A client-supplied `recordedAt` or `durationSeconds` is refused. Validated by `assertValidProposalEnvelope` and, on the card, by `assertValidEnvelopeForCard` (`recordingCardValidation.ts`: an inbox card, an audio attachment of the card's source item).
- Producer (`recordingCardProducer.ts`, `transcription/recordingCardCandidates.ts`): the tick builds an `inbox` card with context role `recording`: duration and recording time read from the gh-1965 metadata (omitted when absent, no fallback), the attachment's source, and candidate Events of the recording's local day (meetings, standups and Events without type; a malformed date is skipped). The proposed name is the first non-blank line of the item's text, else a localized dated or undated default ("Recording D. M. HH:mm" or "Recording"), with no model call.
- Confirm (`recordingCardConfirm.ts`, called from `confirmProposalWithClient`, in the confirm's own transaction): locks the source Inbox item, reads the metadata, moves the attachment into Files by the gh-1703 rule (`moveAttachmentIntoFiles` in `proposalSteps.ts`), writes the recording request through `recordRecordingIntake` (extended with optional name, language, speaker counts, Event and project parameters), lets the intake enqueue `transcriptionCreate` and `transcriptionJob`, records the origin, and sets the follow-up `transcriptionQueued` only when both jobs exist after the intake. A project equal to the chosen Event's project is not written as the recording's own. A repeated confirm returns the stored results and enqueues nothing.
- Labels in `seed/i18n/cs.json` and `en.json`; small branches for the new kind in `proposalOverrides.ts`, `proposalRetention.ts`, `inboxCardKind.ts`, `proposalActions.ts`; `assertValidFileFromAttachmentStep` moved unchanged to `fileAttachmentStep.ts` to keep `inboxTickAction.ts` under its line cap.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-inbox-recording-cards-confirm-into-recording-intake.md` (extends the 2026-11-05 recording-intake ADR: the name, language, speaker and project columns of `recording_requests` get a writer); the manifest comment of `inboxModuleManifest.ts` describes recording cards.

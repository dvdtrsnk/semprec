## What was delivered

Two Event participant write routes of the systemDatabases module, both tenant-scoped and non-public.

- `PUT /api/events/:id/people` with body `{ people: [{ personId }], sendInvites: boolean, mailboxId?: uuid }` replaces the list of non-organizer participants in one transaction (`replaceEventPeople`). The Event, the listed People and the mailbox are row-locked in the writing transaction. A newly added person with an email address and `sendInvites` true gets an edge with a pending `invitation` and one `REQUEST` job; a newly added person without an address, or with `sendInvites` false, is only linked. Staying people are untouched and not invited again. A removed person whose edge carries an invitation produces one `CANCEL` job with the Event's UID in the same transaction. When an invitation is sent and the Event has no organizer edge, the organizer edge is created for the tenant's own person. The response is `{ people, invited, linkedOnly }`.
- `POST /api/events/:id/people/:personId/invite` with body `{ mailboxId }` (`inviteEventPerson`) stores a pending invitation on the edge of a linked person with an email address, creates the organizer edge when missing, and queues one `REQUEST`; it answers `200 { people }`. A person who already has an invitation is 409 `conflict`.
- `mailboxId` is required whenever an invitation is to be sent and must be a live Mailboxes item of the tenant; otherwise 400 `validation_failed` on `mailboxId`. No default mailbox was built.

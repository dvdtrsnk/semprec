**F12 — VTIMEZONE.** `generateVtimezone(zone, year)` scans the zone's changes from 1 January of `year − 1` to the end of `year + 11`. It writes the offset the window starts in (ical.js knows no offset before the first onset), then the changes before the zone's current yearly rule as RDATEs, then that rule as one STANDARD and one DAYLIGHT observance with `RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTH=…;BYDAY=…` (`nth`, `-1`, or `BYDAY=WD;BYMONTHDAY=N..N+6` for "on or after N"). A rule is used only when the tail of changes repeats it every year up to the window's last year and the observed days span the full week from N, which pins the day rule down. A zone without such a rule gets the window's changes as RDATEs and its last offset holds after them. Europe/Prague: 20 lines, 410 bytes (was ~80 lines, ~3 KB); Asia/Kolkata 8 lines; America/Nuuk (rule changed 2023) 33 lines; Africa/Casablanca (no rule) 51 lines.

Deviation from the approved Decisions: the scan reaches `year + 11`, not `year + 5`. With six years after the Event, America/Nuuk's new rule ("Saturday on or after the 24th") had only shown days that also fit "last Saturday", which is wrong in 2029, 2035 and 2040. A weekday-of-month change falls on all seven possible days within twelve years, so `year + 11` pins every such rule. The RDATE fallback therefore lists thirteen years around the Event instead of seven (still half of the previous 26).

**F20 — timeline lookups.** `calendar/timelineLookups.ts` creates one `CalendarTimelineLookups` per read (`readCalendarTimeline`, and also the reminders feed, `computeNextOccurrenceDates` and transcript event matching for their `expandEventOccurrences`/`toTimelineItems` calls). It holds the timeline's system databases (one `getDatabasesByModuleIds` statement), the calendars (default, `systemKey` → lowest id, read-only account calendars; replaces `readCalendarIds` and `calendar/systemCalendar.ts`), the user's locale, the `hostEvents` relation (`loadHostEventsRelationIn`) and `visibleDatabaseIds` for `itemsByIds` (`getItemsByIdsAmong`). `readEventsTasksIn` takes the Events database, relation and zone the timeline already has. `readCalendarCatalog` now takes a locale.

Candidate commit `0f26c4db861cc37f14076020b9424e86042aea73` landed on `feature/backend-parallel` with merge `1f4990cf0adf32805bd0a1fa62597a32a37565fa` (chain `e1-030-mail`, members gh-2030 and gh-2031, in that order). The source is four commits of the chain: `ec58fabed` "Clean up a removed mailbox at the provider after the commit", `82f7102f2` "Separate the last two points of the mailbox cleanup decision record", and from the fix round `e5128b801` "Classify a failed token exchange of the mailbox cleanup like a provider call" and `7af2650a7` "Leave a Google grant in place while another mailbox records the account". The chain landed alone. An earlier attempt to land it as the first chain of a train was abandoned at the build (see "Merge"). The acceptance receipt is `issues/batches/backend/acceptance/sources/gh-2030.md`.

The one declared blocker, gh-2029, is completed on the tip (handover `issues/handovers/gh-2029.md`). The text of gh-2030 marks no blocker as `Shared-file ordering`, and no blocker was open at this landing.

**The second acceptance criterion ("its watch or subscription is cancelled and its token is revoked at the provider") is not met as written, in two respects, and no decision record covers either.** `issues/decisions/gh-2030.md` does not exist.

1. **A Microsoft token is never revoked (BLOCKED, not delivered).** The Microsoft identity platform offers no call that revokes one application's refresh token. A `graph_api` mailbox gets its Graph subscription deleted and no revocation call; a test asserts that none is made. The stored credential is deleted by the removal.
2. **A Google watch is stopped and a Google token is revoked only for a mailbox that is the only one recording its Google account.** While another `gmail_api` mailbox of any tenant records the account, or when the removed mailbox's row records no account address, the cleanup makes no call at Google. This is the fix of the security reviewer's medium finding: both calls act on the whole Google account and reached a mailbox of another tenant.

The worker, the requirements reviewer, the fixer and the recheck each asked for the owner's decision on these. The recheck passed the chain. See "NOT RUN and BLOCKED" and "Review".

## What was delivered

- The removal transaction reads what the cleanup needs before its first write. `removeMailboxWithClient` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailboxRemoval.ts`) calls `readMailboxRemovalCleanupInput` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailboxRemovalProviderCleanup.ts`) after its three locks. From the locked `mail_account_sync_state` row it takes the sync mode, whether a Gmail watch is recorded (`gmail_watch_expires_at` is set) and the Graph subscription id. The values are returned to `removeMailbox` and live in memory only.
- The provider is the one the row's sync mode names: `gmail_api` is Google, `graph_api` is Microsoft. The Mailboxes item's `provider` property is not read. A mailbox without a sync state row, an `imap` mailbox, a `graph_api` mailbox without a recorded subscription and a mailbox whose credential is not an `oauth2_refresh_token` have no step at the provider, and their credential is not read.
- The refresh token is decrypted only when a step needs it, through `getDecryptedCredential` as actor `user` with the purpose `mailbox_removal` (one `credential_access_log` row). A credential that cannot be decrypted does not fail the removal; a failed statement of the read does.
- After the commit `removeMailbox` awaits `cleanUpRemovedMailbox`, which cannot reject. Its steps run in this order and each runs whether or not an earlier one failed:
  1. `liveSyncStop`, for every mailbox: `dependencies.mailLiveSync.stopMailbox(tenantId, mailboxItemId)` with the tenant of the current tenant scope.
  2. `gmailWatchStop` (`POST https://gmail.googleapis.com/gmail/v1/users/me/stop`) for a `gmail_api` mailbox with a recorded watch, or `graphSubscriptionDelete` (`DELETE https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/subscriptions/{id}`, the id URL-encoded) for a `graph_api` mailbox with a recorded subscription. A 404 from the subscription delete counts as done. Each first exchanges the refresh token for an access token through `refreshAccessToken`, which persists nothing.
  3. `tokenRevocation` (`POST https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke`, the token in the request body) for a `gmail_api` mailbox.
- The live-sync stop and each call to a provider are limited to 10 seconds; the token exchange keeps its own 15 seconds. The route answers only after the cleanup, in the worst case after 45 seconds for a Gmail mailbox with a watch and 35 seconds for a Graph mailbox with a subscription.
- A failed step logs one `warn` line "Mailbox removal cleanup step failed" with `{ mailboxItemId, step, classification }`. The classification is one of `notConfigured`, `credentialUnavailable`, `timeout`, `providerRejected`, `failed`. The caught error is never logged. A failed token exchange is classified like a direct call (fix commit `e5128b801`): `timeout` when the exchange's own limit ended it, `providerRejected` for an answer outside 2xx, `failed` otherwise.
- The shared-account rule (fix commit `7af2650a7`). Inside the removal transaction, before the sync state row is deleted, `getGmailAccountUse` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailAccountSyncStateStore.ts`) reads the mailbox's own `gmail_watch_email_address` under row-level security and asks the router function `route_gmail_address` whether another mailbox records that address. It is one statement and returns only `sole`, `shared` or `unknown`. For `shared` and `unknown` the token is not decrypted, no call is made at Google, and each skipped step logs one `info` line "Mailbox removal cleanup step skipped" with the classification `accountShared` or `accountUnknown`. The answer of the removal does not differ.
- `MailLiveSyncRoot.stopMailbox(tenantId, mailboxItemId)` (`backend/packages/data/src/mail/mailLiveSyncRoot.ts`) waits for every discovery pass and every `restartAccount` in flight, stops the entry once when it is hosted for `tenantId`, and resolves only after that stop finished. A caller inside the scope of another tenant stops nothing.
- `createMailLiveSyncControl` (`backend/services/semprec-api/src/mailLiveSyncControl.ts`) forwards the container's `mailLiveSync.stopMailbox` to the bound root and refuses with `CustomRouteConfigurationError` while no root is bound. `startMailbox` still refuses every call.
- `getCredentialType(client, itemId)` was added to `backend/packages/data/src/credentials/externalCredentialsStore.ts`. It reads the type only and writes no access log row.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-11-a-removed-mailbox-is-cleaned-up-at-the-provider-from-memory-after-the-commit.md` (accepted) records seven decision points and the consequences.
- No migration, table, column, route, generic operation, job name, hook registration, tenant cutover entry, export from the data barrel, module manifest entry or i18n key was added.

## Contracts dependents build on

- `removeMailbox(pool, mailboxItemId, options)` still answers whether a live mailbox was removed. `RemoveMailboxOptions` gained `cleanupDependencies?: MailboxRemovalCleanupDependencies` and `cleanupStepTimeoutMs?: number`. A caller that passes no `cleanupDependencies` gets `notConfigured` for the live-sync stop and for the steps that need an OAuth client, and a sole Gmail mailbox's token is still revoked through the global `fetch`.
- `createMailboxRemovalRouteHandler(pool, dependencies, options?)`: the signature changed from `(pool)`. The custom-route mount calls every factory with `(pool, dependencies)`, so the composition is unchanged. `options.cleanupStepTimeoutMs` is for tests and is not reachable from a request.
- `MailboxRemovalCleanupDependencies` is `{ oauth?: { gmail?, msGraph? }, mailLiveSync?: { stopMailbox }, outboundHttp? }`. `CustomRouteDependencies` of `semprec-api` is assignable to it.
- `MailLiveSyncRoot` has the new required member `stopMailbox`. **Every object that implements or stubs `MailLiveSyncRoot`, or is passed to `createMailLiveSyncControl().bind`, must provide `stopMailbox`**, or the build fails.
- `stopMailbox` does not keep a later pass from hosting the mailbox again. The mailbox being in the trash does. A caller that stops a mailbox whose item stays live will see it hosted again within 60 seconds.
- `DELETE /api/mail/mailboxes/:id` answers `200 { id }` also when a cleanup step failed or was skipped. Its answer may now take up to 45 seconds. The response does not show whether a call was made at the provider.
- New stored value: the `credential_access_log` purpose `mailbox_removal`.
- Log lines that operations can key on: `warn` "Mailbox removal cleanup step failed" and `info` "Mailbox removal cleanup step skipped", both with `mailboxItemId`, `step` (`liveSyncStop`, `gmailWatchStop`, `graphSubscriptionDelete`, `tokenRevocation`) and `classification`.
- `getGmailAccountUse`, `getCredentialType`, `readMailboxRemovalCleanupInput` and `cleanUpRemovedMailbox` are exported from their modules only, not from `backend/packages/data/src/index.ts`.
- `getGmailAccountUse` calls the router function `route_gmail_address` from inside a tenant's transaction. It needs the `EXECUTE` grant that migration 0071 gives `semprec_data`. It finds only `gmail_api` rows whose `gmail_watch_email_address` equals the removed mailbox's.
- gh-2031 (same merge) refuses the generic delete of a Mailboxes item, so `removeMailbox` is the only way a mailbox leaves, and this cleanup runs for every removal.
- gh-2032 (restore a removed mailbox as needing reauthorization) meets a mailbox whose Google grant may or may not have been revoked, depending on the shared-account rule at removal time, and whose Microsoft grant was never revoked.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. Outlook token revocation is not delivered (acceptance criterion 2, Task steps 2 and 4). ADR point 6.
2. A Google watch stop and revocation are made only for a mailbox that is the only one recording its Google account; `accountUnknown` is treated like shared. ADR point 7. This narrows acceptance criterion 2 for Gmail.
3. The provider is taken from the sync mode of the locked sync state row, not from the item's `provider` property, so that a relabelled mailbox's token is not sent to the other provider.
4. Gmail has no watch id. The transaction reads whether a watch is recorded and the cleanup calls `users.stop`.
5. The cleanup is awaited by the request after the commit. It is not a `runAfterCommit` callback and not a queue job, because a job would need the token in its payload. It is not retried.
6. Files beyond `Touches`: `mailLiveSyncRoot.ts` (`stopMailbox`), `mailLiveSyncControl.ts`, `serve.ts` (comment), `externalCredentialsStore.ts` (`getCredentialType`), `mailAccountSyncStateStore.ts` (`getGmailAccountUse`), four test files and the ADR.
7. `createMailboxRemovalRouteHandler` changed from `(pool)` to `(pool, dependencies, options)`.
8. The segment is not contiguous on the chain branch: the two fix commits follow the commit of gh-2031.

## Review

Three reviewers read `ec58fabed^..82f7102f2` read-only: requirements and tests (one low finding), project rules and architecture (clear), security (one medium finding). One fix round and one recheck followed. The recheck read `1c46b6fb0..0f26c4db8` read-only and passed with no unresolved finding.

- Low, requirements: a token exchange that times out or is answered 403, 429 or 5xx was logged as `failed`. Fixed in `e5128b801`.
- Medium, security: removing a Gmail mailbox ended the Google grant and watch that another tenant's mailbox on the same account depends on. Fixed in `7af2650a7`. The recheck judged it resolved.

Follow-ups:

- For the owner: the two open points of the second acceptance criterion above.
- New low finding of the recheck, not fixed: `getGmailAccountUse` does not see another mailbox on the Google account whose sync state row records no watch address. `replaceMailboxCredential` (reauthorization, gh-1742) can create such a `gmail_api` row with only `provider_account_address`, for a mailbox created outside the connect flow. The removed mailbox then answers `sole` and the account's grant is revoked. Closing it needs the router function to match `provider_account_address` as well, which is a migration and a grant change. No source covers it.
- Two races the ADR records and does not close: two removals on one account at the same time both keep the grant, and a mailbox connected between the lookup and the revocation (up to 35 seconds) loses its new grant.
- A `restartAccount` that begins while `stopMailbox` waits is not waited for; the next discovery pass retires the lifecycle it hosts.
- The cleanup is not retried. A process that ends between the commit and the end of the cleanup leaves the watch or subscription to expire and the Google grant in place.
- `readRefreshToken` turns every error that is not a `pg` `DatabaseError` into a missing token (`credentialUnavailable`) and logs nothing about it, as ADR point 1 decides. The rules reviewer noted it without a finding.

## What was delivered

- A thin `inboxCards` invalidation `{ op: "created" | "updated" | "closed", cardId, userId? }` through the whole realtime stack: the `RealtimeMessage` branch and its parser in the NOTIFY publisher (`pgNotifyPublisher.ts`; op enum, UUID `cardId`, optional UUID `userId`), the outbound frame `{ type: "invalidate", scope: "inboxCards", op, cardId }` of protocol v1 (`protocolV1.ts`), the sync server fan-out to the sockets of the publisher's tenant (`syncServer.ts`, `userId` only narrows inside the tenant), and the `realtime-client` frame parser (`serverFrame.ts`).
- A hook pair in `@semprec/data` (`realtimeHook.ts`): `setInboxCardsInvalidationHook` and `notifyInboxCardsInvalidation` (barrel exports; the default is a no-op), wired in `wireRealtimeHooks`, which `semprec-api`, `semprec-agents` and `semprec-transcribe` call.
- `publishInboxCardChange(client, cardId, op)` (`inbox/inboxCardsRealtime.ts`) registers the event with `runAfterCommit`; it is called from every tick card write (create, revise, needsClarification create/update, deleted-source invalid, restored-source reopen, compute-failure create/update), from confirm, reject and the speaker-card settle in `proposalActions.ts`, and from the transcription event-match and speaker-suggestion card producers.

# Handover for sp-a177a00b-7349-48f3-abbf-d0f02e2d78a6

Implementation commit: ea1bb27a6; the queue's decision states the rules in 328a49448.

Every answer of the offline set is returned with the writes still queued applied in queue order
(`QueuedWriteProjection`, `QueuedViews`, `QueuedWriteInsertion`, `ItemFilterMatch`); with nothing queued, or an answer
that is not JSON, it is returned unchanged.

- Item changes set the properties on every object of the item and on its timeline and agenda entries, with the
  completion and effective day the server derives for a task; reminders whose time, alerts or status changed are left
  out until the server sends them again.
- Deletes remove the item everywhere and its read answers `404`. Creates answer their own read, join the first page of
  their database's queries whose filter matches (the nodes `ItemFilterMatch` knows; relative dates against the
  device's day from `localToday()`), sorted by the first sort key, and the timeline and agenda holding their day.
- Relations, stored occurrences and invitation answers show too. Repetitions of an item created offline are not
  expanded (first day only); the overview, sleep and Inbox show the server's counts.
- Decision records: `issues/decisions/sp-a177a00b-7349-48f3-abbf-d0f02e2d78a6.md` (Touches: `LocalDay.kt`,
  `QueuedWriteInsertion.kt`, `QueueReplay.kt`; the ADR wording).

Tests: `QueuedWriteProjectionTest`, `ItemFilterMatchTest` (every node, non-matching and unknown nodes) and
`QueuedViewsRepositoryTest` (through `HttpCalendarRepository`, `HttpTaskRepository`, `HttpCatalogRepository` and
`HttpItemRepository`).

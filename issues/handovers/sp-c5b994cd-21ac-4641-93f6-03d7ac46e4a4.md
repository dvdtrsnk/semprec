## Delivered

- `.github/scripts/changed-areas.mjs` decides the areas `backend`, `web`, `android` and `apple` of a change; `.github/scripts/changed-areas.test.mjs` covers its rules, the merge-base comparison, renames, the every-area fallbacks and its three output modes.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `changes` job, the backend and web area conditions on the required jobs and on the steps of `ci` (every one of them runs on a push), the `ci` guard against a failed or malformed detection, the `integration` fan-in that accepts skipped shards only for a pull request without a backend change, the Android and Apple conditions of the mobile jobs, `architecture` in `mobile-apple`, and `gradle/actions/setup-gradle@v6` with the `basic` cache provider in both mobile jobs.
- `mobile/scripts/verify`: the `android` step runs `detektAll -PdetektAutoFix=false`.
- The four Relay workflows run the backend, web and mobile verify only for their area against a freshly fetched `develop`; `.github/scripts/area-gates.test.mjs` runs the real step scripts of `ci.yml` and of those workflows.
- `docs/architecture/delivery-and-operations.md`, `docs/operations/required-checks.md`, the ADR `2026-11-24-mobile-verification-follows-the-android-and-apple-areas` (superseding `2026-10-25-conditional-mobile-verification-in-ci-and-relay`), `.bb/AGENTS.md`, the `implement-issue`, `mobile-verify` and `android-compose` skills, `mobile/docs/verify.md`, `mobile/review-rules/context.md`, and the review-rule items in `review-rules/tasks/ci-workflows.md` and `review-rules/tasks/repo-scripts.md`.

## For dependants

- A pipeline added later needs an area in `AREAS` and rules in `RULES` of `changed-areas.mjs`, its jobs need the same push-or-area condition, and `area-gates.test.mjs` needs the job in its condition check.
- Pushes to `develop` and `main` still run every required job; only pull requests and Relay's verify are scoped.

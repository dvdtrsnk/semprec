## What was delivered

`POST /api/blobs` decides the stored mime from the first bytes (new audio detector for M4A brands `M4A `, `mp42`, `isom` and for MP3, then the gh-1410 detector) and accepts only the Inbox attachment allowlist plus HEIC. Any other body, including an empty one, answers 415 `unsupported_media_type` after the just-written bytes are removed, and no blob row exists. `POST /api/inbox/items` takes `attachments: [{blobId, source}]` (strict entries, at most 10, a repeated blobId compared case-insensitively is refused, `text` may be empty when there is an attachment). It locks and reads the blobs in the caller's tenant: an unknown or foreign id is 400 `validation_failed` with field `attachments`, a blob whose type is outside the allowlist is 415 `unsupported_attachment_type` with `details {blobId, mimeType}`. The rows are inserted with `insertInboxAttachments` in the capture transaction after the item, and only by the call that created the item. The response lists `attachments: [{attachmentId, blobId, mimeType, byteSize, source}]` in array order (an empty list when there are none, replays included).

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The capture route lives in `packages/data/src/inbox/inboxRouteHandlers.ts`, not under `services/alldots-api/src/inbox`; new logic is in `inbox/captureAttachments.ts`.
2. The upload allowlist is the Inbox allowlist plus `image/heic` ("images already allowed"), so a HEIC blob uploads and the capture then refuses it with `unsupported_attachment_type`. JPEG, PNG and any image whose bytes are not recognised are no longer stored as `application/octet-stream`; unrecognised bytes answer 415.
3. The gh-1410 detector already recognised M4A (brands M4A/M4B) and MP3; `audioTypeDetection.ts` adds `mp42` and `isom` and is tried first. An `mp42`/`isom` file is stored as `audio/mp4` even with a video track.
4. A replay (and a concurrent same-key loser) answers the existing item with its stored attachments and does not validate the replay's attachments (same rule as `typeId`).
5. Because `ingestUploadedBlob` refuses unrecognised bytes, fixtures of existing tests moved to recognised signatures, and two `filesAndBlobsHandler` assertions changed from "stored as octet-stream" to 415, as the acceptance criteria require.
6. Added by the fix round, outside the original Task: the ADR above, FOR KEY SHARE instead of FOR SHARE, and the effective (verified) type.

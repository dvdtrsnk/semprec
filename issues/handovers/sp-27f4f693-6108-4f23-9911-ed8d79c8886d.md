## What was delivered

- Shared domain: `PropertyWrites.updateOption(property, key, label?, color?)` and
  `PropertyWrites.removeOption(property, key)`, with the use cases `UpdatePropertyOption` and
  `RemovePropertyOption`, which read the schema again after a successful write (as `AddPropertyOption` does).
- Shared data: `HttpCollectionWrites` sends `PATCH /api/properties/:id/options/:key` with only the given
  `label`/`color`, and `DELETE /api/properties/:id/options/:key` (204 is a success). The key is one
  encoded path segment. Refusals are typed failures (`REJECTED`, `NOT_FOUND`, `INVALID_DATA` for an unreadable answer).
- Shared presentation: `ItemAction.Edit.Choice`, a sealed group of `AddChoice`, `RenameChoice`, `RecolorChoice` and
  `RemoveChoice`, handled by the internal `ItemChoiceFlow`. Each write reads the item again afterwards. A refusal
  is `ItemState.choiceFailure`. A blank name changes nothing. A property that is not a select or multi-select, or that
  the person cannot edit, is refused without a request. `ItemState.swatches` offers the existing palette
  (`CALENDAR_SWATCHES`).
- Apple: the Tags section of `ProjectSettingsSheet` closes the sheet and asks the router for
  `showProjectTags(projectId:)`; the app pushes `AppDestination.projectTags` (`ProjectTagsScreen`, every tag of the
  projects) and, for a tag, `showProjectTag(projectId:tagKey:)` pushes `AppDestination.projectTag`
  (`ProjectTagScreen`), both on the stack of the collection and each over its own model of the project. The tag screen
  renames the tag, picks its colour from `state.swatches` and deletes it after a confirmation that it is removed from
  every project; it closes once the tag it showed is gone (`ProjectTagPage.wasRemoved`). New copy keys `project.tags.*` and
  `project.tag.*` in en and cs, identifiers `project.settings.tags.manage`, `project.settings.tag.*`, listed in the
  architecture policy.
- Android is unchanged (out of scope); it has no item screen.

## Contracts dependents build on

- `ItemAction.Edit.Choice` (Swift `.edit(.renameChoice/.recolorChoice/.removeChoice)`) writes a choice of a
  select or multi-select for every item of its database; failures land in `choiceFailure`, never `writeFailure`.
- `CollectionsRouting.showProjectTags(projectId:)` and `showProjectTag(projectId:tagKey:)` are the routes to the tag
  screens; `AppCollectionsRouter` pushes them on its tab and closes a presenting item sheet first.
- `ItemState.swatches` / CoreKit `ItemState.swatches: [CalendarSwatch]` is the palette a choice can take.
- The projects tag bar shows a removed tag's absence after its collection refreshes (`CollectionAction.Refresh`, which
  the Apple collection screen sends when it appears again); a chip that was selected before the removal stays in the
  filter selection until cleared.

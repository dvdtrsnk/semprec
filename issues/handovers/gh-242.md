## Summary

Implements server-side enforcement in `packages/realtime` for issue #242:
- RFC 6455 heartbeat ping/pong every 30s, with session revalidation riding the same tick (fallback for a missed `session_revoked` NOTIFY); a socket that misses 2 consecutive pongs is closed within the ~90s detection window.
- LISTEN-outage handling: a lost or re-established dedicated LISTEN connection, and a controlled process shutdown, close every socket with 1012 — nothing is replayed since NOTIFY carries no durable state, so clients are forced through their own resume path.
- Inbound frame cap at 2 MB (via `ws`'s own `maxPayload`) and a per-connection cap of 32 open `doc:open` subscriptions, the latter reserved synchronously before any `await` to close a TOCTOU race under a concurrent burst of opens.
- Per-socket coalescing of pending item-scope invalidations by (`databaseId`, `itemId`), keeping only the frame with the newest `updatedAt`.
- Backpressure: a socket whose `bufferedAmount` exceeds 4 MB is closed with 1013 instead of growing an unbounded queue or silently dropping frames.
- Server fault-injection tests covering LISTEN loss, controlled shutdown, and slow consumer.

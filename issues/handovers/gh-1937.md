## What was delivered

`POST /api/proposals/:id/reopen` returns a `rejected` Inbox proposal card to `proposed` with its proposal, `aiProposal`, overrides and history intact, so it is listed again by `GET /api/inbox/cards`. A card that is already `proposed` is answered unchanged. Any other status is refused. (The route is extended to `confirmed` cards by gh-1943 of the same chain, see its handover.)

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- Route `POST /api/proposals/:id/reopen`: manifest `customRoutes` entry `reopenProposal` (handler export `createReopenProposalRouteHandler`, justification `transactional-semantics`) in `inbox/inboxModuleManifest.ts`; route matrix entry `reopen proposal` in `routeMatrix/proposals.ts`; isolation case `reopen proposal` in `ROUTE_CASES` and its `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping.
- `reopenProposalWithClient(client, input)` in the new `inbox/proposalReopen.ts` (re-exported from `inbox/proposalActions.ts`, with `ReopenProposalInput` and `ReopenProposalResult`). It locks the card (`lockLiveProposal`); `proposed` is answered unchanged whatever `ifVersion` says; a `rejected` card validates its stored envelope with `envelopeAllowsRestore` (now exported from `inbox/proposalReferenceInvalidation.ts`) and becomes `proposed`, or `invalid` when the envelope no longer validates; it appends a `reopened` history entry, publishes an inbox cards `updated` event after commit and enqueues the Journal inbox recompute. A card whose source Inbox item is still deleted keeps status `invalid` and its `sourceDeleted` reason (fix of review); a stale `invalidReason` is cleared when the source is live. Any other status answers 400 `validation_failed` on field `status`; `ifVersion` is checked after the state, a stale value on a rejected card answers 409 `version_conflict` with `details.current` and the card stays rejected.
- `LOCKED_PROPOSAL_STATUSES` is unchanged: the generic guards still treat `rejected` as locked, and the tick still skips a rejected card; the reopen writes the status itself through `updateItemWithClient` with an explicit system key list.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-a-rejected-proposal-reopens-without-recomputation.md` narrows the rule that a locked card never changes, as the gh-1379 ADR left it to this issue.

## Tests

The reopen tests cover rejected to proposed with the same proposal and overrides and listing again, a double reopen, the tick still skipping a rejected card, the realtime event and the Journal job, the `invalid` outcome for an envelope that no longer validates or is missing, the `sourceDeleted` cases, a proposed card unchanged whatever `ifVersion` says, 400 for the other statuses, a stale and a current `ifVersion`. The isolation case runs over HTTP with the runtime-role pool: tenant B's reopen of tenant A's card answers like a random id.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. `reopenProposalWithClient` lives in a sibling module because `proposalActions.ts` is near the 900-line ESLint cap (as for gh-1892 and gh-1912); the Touches name `proposalActions.ts`.
2. `LOCKED_PROPOSAL_STATUSES` in `inboxTypesStore.ts` is not changed (the Touches say "only if").
3. The route entry is in the slice `routeMatrix/proposals.ts`, not `routeMatrix.ts`.
4. `envelopeAllowsRestore` was exported for reuse.
5. An ADR was added because the reopen narrows the locked-card rule.
6. The other statuses (`needsClarification`, `invalid`, `computing`, `computeFailed`) are refused with 400 on `status`; the Task does not list them.
7. A stale `invalidReason` of the rejected card is cleared in the same write, except a `sourceDeleted` reason while the source is still deleted (review fix).
8. The Task's criterion "reopen on a confirmed card answers 400" held at this source's segment only; gh-1943 replaces it, so at the chain tip a confirmed card is reopened (reversal of its recorded writes) and the 1937 test of the refusal was removed in that segment.

## What was delivered

- `readBirthdayTimelineItems` in `calendar/timelineBirthdaySource.ts`: one batched query over People with a birthday, one entry per person and per year the range touches: `{ kind: "birthday", id: "birthday:<personId>:<year>", personId, personName, title, date, allDay: true, calendarId, readOnly: true }`. `calendarId` is the live Birthdays system calendar or null. A 29 February birthday falls on 28 February in a common year; trashed people are excluded; a stored birthday that cannot be read is left out with one log line carrying the id. Title from `calendar.birthday.title` (cs "Narozeniny - {name}", en "Birthday - {name}") using the first word of the name, in the tenant user's locale (cs by default).
- Helpers `readCalendarCatalog` (`calendar/calendarCatalog.ts`) and `readSystemCalendarId` (`calendar/systemCalendar.ts`).
- The timeline entry type is now the union `CalendarTimelineEntry` (stored `CalendarTimelineItem` or a derived entry); the merge sorts through a sort key (birthdays before all-day Events of their day); the compact shape returns a derived entry unchanged. The source is registered in `CALENDAR_TIMELINE_SOURCES`.
- The item routes refuse a birthday id (400) for PATCH, DELETE and relation writes.

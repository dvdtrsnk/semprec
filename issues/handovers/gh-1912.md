## What was delivered

`POST /api/proposals/:id/reset-overrides` returns a Processing proposals card to the assistant's own proposal. It restores the stored assistant envelope (`aiProposal`) as `proposal`, empties `userOverrides` and `userEditedKeys`, sets the status to `proposed` (clearing the `invalidReason` of a fixable invalid card) and appends one history entry of kind `reset` whose keys are the previously edited keys, in one `updateItemWithClient` call after the restored envelope is revalidated with `assertValidEnvelopeForCard`.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- Data function: `resetProposalOverridesWithClient(client, input)` and type `ResetProposalOverridesInput` in `inbox/proposalReset.ts`, re-exported from `inbox/proposalActions.ts` by one line.
- Behaviour: locks the card (`lockLiveProposal`), refuses locked, computing, computeFailed, confirmed, rejected and final-invalid (`sourceDeleted`) cards with a `ValidationError`; checks `ifVersion` (a stale value is a `ConflictError` with `details.current`, over HTTP 409 `version_conflict` with `details.currentItem`), also when nothing is edited; returns the locked card unchanged, with no write and no history entry, when `userEditedKeys` and `userOverrides` are both empty; a `needsClarification` card that holds manual values and an envelope becomes `proposed`; a card with manual values and no assistant envelope is refused with a `ValidationError` on field `proposal`; an envelope of an unknown entity kind gives the 409 `ConflictError` of confirm.
- Route: `POST /api/proposals/:id/reset-overrides`, body `{ ifVersion? }` (an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp, parsed by `parseIfVersion` like confirm and reject), handler export `createResetProposalOverridesRouteHandler` in `inbox/inboxRouteHandlers.ts`, one `withTransaction`. Manifest entry `resetProposalOverrides` in `inbox/inboxModuleManifest.ts`, justification `transactional-semantics`. Route matrix entry `reset proposal overrides` in `services/semprec-api/src/routeMatrix/proposals.ts`, with the `ROUTE_CASES` isolation case and owner probe in `tenantIsolationRoutes.test.ts` and the `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping in `chokePointWriteBoundary.unit.test.ts`.
- The Journal recompute is enqueued after a write, as the values route does. No realtime invalidation is published (the values route publishes none). No table, migration or job name is added.

# Handover: leave a malformed Graph or Google Event out of the fetch instead of failing the account's sync

Commit on `fix/ia-provider`: `2eac956c9`.

**Changes.**
- `graphEventMapping.ts`: `GraphEventUnreadableError` (an `invalidResponse` `CalendarSyncProviderError`) for every mapping failure, `parseGraphEvent`.
- `graphCalendarAdapter.ts`: the delta round skips and counts an item outside the schema or an occurrence without its series; singles that cannot be mapped are skipped; `fetchedSeries` leaves a series with an unreadable master or instance out whole; one `{ unreadable }` warning per round. `getEvent` and writes still throw.
- `googleCalendarAdapter.ts`: `eventsPageSchema.items` is `unknown[]`; `readableEvents` parses item by item (events listing, cancelled instances, the override's instance lookup) and logs `unreadableEvent`; the master read of an incrementally touched series is lenient (`readListedMaster`), `readEvent` stays strict.
- Docs: `domain-calendar-tasks-and-media.md` (adapters).

**Behaviour to know.** As with CalDAV, a full fetch treats the stored copy of a left-out Event as no longer listed (it is deleted locally); an incremental round loses that one change until the next full round. The calendar listings stay strict, since a calendar left out reads as removed. The Graph create path's parse is untouched.

**Tests.** Graph: a round with an unknown `type`, an unreadable start, an orphan occurrence and an id-less item answers every other Event and logs `{ unreadable: 4 }`; a series with an unreadable instance is neither upserted nor deleted; a page whose `value` is no list still fails. Google: a first page with three malformed items syncs the other events and logs three `unreadableEvent` skips without content.

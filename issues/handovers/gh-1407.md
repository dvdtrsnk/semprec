## Delivered behavior

- `ModuleDatabaseDescriptor` and its zod schema gained optional `icon` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,63}$`), `color` (`#RRGGBB`), `overviewOrder` / `switcherOrder` (positive integer or `null`) and `clientVisible`. `ModuleRegistry.getDatabases()` passes each through only when declared, so manifests without them are unchanged.
- The table from the issue is declared in `seed/systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts`, `library/libraryModuleManifest.ts` and `mail/mailModuleManifest.ts`. Areas, Emails, Transcripts, Books and Movies carry `clientVisible: true`; Mailboxes and Folders declare nothing. Position 9 of both lists is free for Recipes.
- `manifest/collectionMetadata.ts` exports `createCollectionMetadataResolver(moduleRegistry)` (one `getDatabases()` read; `resolve({ key, system })` returns the five fields) and `compareCollections`. Undeclared system databases resolve to nulls and `clientVisible: false`; user-created databases resolve to nulls and `clientVisible: true`. Both are exported from the `@semprec/data` barrel and the public-surface list.
- `DatabaseEnvelope` / `toDatabaseEnvelope(database, name, metadata)` and `SchemaDatabaseProjection` carry the five fields additively. `toDatabaseEnvelope` now takes a third `metadata` argument.
- `GET /api/databases` reads every `database.list` page (limit 200), sorts with `compareCollections` (switcher order, then overview-only order, then locale-aware case-insensitive resolved name, ties by id) and pages itself. The cursor is base64url JSON of the last row's `[switcherOrder, overviewOrder, name, id]`, so a cursor whose row was archived still resumes correctly. `limit` is 1 to 200 (default 50); a bad `limit` or `cursor` is a 400 naming that field. The response shape `{ databases, nextCursor }` is unchanged. Single-database routes also return the five fields.

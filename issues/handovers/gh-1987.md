## What was delivered

The proposal computation of an Inbox item now receives the facts and the workspace context a good proposal needs, under a size budget.

- The request carries capture facts (`captureDate`, `captureTime`, `captureChannel` when the item has one, the tenant timezone and `today` in that timezone), the item's text or, when it has none of its own, its `transcribedText`, the workspace context, and for a recompute the card's set `aiProposal`, `history` and `userEditedKeys`. The system message gained the relative-date resolution instruction and the rule that Markdown belongs only in text properties.
- The workspace context is read as the Alldots project (the tick's `projectItemId`) through the per-database grants of gh-1810: a database that project may not read yields no group at all. Fields per kind: projects `id, name, status, areaId`, areas `id, name`, people `id, name, relationship`, companies `id, name, registrationNumber`, events `id, name, type, date`; text cut at 100 characters, at most 20 entries per kind, trashed and archived items and done projects left out, events limited to a date window around the capture date. The read runs in one read transaction that is closed before the gateway call and writes nothing.
- A 900 KiB budget on the context: entries are dropped kind by kind (events, companies, people, areas, projects), the last entry of a kind first; the item's text is never cut.
- Today is computed with `getCalendarClock`, which also validates the tenant timezone.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. `proposalWorkspaceContext.ts`, its barrel export and `proposalContext.ts` already existed from the gh-1488/gh-1492 foundation; they were extended, not added.
2. `inboxTickSnapshot.ts` (not in Touches) gained `previousProposal`, because the computation otherwise has no access to the card; a unit test file for it was added.
3. A new ADR records the disclosure and budget decision.
4. Health records are not one of the five context kinds, so "with a grant it does" is shown on a built-in database marked sensitive (Companies, Areas); a Health grant is shown not to add Health records.
5. "Oldest first within a kind" is implemented as the last entry first, the one the reader ranks lowest; for events that is the farthest date.
6. The existing test "captures both relation sides..." was updated: the area id now appears twice in the request (as the area entry and as the project's `areaId`).
7. The previous card state is not covered by the budget (documented in the ADR); the budget measures the context object, with about 124 KiB of headroom under the gateway's 1 MiB body limit.
8. The user message lost its separate `Date:` and `Time:` lines; they are in the capture facts JSON line.
9. The commit trailers name Claude Sonnet 5.5 (the session attribution).

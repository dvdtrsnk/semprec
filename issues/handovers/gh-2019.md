## What was delivered

A final voice transcript that equals exactly `stop` or `zruš` (NFC, trimmed, lower-cased, trailing punctuation removed) is never stored. The voice session stops speech and, in one tenant transaction, lists the running assistant messages of its thread and sends a `semp_turn_cancel` notification for each. `semprec-agents` listens and aborts the job's own controller; the turn handler stores `error` / `cancelled`, closes the run cancelled and pairs dangling tool calls as for any abort. The voice session treats a cancelled reply for a message it stopped as a quiet end of the turn, including a stop that lands between the claim of a reply and the start of the voice watch (review fix). A sentence that only contains a stop word is stored as a normal message; a stop with no running turn does nothing. The approval request of a cancelled turn stays pending.

## Tests

## Deviations and spec seams

1. `inFlightAgentRuns.test.ts` named in Touches is `inFlightAgentRuns.unit.test.ts`.
2. The turn registration is a fifth parameter of `withInFlightRegistration`, so the job's own controller is registered without re-indenting the handler.
3. `index.ts` sat at the line cap of its file; the barrel entry is one `export *` line and the two same-module statements for `agentTools/moduleAgentTools.js` were folded into one (no export changed). On the merged tree the lint step passed.
4. Additions not named in the Task and needed by the feature: `listRunningSempTurnIds`, `startSempTurnCancelListener`, `errorCode` on `getSempVoiceDelivery`, `semp/logger.ts`, `sempTurnCancelListener.ts`, the fixture controls.
5. The stop applies to replies with status running only; a reply still queued has no turn to abort (stated in the ADR).
6. The approval criterion is proven at agents level with a real notification, not through the live voice session (the live gateway fixture cannot hold the second model call deterministically).
7. The `stop` control frame named in the older voice ADR is not part of this issue and was not built.
8. The write-safety baseline got one hand-added entry; `--update-baseline` was not committed because it also pruned unrelated entries.

**What changed (web only; no backend change, as the issue specifies)**
- `web/src/api/adminOperations.ts`: `deleteAccount(userId)` posts to `/admin/accounts/:id/delete` with encoded id and same-origin credentials. It parses the 202 `{ account }` body and uses the file's existing error mapping.
- `web/src/views/admin/AccountsPanel.tsx`: a Delete action on `active` and `suspended` rows of other accounts. Clicking it replaces that row's actions with an inline confirmation naming the email. Only one row can hold the confirmation at a time. Cancel sends no request. Confirming disables the row's buttons while pending, and success reloads the list. A refusal closes the confirmation, keeps the row's status and shows `admin.accounts.lastAdmin` or `admin.accounts.actionFailed`.

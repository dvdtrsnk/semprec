## What was delivered

- `POST /api/mail/threads/:threadId/restore` (`createMailThreadRestoreRouteHandler`), response `{ threadId, moved }`. It selects the counted, non-draft copies in a folder with `specialPurpose` archive or trash (Junk, Inbox, Sent and drafts are untouched). An incoming copy goes to the folder with `specialPurpose` inbox of its own mailbox, the user's own copy to the folder with `specialPurpose` sent (the incoming classification of `mailThreadActions`).
- 409 `mail_thread_not_archived_or_trashed` when nothing is selected (also when the only Archive or Trash message is a draft), 409 `mail_inbox_folder_missing` and 409 `mail_sent_folder_missing` when a mailbox that has a copy to restore lacks the folder; all decided before the first write, nothing changes. A mailbox that holds only own messages needs no Inbox folder.
- The transactional move is the one of gh-1917 (`moveMailThread`, `planMailThreadMoves`, `applyMailThreadMoves`): guard first, desired move with purpose `inbox` or `sent` recorded before the edge is replaced, edges replaced as the Emails module without UID metadata, one account sync per mailbox, one `mailThread` invalidation after the commit. A thread archived through the archive route before the sync ran is restored from the source the archive recorded.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- Route `mailThreadRestore` (manifest `customRoutes`, handler export `createMailThreadRestoreRouteHandler` from `mail/mailThreadRoutes.ts`, re-exported by `mailModuleManifest.ts`, justification `transactional-semantics`); route matrix entry `mail thread restore` in `routeMatrix/mail.ts`.
- `selectMailThreadRestoreMoves` in `mail/mailThreadActions.ts`; the three selectors now return the purpose with each copy.
- The 409 `mail_move_source_unverified` of gh-1917 also applies when a copy cannot be addressed at its provider.
- Tables: none new; writes the same move rows, folder edges and sync jobs as gh-1917.

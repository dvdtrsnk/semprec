## What was delivered

An open Processing proposals card that refers to a project that is archived, or to an item that is deleted, becomes `invalid` in the same transaction as the write, and returns to `proposed` when the item comes back or another target is chosen. Every writer of a card's proposal now stores `referencedItemIds`, the ids the envelope refers to, so a hook can find the cards with one jsonb containment query.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- `inbox/proposalReferences.ts`: `envelopeReferencesOf(envelope)` (item id plus the editor field the `invalidReason` names), `referencedItemIdsOf(envelope)` (database-envelope relation targets, `pageContent` and relation target as field target, active multi-step `itemId` and relation ids; UUIDs only, lower case; a synthetic occurrence id and a disabled or skipped step contribute none), and `runWhileConfirmingCard(client, cardId, run)` / `isCardBeingConfirmed(client, cardId)` (a per-connection mark, removed in `finally` and nesting-safe, that `confirmProposalWithClient` sets around everything after `lockLiveProposal`/`assertProposalVersion`).
- `inbox/proposalReferenceInvalidation.ts`: `invalidateProposalsForItem(client, config, itemId, reasonCode)` finds `proposed` cards by containment on `referencedItemIds` (index `items_props_gin`), locks them `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, skips a card that the same connection is confirming, writes status `invalid`, `invalidReason { code, field, itemId, label }` (code `targetArchived` or `targetDeleted`, label trimmed to 80 code points), one history entry of kind `invalidated`, publishes the card change and enqueues the Journal recompute. `restoreProposalsForItem` returns the `invalid` cards of that item and reason to `proposed` (history kind `reopened`, `invalidReason` null) when `storedEnvelopeStillValid` passes; a `ForbiddenError` from the revalidation counts as not valid and leaves the card invalid. Hooks `proposalReferenceItemUpdateHook` (a Projects update to `archived` invalidates; any other status in the patch restores), `proposalReferenceItemDeleteHook` and `proposalReferenceItemRestoreHook`, registered at the end of the hooks in `domainWriteHooks.ts`, after the gh-1905 entries and before the gh-1923 hook, for items of any database.
- Every writer of a card's proposal lists `referencedItemIds` in `allowedSystemKeys` and stores `referencedItemIdsOf(envelope)`: the tick's four writes (needsClarification create and revise store `[]`), `reviseProposalWithClient`, `reviseProposalFromThreadWithClient`, `updateProposalValuesWithClient`, `resetProposalOverridesWithClient`, the Event and speaker transcript card creators, and (added in the refresh) the proposal split siblings, the speaker suggestion clarification refresh and the transcript card recompute apply.
- `storedEnvelopeStillValid` of `inbox/inboxTickAction.ts` is now exported and in the public surface list of `publicSurface.test.ts`.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-an-item-hook-invalidates-the-open-cards-of-an-item-without-waiting-for-a-card-a-confirm-holds.md` records the lock order, `SKIP LOCKED`, the own-transaction mark and the no-backfill consequence. No route, migration, table or job name is added.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The invalidation, restore and the three hooks live in the sibling module `inbox/proposalReferenceInvalidation.ts` (the Task names only `proposalReferences.ts`), so `inboxTickAction.ts`, which imports `referencedItemIdsOf`, does not import itself in a cycle. The edit route, thread revision and reset route live in `proposalValueUpdates.ts`, `proposalThreadRevision.ts` and `proposalReset.ts`.
2. `envelopeReferencesOf` is an extra export that returns the editor field the `invalidReason` needs.
3. Two existing tests of `transcriptProposalActions.test.ts` trashed a project that a card referred to and expected the card to stay `proposed`; with the hook the card becomes invalid, so they now remove `referencedItemIds` first (helper `forgetReferencedItems`) to keep testing confirm's own validation of a stale target.
4. ESLint `max-lines`: `inboxTickAction.ts` sat at exactly 900 counted lines. After the chain was refreshed onto the newer tip the merged `proposalActions.ts` (902) and `inboxTickAction.ts` (901) were over the cap, so `resolveItemOriginSource`, `inboxCaptureInstant` and `requireRelationPropertyId` moved unchanged into `proposalItemOrigin.ts` and `invalidReasonLabel` into `invalidReason.ts`. Counted lines are now 857 and 894.
5. The refresh found that the tip's value-link resolver needs a tenant scope for a Projects status write, so `proposalReferences.test.ts` was moved into `runInTenant` (no assertion weakened, checked by a whitespace-insensitive diff), and that three writers the tip added (proposal split siblings, speaker suggestion clarification refresh, transcript card recompute apply) did not store `referencedItemIds`; they now do, with assertions.
6. Cards stored before this change have no `referencedItemIds` and are not found by the hooks until a writer next stores their proposal; the Task adds no backfill (recorded in the ADR). An open proposal that the hook skipped because another transaction held its lock keeps its status (the accepted trade-off of the ADR).

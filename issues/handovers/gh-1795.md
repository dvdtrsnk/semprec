## What was delivered

- People seed: `birthday` (type `date`, no time, user-owned) and `birthdayYearKnown` (`checkbox`, user-owned) in `seed/seedTenDatabases.ts`, named in cs and en as `property.people.birthday.name` and `property.people.birthdayYearKnown.name`. A birthday with an unknown year stores a placeholder year (2000) with `birthdayYearKnown` false.
- `runPeopleBirthdayCutoverMigration(pool)` in `calendar/peopleBirthdayCutoverMigration.ts`: takes `LOCK TABLE properties IN EXCLUSIVE MODE`, inserts only the absent keys, does nothing for a tenant without People; registered in `runTenantCutoverMigrations` after the library cover override cutover of gh-1793 (its blocker) and before the sensitive databases cutover and the area-note cutover that stays last.
- `personBirthdayItemCreateHook` and `personBirthdayItemUpdateHook` in `calendar/personBirthdayHook.ts`, registered in `domainWriteHooks.ts`: for the People database `birthday` must be null/absent or a real `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar date with a year of 0001 or later, else `ValidationError` (400 `validation_failed`, field `birthday`). A person whose stored birthday is unreadable can still be renamed.

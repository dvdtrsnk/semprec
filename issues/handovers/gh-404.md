## Summary

`GET /healthz` gated on the `agents` process's `process_heartbeats` row, but the `api` process (which serves `/healthz` from `serve.ts`) only ever UPSERTs a heartbeat row under its own name, `api`. In any deployment where the `agents` process isn't running alongside `api`, the health probe would report unhealthy even though the `api` process itself is perfectly healthy.

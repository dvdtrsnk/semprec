# Handover: open password-reset links of alldots.app in the Apple app

Implementation commits on `bb/alldots-app-universal-links`:
- `4d68f3ba1`: shared layer.
- `f855a58ba`: Apple app.
- `becbd77d4`: ADR and docs.

**Shared (KMP).**
- Domain types:
  - `PasswordResetRefusal`: TOKEN_INVALID, TOKEN_EXPIRED, TOKEN_CONSUMED, WEAK_PASSWORD.
  - `PasswordResetOutcome`: `Done` or `Refused`.
- `AuthRepository.resetPassword(serverUrl, token, newPassword)`.
- Use case `ResetPassword`:
  - `accepts(new, repeated)`: both entries equal and not empty.
  - Passwords that do not pass are REJECTED with no request.
- `HttpAuthRepository.resetPassword` sends `POST /api/auth/password-reset/consume` `{ token, newPassword }`, with no bearer and no session change.
- A 400 body `{ error, code, details }` maps as follows:
  - Each of the three `password_reset_token_*` codes → its own refusal.
  - `validation_failed` with `details.field == "newPassword"` → WEAK_PASSWORD.
  - Anything else (other codes, unreadable body, 429, 5xx, no answer) stays its `FailureKind`.
- Presentation:
  - `PasswordResetViewModel` (effect type `Nothing`) with `PasswordResetState(phase EDITING/SENDING/DONE/FAILED, canSubmit, refusal: PasswordResetRefusalReason?, failure: FailureKind?)`.
  - Actions: `Edited(new, repeated)` and `Submit(new, repeated)`.
  - A refusal of the link is final.
  - A weak password or another failure keeps the form usable.
  - Submits are ignored while sending and after DONE.
- SDK: `HomeSDK.passwordReset.viewModel(serverUrl, token)` (`PasswordResetSDK`). It is a sub-SDK because one more `HomeSDK` function trips detekt's TooManyFunctions.

**Apple.**
- CoreKit:
  - `PasswordResetScreenModel` values.
  - `SharedPasswordResetScreenModel` bridge, which closes its lease in `deinit`.
  - `ScreenModelFactory.makePasswordReset(serverUrl:token:)`.
- AuthFeature:
  - `PasswordResetScreen` and its `PasswordResetViewModel`.
  - Two `SecureField`s and Set/Cancel.
  - The message for each refusal, the unavailable server and the unexpected failure.
  - A confirmation with "Sign in" that dismisses the sheet.
  - en/cs copy and the identifiers `passwordReset.*`.
- App:
  - `applinks:alldots.app` in both entitlements files.
  - `PasswordResetLink`, a strict parser: https, host `alldots.app` only, no port/user/path, exactly one `page=password-reset`, fragment exactly `token=<base64url ≤ 256>`.
  - `PasswordResetLinks`, an `AppScene` modifier for `onOpenURL` and `onContinueUserActivity(NSUserActivityTypeBrowsingWeb)`. It opens the sheet over the window whether the person is signed in or not, after closing stack sheets, and ignores a link while the wake guard blocks navigation. It sends the token to `https://alldots.app`.
- The OAuth `alldots://oauth` callback is untouched; the parser ignores it.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-28-the-apple-app-opens-one-universal-link-for-password-resets.md`. `mobile/docs/architecture.md` and the `mobile-navigation` skill were updated.

**Android.** Out of scope; nothing changed.

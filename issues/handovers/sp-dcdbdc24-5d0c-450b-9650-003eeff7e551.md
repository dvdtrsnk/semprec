# Handover: show internet accounts with service switches on iOS

Implementation commits `04e304ad2` (main) and `a8f800a3d` (a test fix) on `feat/internet-accounts`.

**What changed.**
- `:shared:domain`: `InternetAccounts.kt` adds `InternetService` (MAIL, CALENDARS), `CalendarSyncStatus` (OFF, PENDING, NEEDS_CONSENT, OK, ERROR, NEVER, NEEDS_REAUTHORIZATION), `InternetAccount` (the `MailAccount` plus `mailEnabled`, `calendarsEnabled` and `calendarStatus`) and the port `InternetAccountRepository` (`accounts()`, `account(id)`, `setServiceEnabled(id, service, enabled)`, `syncCalendars(id)`). `MailPasswordAccount` gains `caldavUrl` (default `null`).
- `:shared:data`: `HttpMailboxRepository` also implements `InternetAccountRepository`. It reads the switches and `calendarStatus` from `GET /api/mail/mailboxes` and `/:id` rows. `calendarStatus` is read as text. A row without it, or with a value this app does not know, is OFF when Calendars is off and PENDING otherwise. A switch is an item `PATCH` of `mailEnabled` or `calendarsEnabled` with `ifVersion` taken from a fresh `GET /api/items/:id`, the same as rename. A 409 `mailbox_sync_busy` is `FailureKind.REJECTED`. Sync now is `POST /api/accounts/:id/calendars/sync`. The connect body carries `caldavUrl` only when it is filled in.
- `:shared:presentation`: `InternetAccountsViewModel` and `InternetAccountViewModel` replace `MailAccountsViewModel` and `MailAccountViewModel`. They are built by the new `InternetAccountViewModelFactory`, and `MailSDK` exposes them as `internetAccountsViewModel()` and `internetAccountViewModel(accountId)`. Both reload on `SyncTopic.MAIL_ACCOUNTS`.
  - Each service's status is an `InternetServiceStatus` (OFF, PENDING, SYNCING, OK, ERROR, NEEDS_REAUTHORIZATION, NEEDS_CONSENT). The switch decides OFF. Calendar NEVER shows as SYNCING, and a server OFF while the switch is on shows as PENDING.
  - Switching a service on writes it at once. Switching one off first sets `confirmingOff`; `ConfirmServiceOff` writes it and `CancelServiceOff` drops it. The new status is shown optimistically and the account is read again after the write. A refused write puts the switch back and sets `actionFailed`. Calendars switched on emits `InternetAccountEffect.CalendarsTurnedOn`.
  - `SyncNow` runs only while Calendars is on, one request at a time, and is tracked in `syncRequested`.
  - Removal actions are nested as `InternetAccountAction.Removal.{Request,Cancel,Confirm}`. This keeps detekt's complexity and function-count limits.
  - `AccountPage.MAIL_ACCOUNTS` is now `INTERNET_ACCOUNTS`.
- Apple CoreKit: the models and bridges were renamed to `InternetAccounts*` and `InternetAccount*`, with `SharedInternetAccountValues.swift` added. `makeInternetAccounts()` and `makeInternetAccount(accountId:)` sit under Account in `ScreenModelFactory`, and the two mail account factory methods are gone.
- Apple Account feature:
  - `InternetAccountsScreen` lists each account with a provider badge and a status line for Mail and for Calendars.
  - `AddInternetAccountSheet` is the provider picker: iCloud, Google, Microsoft, Other. Google and Microsoft show an alert saying sign-in comes in the next update; no browser flow.
  - `InternetAccountConnectForm` is the old connect form moved over. iCloud asks for an app-specific password; Other asks for IMAP, SMTP and an optional CalDAV server.
  - `InternetAccountScreen` has a Services section with Mail and Calendars toggles and their statuses, a consent or error notice, and the sync-now button. It keeps rename, the mail rows, the connection limit, the Semp rows and removal. Turning a service off shows a confirmation dialog.
  - `AccountRouting` gains `showInternetAccount(accountId:)`. The profile row is now "Internet accounts", with the reauthorization count as its subtitle.
- App:
  - The profile sheet's stack is now `[ProfileSheetPage]` (`.page(AccountPage)` or `.internetAccount(accountId:)`).
  - `AppDestination.profile(opening:)` opens the sheet with pages already pushed. `AppAccountRouter` pushes the internet accounts and an account onto the sheet's own stack; it no longer pushes onto the Overview stack.
  - Mail's `showAccounts`/`showAccount` and Semp's `showMailbox` now present the profile sheet opened on those pages. `.mailAccounts`/`.mailAccount` and `showMailAccounts` are removed.
  - Calendars-on reaches `AppNotifications.requestPermission()` through the existing `requestPermission` closure.
- Copy: 81 new `internetAccounts.*`, `internetAccount.*` and `profile.*` keys in the Account catalog (en and cs). The 44 `mail.account(s).*` keys and the 2 replaced profile keys were removed. The policy's `copy` and `viewModels` entries were updated. `mobile/docs/architecture.md` (Notifications and Live updates) was updated.

**Deviations and seams.**
- The connect form moved from `Features/Mail` to `Features/Account`, although the issue's Touches list Mail for it. Features cannot import each other, and the form is driven by the accounts list ViewModel, which now lives under Account. The Mail feature keeps folders, threads, compose and its `showAccounts`/`showAccount` router calls, which now open the settings sheet.
- Sync now calls `POST /api/accounts/:id/calendars/sync`, which internet-accounts 05/18 adds. Until it lands, the request fails and the screen shows the generic "could not be done" alert. There is no mail sync-now endpoint, so sync now covers calendars only.
- `caldavUrl` is rejected by the strict connect schema until 06/18 accepts it. The field is optional and sent only when it is filled in, so an empty field connects today.
- The needs-attention count on the profile row is the server summary's `mailAccountsNeedingReauth`. Calendar problems are not counted yet: the summary has no such figure.
- Turning a service off asks for confirmation. The issue does not mention this; it was added because switching off deletes synced data on the server.

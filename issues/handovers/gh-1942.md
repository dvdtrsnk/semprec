## What was delivered

- `transcriptSpeakerSuggestionEdgeChangedHook`, registered in `domainWriteHooks.ts` through `registerRelationEdgeChangedHook`: a created or deleted Transcripts `event` edge and a created or deleted Events `people` edge enqueue `transcriptSpeakerSuggestion` for every live linked transcript. Updates, other relations, trashed transcripts and the dry-run revise enqueue nothing.
- The task `transcriptSpeakerSuggestion` on the short-model-task runner of `semprec-transcribe` (created by gh-1940). It shares `runSpeakerSuggestion` with step 7 of the transcription pipeline, also asks about finished transcripts, takes the pipeline's locks and writes nothing to `item_automation` (attempts unchanged).
- The `suggestSpeakers` checkpoint is now `{ proposedSpeakers, eventId, candidateIds }`; the model is asked again only when the Event or its candidates differ (an old checkpoint counts as different), so an unchanged Event and participants make no second paid call.
- `proposeSpeakerMappings` refreshes an untouched `needsClarification` speaker card (its context, and the proposal when confident) under a `NOWAIT` row lock taken after the transcript lock, and rewrites no other card; a card that is proposed, rejected, in the Trash, touched by the user or held by another transaction is left as it is. At most one card per speaker.
- A gateway budget refusal makes the task fail visibly with the code-only `TranscriptSpeakerSuggestionError` (`budgetExceeded`), as step 7 does.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The shared body is `runSpeakerSuggestion` in a sibling file because `transcriptionTask.ts` sits near the line cap.
2. The data module's index entry became `export *` (the named list would not fit under the cap of `index.ts`).
3. `proposedSpeakers` in the checkpoint is cumulative.
4. The shared step returns without work for a transcript without segments instead of throwing.
5. The refresh takes an existing card `FOR UPDATE NOWAIT` in a savepoint after the transcript lock (the opposite order to confirm would risk a deadlock) and skips a busy card.
6. The Semp write is covered through the generic operation gateway (`semprec.relation.put`), not by running `semprec-agents`.
7. No hook-order entry in `db/tenantCutoverMigrations.ts` is needed (no cutover).

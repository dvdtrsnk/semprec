## What was delivered

`GET /api/calendar/agenda`, a read-only custom route of the calendar module that pages the calendar timeline as one flat list of Events and Tasks from a day, forward or backward.

- Query: `from` (a calendar day, required), `direction` (`forward` or `backward`), `limit` (1 to 200, default 50) and `cursor` (opaque). An invalid `from`, `direction`, `limit` or `cursor` is `400 validation_failed` naming the field; a cursor is capped at 1024 characters, shape-checked and never echoed.
- The handler reads `readCalendarTimeline` in windows of 31 days, scans at most 366 days per request and ends five years from the start day. A day without an entry does not appear. A multi-day item appears once, on the first shown day, and is not repeated across windows or pages.
- The cursor encodes the day and the id of the last item. When that item is gone or moved, the next page continues from the next day. At the 366-day horizon the response carries a continuing cursor; `nextCursor` is null only when the agenda has ended.
- After the derived timeline sources landed on the tip (birthday, holiday and sleep entries, `CalendarTimelineEntry`), the controller decided that the agenda lists stored items only. `agendaRouteHandler.ts` filters `timeline.items` with the type guard `isStoredItem` (kind `event` or `task`) before the day assignment, so a derived entry never reaches a page, the window slicing or a cursor, and `limit` counts stored items only.

## Deviations and spec seams

1. The route entry lives in the slice module `routeMatrix/calendar.ts`, not in `routeMatrix.ts`; its isolation case and handler-file mapping went with it.
2. The agenda lists stored Events and Tasks only; birthday, holiday and sleep entries of the timeline are not part of it (controller decision recorded in the fix round; the Task predates those sources).
3. Two existing agenda tests were adapted to that decision: the full-shape comparison now compares with the stored entries of the timeline, and the first/last calendar date test turns off `showInCalendar` for sleep.
4. The 366-day horizon is relative to the start day of each page, the only reading under which the horizon cursor can continue.

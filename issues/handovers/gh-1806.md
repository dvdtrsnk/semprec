## What was delivered

- `readSleepTimelineItems` in `calendar/timelineSleepSource.ts`: reads the sleep settings item, the plans and the night exceptions of [from minus one day, to] with its own queries, calls `resolveSleepNights` in the tenant time zone and returns one entry per night: `{ kind: "sleep", id: "sleep:<night>", readOnly: false, night, planId, bedTime, wakeTime, alarm, changed, bedAt, wakeAt, prepReminderAt }`. Nothing is returned when there is no settings item, when there are several (logged with the count), or when `enabled` or `showInCalendar` is not true. Plans and exceptions that do not parse are left out with one log line carrying ids; with `prepReminder` on and unusable `prepReminderMinutes` (outside 0..1440 whole numbers) the entry has no `prepReminderAt` and one warn line is logged (item id, database id, property name, never the value). The entry sorts by its night after the day's timed Tasks. Registered in `CALENDAR_TIMELINE_SOURCES`.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-12-a-sleep-night-in-the-timeline-is-a-derived-entry-the-user-edits.md`: a sleep entry has `readOnly: false` (the sleep module edits it); this narrows decision 6 of the timeline ADR for the sleep kind only.
- The item routes refuse a `sleep:<night>` id (400).
- Test helper `calendar/__tests__/hideSleepInCalendar.ts`: because a seeded tenant has sleep on and shown, the fixtures of the core timeline, birthday and holiday tests switch `showInCalendar` off through it.

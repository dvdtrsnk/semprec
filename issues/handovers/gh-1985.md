## What was delivered

- `inbox/attachmentRouting.ts`: pure `routeInboxAttachments(attachments)` returning `"notReady"` (any audio attachment without metadata, whatever else the item holds), `{ recording: attachmentId }` (the first audio attachment by position whose `audioKind` is `recording`, even when the item also has text or other attachments) or `"compute"`. The 120-second boundary is not decided here: `audioKind` is the one of `inboxAttachmentMetadata.ts` (gh-1965).
- `inboxTickAction.ts` (`readTickSnapshot`): after the fingerprint gate and before the recognition and the model computation, the tick reads the item's attachments with `listInboxAttachments`; `notReady` returns without writing (the metadata job announces its result, which runs the tick again); a recording builds the gh-1978 recording card through `buildRecordingCard(client, item, attachmentId)` with no model call and writes it with `writeTickResult`; otherwise the tick continues unchanged. Audio whose metadata says it cannot be read (no `audioKind`) is computed.
- `buildRecordingCard` now takes the attachment id chosen by the router and checks that it is an audio attachment of the item.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- `routeInboxAttachments` and the type `InboxAttachmentRoute` (module `inbox/attachmentRouting.ts`, not exported from the barrel).
- Tick behaviour: 23-minute audio gives the recording card and no model call; 119 seconds continues to the computation, 120 and 121 give the card; 40 seconds goes through the normal computation; no card is written until the metadata exists.

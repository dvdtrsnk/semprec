## What was delivered

The proposal computation (`services/alldots-api/src/proposalComputation.ts`) now replaces a computed create of a company or person that exactly matches a live item by a step that refers to the existing item and writes nothing. The duplicate step runs after the registered-name step and before the result is returned.

- Company: the computed `ico` is normalized to its eight-digit form (spaces removed, left-padded; a stored or computed value that is not 1-8 digits matches nothing) and compared by exact equality among live Companies (the Trash and a Companies database that is archived are excluded). Check-digit validity is not required for the match.
- Person: the computed addresses (split on whitespace, commas and semicolons) are tried in order against `person_email_index`; a hit on a person in the Trash is skipped (through `getItemsByIds`) and the next address is tried.
- A nested record (a company in a person's answer, a person in a company's answer) becomes a link step the parent's relation refers to; the parent create stays. The alias of the replaced create is kept; its database, properties and relations are dropped.
- The proposal's own record (the first create into the target, or the top-level properties of an answer without operations) becomes an update marked primary with no properties and no relations, also for an answer without operations. The company create of a person's new company stays when the person itself is the duplicate.
- An `invalidProposal` result is returned unchanged. Two replacements naming one item (a model link plus a company create with its IČO, or two creates with one IČO) are left as they are and refused downstream by `prepareComputedUpdates` as `Repeated mutation of a proposal target` (`needsClarification`), by design of the Task.
- The lookups are reads inside one short `withTransaction` that is closed before the function returns; the computation writes no row and keeps no transaction open across the model or ARES call. Isolation is by the tenant scope of `withTransaction` (RLS).

Entry points and contracts dependents build on:

- `backend/packages/data/src/inbox/companyIcoLookup.ts` (new): `lookupLiveCompanyIdByIco(client, ico)` returns the id of the live company with that eight-digit number or `null`.
- `ProposalComputationOperation` (`backend/packages/data/src/inbox/proposalOperations.ts`) has the optional `primary` marker. `buildProposalSteps` honours it (the marked operation is the primary step, ahead of the existing rule) without storing the marker in the step; `buildProposalEnvelope` stores exactly one marked operation as a multi-step envelope. The model's answer cannot set it (the zod operation schema strips unknown keys).
- Public exports of `@alldots/data`: `lookupLiveCompanyIdByIco`, `lookupPersonIdByEmail` and `normalizeEmailAddress` (the last two were not public before). They reach `index.ts` through the whole-module export of `inbox/inboxTickAction.ts`, three re-export lines there, and are listed in `publicSurface.test.ts`.
- Confirming a card whose primary step is such an update creates no item, writes nothing to the existing one and answers it as `resultItemId`. The undo (reopen) leaves the item alone and is refused with 409 `version_conflict` after the item changed. `assertValidEnvelopeOperations` and the confirm path are untouched.
- No route, operation, table, migration or job name was added or changed.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. `backend/packages/data/src/index.ts` holds exactly 900 counted lines (the max-lines cap), so the Touches entry `index.ts (export the lookup)` is not literally done: the three names are re-exported from `inbox/inboxTickAction.ts`, which `index.ts` already exports whole. Any other source that adds a line to `index.ts` will collide with the cap.
2. `lookupPersonIdByEmail` and `normalizeEmailAddress` were not exported from `@alldots/data` before; the Task presupposes them, so they are public now.
3. The duplicate step is written inside `proposalComputation.ts` as the Task says (about 80 lines, file about 690 lines).
4. The company lookup compares the stored ico in its eight-digit form, not only the raw string.
5. A link the model gave to a company plus a company create with its IČO is left as is and refused downstream; no code of its own.
6. Person addresses are split on whitespace, commas and semicolons; the index parser splits on newline and comma only (no indexed address contains the extra characters).

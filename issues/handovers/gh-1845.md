## What was delivered

`POST /api/proposals/:id/reject` closes a Processing proposals card without writing a destination.

- `createRejectProposalRouteHandler(pool)` in `inbox/inboxRouteHandlers.ts` validates the optional body `{ message?: string }` and runs `rejectProposalWithClient` in one `withTransaction`. It answers 200 with the card item envelope (no confirm-only `results` member).
- `message` is optional (an absent or `null` body is valid); when present it must be a string that is not blank and has at most 1000 characters. Any other body shape, an empty, blank or over-cap message is a 400 `validation_failed` with field `message`. Validation runs before the lookup and does not depend on the id.
- The action locks the card, answers an already `rejected` card unchanged (no second history entry), refuses a `confirmed` card with 400 `validation_failed` on field `status`, answers an unknown, soft-deleted or foreign card with the same 404, and enqueues the Journal inbox recompute in the same transaction. Proposed, needsClarification, invalid and transcript cards are all closed.
- The history entry the action writes now carries the typed kind `rejected` (the kind already existed in `PROPOSAL_HISTORY_KINDS` from #1430).

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The reject answer is the card item envelope without the confirm-only `results` member; a rejected card never holds confirmation results.
2. Spec seam: the route matrix entry lives in the existing slice `routeMatrix/proposals.ts`, not `routeMatrix.ts`.
3. Spec seam: the history helpers live in `proposalHistory.ts` (re-exported by `inboxTickAction.ts`); `proposalActions.ts` still imports them through `inboxTickAction`.
4. Whitespace-only messages are refused in addition to empty ones.
5. The needsClarification and invalid fixtures set the status through the choke point because the tick of those tests does not produce them.

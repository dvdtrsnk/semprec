## What was delivered

- The first contributor of the related-row registry (ADR `2026-11-13-related-rows-are-contributed-through-a-per-process-registry`): `mail/threadRelatedCount.ts` exports `personMailThreadsRelatedRow`. For a person it counts the distinct live Threads items that hold a live Emails item with a `senderPeople` or `recipientsPeople` edge to the person, leaves out a message that sits only in folders of `specialPurpose` trash (a message in Trash and in another folder counts, a message with no live folder counts, a thread whose own item is in the Trash does not), and returns one Threads row (`via` direct, filter null) with that count.
- It is registered for the People module in `domainWriteHooks.ts` (`registerRelatedRowContributor(PEOPLE_MODULE_ID, personMailThreadsRelatedRow)`, appended at the end of the file; it is a read contributor, not a write hook).
- The People descriptor in `seed/systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts` gains a `relatedOrder` with an omit list that leaves out Emails, so a person's related result has the mail row and no Emails entry.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- `relation.related` of a People item returns the Threads row built by `personMailThreadsRelatedRow`; no new route, table, job, operation or hook entry. The contributor reads with the client of the read's own transaction, with fixed SQL and one parameter (the person id), scoped by `app_tenant_default()`.
- Project, area and company mail rows, and the client copy, are out of scope and not built; they would register further contributors with the same registry.

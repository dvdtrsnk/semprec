## What was delivered

`POST /api/events/series/:seriesId/occurrences/:date` turns one occurrence of a recurring Event into an Events item, or returns the item that already stands for it: 201 on creation, 200 afterwards (also for an occurrence item in the Trash, which is returned as stored and not recreated). Occurrences that nobody edited stay virtual.

- `materializeEventOccurrence(client, { seriesId, occurrenceDate, options })` (`calendar/materializeEventOccurrence.ts`) takes a tenant-scoped advisory lock keyed by tenant, series and date, looks up an existing occurrence item (live or trashed), then locks and reads the series Event (404 for an unknown, foreign, trashed or non-series id), checks `eventRecurrenceOccursOn` (400 `validation_failed` on `date`), creates the copy through `createEventInCalendar` (or `createItemWithClient` when the series has no calendar edge) with the system-owned `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate`, and copies the edges with invitations dropped.
- `formatOccurrenceId` / `parseOccurrenceId` (`calendar/eventOccurrenceId.ts`): the id of an occurrence is `<seriesId>@<date>`.
- The Events database gets the system-owned properties `seriesId` (text) and `occurrenceDate` (date), owner process `calendar`, in the seed (`seedTenDatabases.ts`, labels in `seed/i18n/{cs,en}.json`, `systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts`) and for existing tenants through `calendar/eventsOccurrenceCutoverMigration.ts` (`runEventsOccurrenceCutoverMigration`), registered in `db/tenantCutoverMigrations.ts` after the gh-1830 and gh-1833 entries and before the area-note step.
- A PATCH of the occurrence item moves only that occurrence; a PATCH of `seriesId` or `occurrenceDate` is 403 `owner_violation`.

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. No earlier source gave Events the properties `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate`; they are added here with seed, i18n and cutover entries, none of them in Touches.
2. The series Event is the series identity (ADR above).
3. The route entry is in `routeMatrix/events.ts`, not `routeMatrix.ts`.
4. Helpers of `duplicateCalendarItem.ts` are exported; `copiedEndDate` changed its signature; `copyEdges` accepts an undefined user id so the system writer can copy.
5. A non-UUID series id is 400 on `seriesId` (as the Tasks occurrence route does), not 404.
6. An Event whose stored recurrence is not a rule, or that already has a `seriesId`, is not a recurring Event and answers 404. A series whose first date does not match its own rule has no occurrence on that date (literal `eventRecurrenceOccursOn` of gh-1817); a trashed series gives no entries and neither do its live occurrence items.
7. The advisory lock key includes the tenant, so a foreign request never waits on another tenant's work; the series row is then locked `FOR UPDATE`.

Implemented issue #723: building `web/dist` into releases and serving the SPA from Caddy.

- **`deploy/deploy.sh`** (`build_release`): added a second subshell that installs and builds `web/` after the backend build, failing with `build of the staged web client failed` before any activation step if it fails.
- **`deploy/Caddyfile`**: replaced the bare `reverse_proxy` with a `@backend` matcher (`/api/* /mcp /healthz`) routed to `semprec-api`, and a catch-all `handle` block serving `/opt/semprec/current/web/dist` with `try_files {path} /index.html` for the SPA's query-param routes. Updated the header comment accordingly.
- **`deploy/deploy.test.sh`**: fixture releases now include a `web/package.json`; the `pnpm` mock produces `dist/index.html` on `run build` and can simulate a web-build failure independently of the backend-build failure; added assertions that `web/dist/index.html` exists after a successful deploy and that a web-build failure is refused pre-activation.
- **`deploy/README.md`**: updated the `Caddyfile` bullet and step 2 of "Deploying a release", and added a `## Web client` section after `## Front door (Caddy)`.

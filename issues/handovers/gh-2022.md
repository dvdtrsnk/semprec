## What was delivered

- `PATCH /api/approval-requests/:id` answers the decision view plus a top-level `outcome`: `"decided"` when this call made the transition, `"unchanged"` when the request was already decided (same or opposite decision, including the loser of two concurrent decisions). - `outcome` is taken from the result `decideAndEnqueueApprovalRequest` already returns; the handler spreads it into the PATCH answer only. `GET /api/approval-requests` (list) and `GET /api/approval-requests/:id` carry no `outcome`.
- A foreign, unknown or other-tenant id still answers the existing 404 without an `outcome`.

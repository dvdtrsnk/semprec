# gh-1730 implementation handover

## Behavior and interfaces

- `OperationMetadata.reversible: boolean` in `genericOperations/capabilities.ts`. All non-approval operations are
  `true`; of the five approval-gated ones `item.delete` and `database.archive` are `true`, `property.delete`,
  `view.delete` and `relation.delete` are `false`. The `destructive()` helper now takes `reversible` explicitly.
- `deriveApprovalRiskLevel({ riskClass, operationName? })`, `APPROVAL_RISK_LEVELS` and `ApprovalRiskLevel`
  (`irreversible | outbound | breaksLink | write`) are exported from `@semprec/shared`. `breaksLink` and `outbound`
  map to themselves; `destructive` gives `irreversible` unless `operationName` is a known generic operation flagged
  `reversible` (an unknown or absent name stays `irreversible`); everything else gives `write`.
- `riskLevel` is on `ApprovalRequestQueueEntry` and `ApprovalRequestDecisionView` (`approvalRequestsQueue.ts`),
  computed from the request's `riskClass` and, for generic-operation payloads, `payload.operationName`.
  All earlier fields are unchanged. No migration; the level is derived at read time, not stored.

## Tests

- `operationCatalog.unit.test.ts`: expected metadata includes `reversible`; table-driven `deriveApprovalRiskLevel` cases
  (five gated operations, MCP destructive, outbound, breaksLink, write/read/unclassified, unknown operation name).
- `approvalRequestsQueue.test.ts`: `riskLevel` on queue entry and decision view for the five operations and for MCP
  requests of class destructive, outbound, breaksLink and unclassified.

## Notes for dependants

- Clients map `riskLevel` to labels from their own catalog; no label text is stored or returned.
- #1773 (push body) can call `deriveApprovalRiskLevel` directly with the request's class and operation name.

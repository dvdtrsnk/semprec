## What changed

Only `backend/packages/data/src/__tests__/docsPerTenant.test.ts` changes; production code is untouched.

- The concurrent-first-write test now runs through a local `runFirstWriteRace` seam that owns both clients:
  - `startLoser` attaches a handler for both resolve and reject as soon as the loser promise is created. A loser rejection is therefore never unhandled, and cleanup can wait for the loser even though the scenario never awaited it.
  - If the scenario fails before the commit, the winner is rolled back first, which releases the row lock the loser waits on.
  - Cleanup then waits for the loser to settle, and only after that releases the winner client and then the loser client.
  - If the scenario fails after `commitWinner`, the ROLLBACK is skipped.
  - The scenario's own error is always the one rethrown. If the ROLLBACK also fails, that failure is logged with `console.warn` and does not replace it.
- The real-Postgres race keeps its successful path and its final counts: one doc, one snapshot, one history baseline.
- A same-file regression drives the cleanup with fake clients that log every step. The fake loser settles on a later macrotask, after the winner's ROLLBACK or COMMIT. Cases:
  - pre-commit failure, loser resolves or rejects: events are `winner BEGIN, winner ROLLBACK, loser <outcome>, winner released, loser released`, the forced error is rethrown, and no unhandled rejection occurs;
  - the same, with the winner ROLLBACK also failing: the forced error is still the reported one and exactly one warning is logged;
  - failure after `commitWinner`, loser resolves or rejects: events are `winner BEGIN, winner COMMIT, loser <outcome>, winner released, loser released`, with no ROLLBACK. This covers the `committed` guard, as the review asked.

## What was delivered

- Deleting any instance of a recurring task series puts every instance of the series in the Trash under one `deletedAt`; restoring any one of them restores the series. While the series is in the Trash its repeat rule is off: the new column `task_recurrence.trashed_active_item_id` records the instance that held the active rule, the restore turns the rule on again for that instance only, and a rule the user had turned off stays off. A task without a series, and another series, are not touched.
- Purge after the retention removes the whole series including its `task_recurrence` row.
- The delete preview (`delete-impact`) reports `seriesInstanceCount`, `attachmentCount` and `hostEventCount`, read through a per-module delete impact reader. The `item.delete` approval projection hashes the numbers into the snapshot (an approved request is refused as stale, with the current numbers, after the series gained an instance), and the approval description and the notification body (new body entry `approval.itemDeleteImpact`, en and cs) carry them. The counts are reader-aware: targets in databases the reader may not read, and Files that inherit sensitivity from an unreadable database, are not counted for an agent.
- The attachments' projects stay their own on delete and are not re-inherited on restore. Events keep their edges to a deleted task and show it again after the restore.
- ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-14-a-recurring-task-series-is-trashed-and-restored-as-one-cascade.md` records the cascade, the lock order and the reader scope.

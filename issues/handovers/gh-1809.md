## What was delivered

- `loadSleepNights(client, { from, to })` in `packages/data/src/sleep/sleepRouteHandlers.ts`: validates the range (both dates required, `YYYY-MM-DD`, `from` not after `to`, at most 62 nights) before any read, then reads the settings item, the non-deleted plans, the non-deleted night items of the range and `getSystemTimezone`, calls `resolveSleepNights` and returns `SleepNightsResponse`.
- `sleepNightsGet(pool)`: the route handler factory (`customRouteMount.ts` requires `(pool) => handler`). It opens the transaction with `isolation: "repeatable_read"` so the four reads describe one state (review fix `0ec01f820`).
- The sleep manifest declares `GET /api/sleep/nights` as a custom route (`handlerExport: "sleepNightsGet"`, justification `transactional-semantics`) and re-exports the handler.
- Route matrix entry `sleep nights` (tenancy `tenant`, not public) in the new slice `routeMatrix/sleep.ts`, the `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping and a tenant isolation case.

## Contracts dependents build on

- Route `GET /api/sleep/nights?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD`, session required (401 without), tenant scoped.
- Response: `{ enabled, showInCalendar, muteSemp, alarmSound, alarmSnooze, timezone, planCount, goalMinutes, nights: [{ night, planId, planName, bedTime, wakeTime, alarm, changed, bedAt, wakeAt, durationMinutes, prepReminderAt }] }`. `planCount` counts non-deleted plans; nights are computed also when `enabled` is false; the preparation reminder settings are consumed, not returned.
- Errors: 400 `validation_failed` with `field` `from` (missing, malformed, or after `to`) or `to` (missing, malformed, or a range over 62 nights); 404 when the tenant has no sleep settings item (no module, or item deleted); 500 for stored data that breaks the schema (ZodError) or for more than one settings item.
- `loadSleepNights` and `SleepNightsResponse` are exported from `sleepRouteHandlers.ts` for #1833 (areaId), #1835 (single night PUT returns the same night shape) and #1840 (agent tools). They are not in the `@semprec/data` barrel; the first cross-package consumer adds them.

# Handover: run every IMAP move of a pass's move phase over one connection

Commit on `fix/followup-mail-latency`: `508cc1a62`. Nothing was run against a real server.

**Root cause.** `drainImapMoves` (`mail/mailMessageMoveSync.ts`) called its client factory inside the per-move loop and closed the connection after each move, so N pending moves cost N connects, logins and logouts before the pass's reconcile connection.

**Changes.**
- `mail/mailMessageMoveSync.ts`: the move phase opens its connection for the first move it executes and reuses it for the rest; a move with an unknown provider outcome (`prepareMove` without a result, a thrown or non-acknowledged step) closes it and the next move reconnects. On a failure that ends the phase the connection is closed without its close failure replacing the move's; a close failure at the end of a successful phase is the fixed `IMAP move provider cleanup failed unexpectedly`.
- `docs/architecture/domain-mail-and-inbox.md`: Provider moves.

**Measured with the fake IMAP account** (`__tests__/imapMoveWritebackConnection.test.ts`): three archived threads, one pass: connections opened 4 → 2 (one move phase, one reconcile). At Purelymail's ~0.5 s connect and login that is about 0.5 s less per extra move.

**Tests.** `moves every archived message of a pass over one connection and closes it` (failed before: 4 connections); `opens a fresh connection for the next move after a move whose outcome is unknown` (3 connections: the failed MOVE stays uncertain, the other two run on a new connection).

**Review round** (commit `5dc4226e3`). A close failure after a move failure is logged (stage `imap_move_close`, error name) instead of dropped; the fixed cleanup error at the end of a phase keeps the client's already provider-safe close error as its `cause`.

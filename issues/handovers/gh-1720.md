## What was delivered

- `POST /api/tasks/series/:seriesId/occurrences/:date` with body `{status?, completedOn?, date?, timeFrom?, timeTo?}`: a custom route of the `systemDatabases` module, `customRoutes` entry `materializeTaskOccurrence` in `backend/packages/data/src/seed/systemDatabasesModuleManifest.ts` (method `POST`, `handlerExport: "createTaskOccurrenceMaterializeRouteHandler"`, justification `transactional-semantics`). The manifest file re-exports the handler factory.
- `createTaskOccurrenceMaterializeRouteHandler(pool)` in `backend/packages/data/src/tasks/taskOccurrenceRoutes.ts`, exported from the data barrel. It returns `{ status, item }`; the adapter answers the full item envelope with that status.
- Validation before any database access:
  - `:seriesId` must be a UUID (`ValidationError`, field `seriesId`). It is used in lower case, the form PostgreSQL prints for `task_recurrence.series_id` and the form every occurrence stores in `seriesId`.
  - `:date` must be a calendar date that exists (`isCalendarDate`), else `ValidationError` on field `occurrenceDate`.
  - The body must be an object (field `body`) whose keys are only `status`, `completedOn`, `date`, `timeFrom`, `timeTo` (any other key is refused with that key as the field, also `seriesId`, `occurrenceDate`, `name`, `flagged`). `status` must be `notDone`, `done` or `wontDo`; `missed` is refused.
- In one transaction (`withTransaction`):
  1. The live Tasks item with that `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate` is found and locked in one statement (`FOR UPDATE`, lowest id first), so a row trashed or taken out of the series while the request waited is not returned.
  2. The `task_recurrence` row is locked `FOR UPDATE` after it (items before `task_recurrence`, `docs/adr/2026-10-25-task-and-event-recurrence-model.md`).
  3. A tenant without a Tasks database, an unknown series, a turned-off series (`active` false) and another tenant's series all throw the same `NotFoundError` (404 `not_found`).
  4. A date the locked rule does not give on or after `anchor_date` (`occurrenceDatesInRange(rule, anchorDate, date, date)` is empty) throws `ValidationError` on field `occurrenceDate` (400 `validation_failed`), whether or not an item is stored for the date.
  5. Without a stored item the route checks `hasSeriesOccurrence` again under the series lock. If another request stored the occurrence in between, it throws `ConflictError` "The occurrence was stored while this request waited" (409 `version_conflict`) and creates nothing; a repeat of the request answers 200. Otherwise it creates the item through `createItemWithClient` as the Tasks module (`systemOwnerProcess: TASKS_MODULE_ID`, system keys `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate`): the public series template (`name` left out when null, `timeFrom`, `timeTo`, `notifications`, `flagged`, `description`), `status: "notDone"`, `date` and `occurrenceDate` equal to `:date`, and `seriesId`. The new item carries no `recurrence` property.
  6. A non-empty body is applied to the item through `updateItemWithClient` as the caller's user write, so the Tasks pre-write transform validates `completedOn`, `date`, `timeFrom` and `timeTo`, and the item-update hook of gh-1670 advances the series when the item is the series' current occurrence and becomes `done` or `wontDo`. A refusal there rolls the new item back. A body `date`, `timeFrom` or `timeTo` changes this item only; its `occurrenceDate` stays.
- Answers: 201 when this request created the item, 200 when it was stored before. An empty body `{}` writes nothing more and answers the item.
- A series row without `anchor_date` or `template` raises a plain `Error` (500), as the advance does.
- Fix round, also in `backend/packages/data/src/tasks/taskSeriesRoutes.ts` (`PATCH /api/tasks/series/:seriesId` of gh-1667):
  - `seriesIdOf` returns the id in lower case there as well. Before, an id sent in upper case found the series but none of its open stored occurrences, and the response echoed the upper-case id.
  - `lockOpenOccurrences` became `openOccurrenceIds(client, databaseId, seriesId, current, lock)`. The edit calls it with `lock: true` before the series row is locked, as before, and again with `lock: false` once it holds the row. When the second read returns an id the first did not lock, the edit throws the existing `ConflictError` "The series changed while it was being edited" (409). That is the case of an occurrence this route stored and committed while the edit waited for the series row.
- Registration: the route matrix entry `{ name: "task occurrence materialize", method: "POST", path: "/api/tasks/series/<EXAMPLE_ID>/occurrences/2026-10-25", surface: "api", public: false, tenancy: "tenant" }` in `backend/services/semprec-api/src/routeMatrix/tasks.ts` (`TASKS_ROUTES`); the `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping of "task occurrence materialize" to `packages/data/src/tasks/taskOccurrenceRoutes.ts`; the `ROUTE_CASES` case "task occurrence materialize" with the helper `seriesOccurrenceState` in `tenantIsolationRoutes.test.ts`; the barrel export in `backend/packages/data/src/index.ts` and its name in the expected list of `publicSurface.test.ts`.
- No SQL migration, no table, no column, no index, no job name, no write hook, no generic operation, no stored key, no user-facing label and no ADR was added.

## Contracts dependents build on

For #2012 (place one occurrence of a recurring task into an event), #1886 (task occurrences in the calendar timeline), and #1722 and #1724, which name this source as a shared-file-ordering blocker only.

- To get an item for an occurrence that is still virtual, call `POST /api/tasks/series/:seriesId/occurrences/:date` with `{}`; the answer is the item (201 or 200). #2012 materializes through this route and then places the returned item; it adds no route of its own.
- The pair `seriesId` plus `occurrenceDate` identifies an occurrence. The route keeps one live item per pair on its own paths: the lookup locks, and the create happens under the series row lock that the advance (`advanceTaskRecurrenceWithClient`), the nightly close (`closeMissedOccurrences`) and this route all hold when they store an occurrence. A new writer that stores an occurrence of a series must hold the `task_recurrence` row `FOR UPDATE` as well, or the series edit's second read and this route's re-check can miss it.
- A stored occurrence created here has `status: "notDone"` before the body is applied, also for a past date. Marking an occurrence `missed` stays with #1668 and #1677.
- After the POST, `projectTaskOccurrences` (gh-1672) returns the stored item for that date instead of a virtual occurrence.
- `task_recurrence.item_id` is not moved by this route. It moves only through the advance, when the body completes the series' current occurrence.
- Compare a series id as text only in lower case. A handler that takes a series id from a path or a body and compares it with `properties->>'seriesId'` lower-cases it first, as `seriesIdOf` does in both task route files.
- A client of `PATCH /api/tasks/series/:seriesId` can now get 409 `version_conflict` in one more case and repeats the edit.
- Later `/api/tasks/...` routes add their matrix entries to `routeMatrix/tasks.ts`, with the `ROUTE_CASES` case and the `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` mapping in the same change.
- The route rejects a request without a session and answers it with `Cache-Control: no-store` (`routeMatrix.test.ts` and `cacheControl.test.ts` have one test each that names the entry).

## Deviations from the specification and spec seams

1. The route entry is in the slice module `routeMatrix/tasks.ts`, not in `routeMatrix.ts`, which `Touches` names (the known seam of the route matrix split).
2. Not in `Touches`: `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` in `chokePointWriteBoundary.unit.test.ts` and the expected export list in `publicSurface.test.ts`. Both inventories fail without the entry.
3. Not in `Touches`, from the fix round: `backend/packages/data/src/tasks/taskSeriesRoutes.ts` and `backend/packages/data/src/__tests__/taskSeriesRoutes.test.ts`. The series edit of gh-1667 gains the lower-case series id and one more 409 case. `Out of scope` of gh-1720 names editing the whole series (#1667); the recheck judged that the change does not implement series editing and is the fix the first review proposed. No decision record `issues/decisions/gh-1720.md` exists.
4. Decisions the issue does not state, each with a test:
   - Of two concurrent requests for the same virtual occurrence, the one that finds the item stored by the other after waiting for the series row is refused with 409 and creates nothing. Locking that item then would take an item lock after the `task_recurrence` lock, against the lock order of ADR 2026-10-25.
   - A turned-off series answers 404 like an unknown one, as `PATCH /api/tasks/series/:seriesId` does. `projectTaskOccurrences` still returns virtual occurrences of such a series up to its last stored occurrence (the requirements reviewer's note, left to the owner).
   - 201 when this request created the item, 200 when it was stored.
   - The date check applies whether or not an item is stored. A stored occurrence on a date the current rule or anchor does not give answers 400 here and stays writable through the generic item write. Such an occurrence exists after a rule change, when a series was started on a date its rule does not give (for example the `weekdays` preset on a task dated on a Saturday), and when a floating series advanced from a completion day that is not a whole number of intervals after the anchor date.
   - For a floating rule the accepted dates are `anchor_date` plus whole multiples of the interval, the dates the projection of gh-1672 offers as virtual.
   - The created item carries no `recurrence` property, unlike the items the advance and the nightly close create (Task: "a copy of the series template with `occurrenceDate` set").
   - A tenant without a Tasks database answers 404.
5. No ADR was added: the lock order, the stored-versus-virtual model and the Tasks-module ownership of `seriesId` and `occurrenceDate` are in ADR 2026-10-25, the route uses the existing manifest justification, and the series edit's second read takes no new lock.

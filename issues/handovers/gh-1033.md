## What changed

- **Spawn re-check** (`mcp/mcpConnectionFactory.ts`): for `stdio`, `connectMcpServer` calls `tenantAllowsStdio(client)` after parsing the config and before resolving the credential. A refusal throws `transport_not_allowed`, with no decryption, no `credential_access_log` row and no child process. `sse` and `http` are unchanged.
- **Error reason** (`mcp/mcpConnectionError.ts`): added `transport_not_allowed`.
- **Environment name rule** (`mcp/mcpConnectionConfig.ts`): `env` keys and `credentialEnvVar` must match `^[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*$`. They may not be one of the six inherited names, may not start with `LD_` or `DYLD_`, and may not be one of the listed interpreter or shell hooks. The same schema runs at write time and at spawn.
- **Explicit child environment**: `buildStdioTransport` always passes `{ ...getDefaultEnvironment(), ...config.env }` plus the injected credential. The misleading comment about `env` stripping defaults is rewritten to describe the actual merge.
- **Fixture**: the stdio contract child records the names (never values) of its environment. `StdioContractServer.getChildEnvironmentNames()` exposes them.
- **Tests**:
  - `mcpConnectionFactory.test.ts`: member refused with no spawn and no log row; exact child environment, with a parent-only secret absent; forbidden names refused at spawn as `invalid_config`. Existing stdio tests now make the tenant user an admin.
  - `mcpServerWriteHook.test.ts`: one case refusing forbidden names at write, even from an admin.
  - New `mcpConnectionConfig.unit.test.ts`: schema accept and reject cases.
  - `mcpSync.test.ts` and `mcpInvokeTool.test.ts`: setup makes the tenant-zero user an admin. `approvalRequestExecution.test.ts` already did this.
- **Comment fix**: `stdioTransportPolicy.ts` no longer describes the spawn use as future work.

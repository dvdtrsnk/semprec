# Handover: lock and purge only a row that is still trashed past the cutoff in the permanent purge

Delivered on chain `e1-244-purge`, candidate commit `934058b571b8afa82c9a5db3eab9dfddbcb30ade`, landed by a `--no-ff` merge (`8c6adbc2d35a91f6ffc6d31b7d1e177afe758615`) on `feature/backend-parallel` as the third chain of a train with `e1-235-value-link-locks` and `e1-237-db-roles`. The segment commit of this source is `72ef8556a155ce4f355fa991ff0edc3be24af32e`; the review fix is `934058b571b8afa82c9a5db3eab9dfddbcb30ade`.

**What changed.**
1. New `lockTrashedItemBefore(client, databaseId, itemId, cutoff)` in `backend/packages/data/src/chokePoint/itemsStore.ts`: a `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` of the same columns as `lockItemById`, whose own WHERE requires `deleted_at IS NOT NULL AND deleted_at < cutoff`. A row that is gone, live, or trashed at or after the cutoff returns nothing and is not locked.
2. The delete loop of `purgeExpiredTrashSubtree` (`chokePoint/itemTrash.ts`) uses it instead of `lockItemById`. A skipped row gets no lock, no delete, no dependent delete and no hook call; every hook call therefore receives a row whose `deletedAt` is older than the cutoff of the call. The docstring and the loop comments describe this.
3. New ADR `docs/adr/2026-11-15-the-permanent-purge-locks-only-a-row-that-is-still-trashed-past-the-cutoff.md` (accepted, backend, standalone), narrowing three statements of `2026-11-10-item-purge-domain-hooks` without editing it.

**Contracts dependents build on.** `itemsStore.lockTrashedItemBefore` (internal to the choke point, not exported from the package barrel). Purge hooks may rely on `item.deletedAt < cutoff` for the row they receive. `hardDeleteItem` and its condition are unchanged. No route, table, migration, operation or job name changed.

**Files.** `backend/packages/data/src/chokePoint/itemTrash.ts`, `backend/packages/data/src/chokePoint/itemsStore.ts`, the ADR above.

**Tests.** `backend/packages/data/src/__tests__/itemPurgeHooks.test.ts`, four new cases (existing 14 unchanged): a nested row restored before the purge reaches it is skipped, with no hook, no dependent delete and no lock held (`FOR UPDATE NOWAIT` probes); a row restored and trashed again after the walk is not removed, nor its cascade, and a later purge with a later cutoff does remove it; `lockTrashedItemBefore` locks only the row trashed before the cutoff (not a live row, a row trashed at or after it, a missing row); a writer of the restored row commits while the purge is still running once the row was skipped. The worker's mutation (loop switched back to `lockItemById`) failed three of the four.

**Deviations and spec seams.** No spec seam. The `hardDeleteItem` docstring in `itemsStore.ts` still says its condition is "the only thing standing between a race and permanently losing a freshly-restored item"; that is no longer strictly true now that the locking read also guards, but `hardDeleteItem` is Out of scope and was not reworded. The tests backdate trash with the file's existing `setDeletedAt` helper to get a real cutoff. The ADR's claim that a restore committing during the locking statement's own wait still leaves a lock on the newest version rests on the issue and PostgreSQL's `FOR UPDATE` recheck behaviour; no test pins that single case.

**Criteria NOT RUN or BLOCKED.** None.

**Review.** Requirements and tests lens: one low finding (two test comments claimed the purge had passed the restored row before the sibling's hook, which the walk order does not guarantee). Rules and architecture lens: clear. Security lens: clear. The finding was confirmed and fixed in comments only (`934058b571b8afa82c9a5db3eab9dfddbcb30ade`); no recheck (low, no behaviour change).

**What changed**
- **Route flag:** `RouteDefinition` has `admin?: true`, forwarded to the listener. `mountRoutes` throws at construction, naming the path, for any `/api/admin/` route without the flag.
- **Gate:** a non-admin caller gets `403 { error: { code: "admin_required" } }` after authentication and before the body is read. For an admin, body parsing, parameters and the handler run in `runAsSystem("adminRoute")`. Routes without the flag keep the caller's tenant scope.
- **Migration:** `0074_admin_audit_log.sql`. 0074 was still the next free ordinal on `origin/develop` when I checked.
- **Store:** `tenancy/accountsAdminStore.ts` has `listAccounts`, `ADMIN_AUDIT_ACTIONS` and `recordAdminAction`, which writes in the caller's transaction. It is exported from `index.ts` and added to `EXPECTED_RUNTIME_EXPORTS`.
- **Handler and wiring:** `adminHandler.ts` has `createAdminRoutes` with `GET /api/admin/accounts`, mounted in `app.ts`. `routeMatrix.ts` has the new entry and `ROUTE_HANDLER_FILES` maps it to its handler file.
- **Import restriction:** an ESLint override in `eslint.config.mjs` forbids `runInTenant` in `adminHandler.ts` and `accountsAdminStore.ts`.
- **ADR:** `docs/adr/2026-10-17-admin-role-without-content-access.md`.
- **Tests:** new `adminHandler.test.ts`, `accountsAdminStore.test.ts` and `adminScopeImports.unit.test.ts`, plus an admin-scope case in `adapterRoute.test.ts`.

**Deviations from the issue**
- In `leastPrivilegeRoles.test.ts` I added `admin_audit_log` to the `NO_UPDATE_TABLES`, `READ_ONLY_FOR_SIDE_TABLES` and `NO_DELETE_TABLES` sets. The issue only says to add it to the grant exceptions.
- `semprec_data` also reads the audit table through its membership in `semprec_side`, so it gets `SELECT` without a direct grant.
- The lint unit test loads the full type-aware ESLint program, so it carries a 60-second timeout.
- The account-list test asserts the exact `lastSeenAt` on the member who has two sessions. The admin's own requests refresh its session's `last_seen_at`, so that value can't be asserted exactly.

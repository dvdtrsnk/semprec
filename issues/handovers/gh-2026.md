## What was delivered

A card that has been `computing` for longer than ten minutes is listed as `computeFailed` with `computeTimedOut: true`, derived on read; nothing is written. A recompute of such a card is accepted like any other status, and a late tick result turns it into `proposed` again.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- `inbox/inboxCardsStore.ts`: `COMPUTING_TIMEOUT_MS` (10 minutes) and the pure `isComputingTimedOut(card, now)` (stored status `computing` and `updatedAt` strictly older than `now` minus the timeout; exactly the timeout is not timed out; an unparseable `updatedAt` never times out). The card list builds a timed-out card with status `computeFailed` and `computeTimedOut: true` (new optional field of `InboxCard`); `version` stays the stored `updatedAt`; one `now` per page; the count query and the candidate grouping are unchanged, so a timed-out card is not counted, like every `computing` card.
- `inbox/proposalRecompute.ts`: the `computing` early return of `recomputeProposalWithClient` now reads "computing and not timed out" and sits before the `ifVersion` check. A timed-out card goes through the normal write: `computing` with a new `updatedAt`, a second `recompute` history entry, exactly one tick job (revision 0).
- The timeout flag is computed from the stored item's own status and `updatedAt`; no sweep, no failure record and no push were added (out of scope).

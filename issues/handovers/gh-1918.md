## What was delivered

Two user-narrowed realtime scopes, `notificationPreferences` and `badgeCounts`, end to end, and their publishers.

- `InvalidationEvent` (`packages/data/src/realtimeHook.ts`) gained `{ scope: "notificationPreferences"; userId: string }` and `{ scope: "badgeCounts"; userId: string }`. `RealtimeMessage` and `parseRealtimeMessage` (`packages/realtime/src/protocolV1.ts`) carry both with a `userId` that must be a UUID; a message without a valid `userId` is malformed and dropped by the parser, never widened to the tenant. `OutboundFrame` gained `{ type: "invalidate"; scope: "notificationPreferences" | "badgeCounts" }`. `syncServer.ts` sends the frame with `sendToTenant(tenantId, frame, parsed.userId)`, so only that user's sockets of the envelope's tenant receive it. `parseServerFrame` (`packages/realtime-client/src/serverFrame.ts`) parses both and keeps nothing but `type` and `scope`.
- `notifications/notificationInvalidation.ts` exports `publishNotificationPreferencesInvalidation(client, userId)` and `publishBadgeCountsInvalidation(client, userId)`; both defer `notifyInvalidation` with `runAfterCommit`, so a rollback sends nothing.
- `patchNotificationPreferences` publishes after its checked UPDATE; an empty patch writes and publishes nothing.
- `publishApprovalRequestChange` (`mcp/approvalRequestRealtime.ts`) now also publishes `badgeCounts` to the run's `actor_user_id`, which it already reads. It is the one funnel every write that changes the pending-decision count already calls in its transaction (`createPendingApprovalRequestAndPublish`, `decideAndEnqueueApprovalRequest`, `approvalRequestExecution`, `moduleToolApprovalExecution`, `genericOperationGateway`), and it is called only when the write changed a row.

## Entry points and contracts dependents build on

- Wire frames: `{"type":"invalidate","scope":"notificationPreferences"}` and `{"type":"invalidate","scope":"badgeCounts"}`, no other field, sent only to the sockets of the affected user in the tenant. A client refetches the preferences or the badge counts.
- NOTIFY message: `invalidation` with `scope` and a UUID `userId`; the tenant comes from the NOTIFY envelope (`app_tenant_default()`), never from the message.
- The two publishers are exported from `notifications/notificationInvalidation.ts`; they are not added to the `@semprec/data` barrel (`index.ts` is at the 900-line ESLint cap), so another package reaches them through the barrels that already export the stores.
- A write that leaves the count as it was may still announce `badgeCounts` (over-publication is allowed by the issue; the pending count is read again by the client).
- No table, migration, route, operation or job name was added.

## What was delivered

- `MailRepository.facets()` takes no argument; `HttpMailRepository` sends `GET /api/mail/threads/facets` without query
  parameters. `MailFacets` no longer carries `selectedProjectName`.
- `MailThreadsViewModel` loads the facets at start and on refresh only (no longer on `SetProject` or `ClearContext`),
  and names the chosen project from the facet options.
- `SearchSubtitleKind.COUNT` is gone from `:shared:core`, the search DTO mapping, the CoreKit `SearchSubtitleKind` and
  its bridge, the Overview `SearchSubtitle`, the `search.count` copy key and the architecture policy's copy list.

## Contracts dependents build on

- `MailRepository.facets(): RepositoryResult<MailFacets>` with `MailFacets(total, projectsTotal, projects)`.
- A search subtitle part of an unknown kind, `count` included, is dropped by the data layer.

# Handover: sync again 15 s and 60 s after an IMAP/SMTP send

Commit on `fix/followup-post-send-sync`: `9186c9679`.

**Changes.**
- `mail/mailSyncEnqueue.ts`: `MAIL_POST_SEND_SYNC_DELAYS_MS` (15 s, 60 s), `mailPostSendSyncJobKey(mailboxItemId, delayMs)` (`mail-account-sync-after-send:<ms>:<mailbox>`), `enqueuePostSendMailSyncs(client, mailboxItemId)`: one `mailAccountSync` per slot, `runAt` now + delay, `jobKeyMode: "preserve_run_at"`, `maxAttempts` 3.
- `mail/mailSentCopies.ts`: `recordPendingSentCopy` calls it on its client, so the follow-ups commit with the send's finalization (draft send, approved agent/MCP send) or with `recordPendingSentCopyAfterSend` (invitation and invitation-reply jobs, a send whose finalization failed). Only sends that answer `needsSentCopy` (IMAP/SMTP) reach it; Gmail and Graph are unaffected.
- `docs/architecture/domain-mail-and-inbox.md`: why and how.

**Choice to know.** `preserve_run_at` rather than `replace`: a send while a slot is pending keeps the slot's due time, so a stream of sends cannot postpone the follow-up. A send at t=10 s while the 15 s slot is pending is covered by the 60 s slot. The follow-ups are the normal `mailAccountSync` task, so one that starts during a pass only asks that pass for one more round (existing single-flight).

**Tests** (`mailSentCopyJob.test.ts`): an accepted send schedules exactly the two jobs (keys, revision 0, due within a second of +15 s / +60 s); three sends keep one job per slot at the first due time (revision 2); a refused send and a send whose provider files its own copy schedule none; `recordPendingSentCopyAfterSend` schedules both. The three positive tests failed with the enqueue removed.

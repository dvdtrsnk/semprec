# Handover for gh-1378

Added the accepted ADR `docs/adr/2026-10-25-multi-step-proposal-envelope.md`. It is documentation only; no code, schema or test changed.

**What the ADR records**
- **Shape:** optional `operations` array; steps carry `ref`, `op` (`create`/`update`/`link`), `databaseKey` or `itemId`, `properties`, `relations` (item id or `{ ref }`), `role`, `optional`, `enabled`. An `update` step carries `before`/`after` and is a general write through the item-write choke point under the usual rules.
- **Compatibility:** single-step envelopes stay valid; a multi-step envelope mirrors its primary step in the top-level `entityKind`, `target`, `properties`, and `resultItemId`/`resultLabel` are the primary step's result.
- **Skipping, refs, confirm:** disabled or unreferenced optional steps are skipped; refs are same-proposal, acyclic, exactly one primary, written in dependency order; confirm is one transaction under the proposal row lock, failures return 400 naming `ref` and `field`; moving to another "one"-side owner is `deleteRelationWithClient` plus `createRelationWithClient`.
- **User edits** address a step by `ref` (`primary` for single-step), the key `userOverrides` uses.
- **Rejected:** child proposals after the first confirm; steps encoded in `properties`.

**Tests:** none apply; `node .github/scripts/adr.mjs --platform backend` lists the new ADR.

**Notes for dependants**
- #1479 (validation) and #1486 (confirm) must implement the shape literally; the ADR does not define per-step results (a separate issue) or tick computation (#1492).

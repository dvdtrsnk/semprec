# Handover for sp-a4e32028-4e3a-4320-9191-522c2edc851c

Implementation commit: efd52d53e.

- `CalendarDay.calendarWindow()` (`:shared:domain`) replaces `windowOf`; the calendar ViewModel calls it.
- `OfflineWarmup` (`:shared:data`) reads, with the screens' own requests through repositories over the window's API:
  the schema and databases, the task lists of every `TaskScope`, areas, projects and people, calendars, invitations, the
  calendar windows of the current month, the one before and the two after, the agenda's first page from today, the
  diary days from 7 back to 7 ahead with their pages, the overview, tonight's sleep, and the Inbox cards and types (the
  count is left out: no screen reads it). A failing read is left as it is; the reads stop once the process is offline.
- `OfflineWarmupSchedule` runs the warm-up of the window registered last after the queue replay that follows a window's
  start, a connection coming back or a socket connecting has finished (the replayer now runs what follows each replay
  it finished), at most once every 30 minutes per account, never offline or signed out. `HomeSDK` registers the
  window's warm-up. `OfflineLayer`'s kept-answer reads moved into `KeptReads` (same file).
- The offline set's ADR states the warm-up; decision record `issues/decisions/sp-a4e32028-4e3a-4320-9191-522c2edc851c.md`.

Tests: `OfflineWarmupTest` (the exact requests, the four windows and fifteen days, the screens opening offline after a
warm-up, failing reads, offline stop, the 30-minute limit, signed out and offline triggers, the order after the replay)
and the `CalendarDayTest` window case.

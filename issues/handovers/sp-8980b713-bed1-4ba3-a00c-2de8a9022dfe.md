# Handover: sync iCloud and generic CalDAV calendars

Implementation commit `0aa28f012` on `feat/ia-caldav` (from `feat/internet-accounts` at `7ff7036e9`).

**What changed.**
- New `backend/packages/data/src/calendar/sync/adapters/caldav/`:
  - `caldavHttp.ts`: `withCalDavSession(login, { signal, policy? }, fn)`, the only fetch any `tsdav` call gets. It is built on `createOutboundHttp()` and adds the stored user name and password as Basic credentials. Each request has 20 s (combined with the pass's signal) and a 10 MiB body cap (`invalidResponse` above it). It follows at most 3 redirects (301/302/307/308) itself, so each hop passes the outbound policy (https only in production). A refused address and every transport failure become `CalendarSyncProviderError("providerUnavailable", "The CalDAV server could not be reached")` with no cause; a timeout becomes `timeout`.
  - `caldavClient.ts`: the requests, through `tsdav`'s `propfind`, `syncCollection`, `calendarQuery`, `calendarMultiGet`, `createObject`, `updateObject` and `deleteObject`. Each answer is zod-validated before it is read. 401 → `CalendarSyncReauthorizationRequiredError`, 429 → `rateLimited`, 5xx → `providerUnavailable`, anything else unexpected → `invalidResponse`.
  - `caldavIcal.ts`: VEVENT ↔ `RemoteEventFields` with `ical.js`, the VTIMEZONE generator, and the resource edits (`updateResource`, `removeOccurrence`).
  - `caldavAdapter.ts`: `createCalDavAdapter(account, { policy? })`. Registered in `adapters/index.ts` as `imap`.
  - `caldavConnectVerification.ts`: `verifyCalDavServer` for the connect route.
  - `caldavErrors.ts`: `CalendarSyncEventConflictError` and the three connect refusals.
- Discovery starts at `https://caldav.icloud.com/` (provider `icloud`), else at `caldav_url`, else at `https://<domain of the primary address>/.well-known/caldav`. It reads `current-user-principal`, then `calendar-home-set`, then a Depth-1 PROPFIND of the home. A collection is listed when its resourcetype is `calendar` and it supports VEVENT (or declares no component set).
  - `remoteId` is the collection path; `url` is absolute.
  - `readOnly` is true unless the user has `write`, `write-content` or `all`. A missing privilege set counts as writable.
  - Colours: `#RRGGBB` or `#RRGGBBAA` becomes `#RRGGBB` (upper case).
- `fetchChanges`:
  - With a stored sync token: sync-collection, following a 507-truncated answer for up to 20 rounds. Changed hrefs are fetched by calendar-multiget (batches of 50) and returned as `full: false`.
  - The delta is turned into a full fetch when it holds a deletion or a changed resource that is a series (RRULE or overrides), or when the server answers 400/403/404/405/409/410/412/501.
  - Without a token: an unchanged `getctag` returns no changes and makes no request; a changed or missing one makes a full fetch.
  - A full fetch: a PROPFIND of `sync-token` and `getctag` first (the next cursor), then a calendar-query of VEVENT ETags in the window (widened by a day on each side, UTC bounds), then multiget.
- Mapping (`caldavIcal.ts`):
  - `remoteId` is the UID; an override's is `JSON.stringify([uid, recurrenceId])` (`overrideRemoteId`, `parseOverrideRemoteId`). `seriesRemoteId` is the UID. `href` is absolute; `etag` is the server's.
  - Times are wall clock in the master's zone. An IANA TZID is used as is, with vendor prefixes (`/mozilla.org/.../Europe/Prague`) stripped. `Z` is `UTC`. A TZID only a VTIMEZONE defines (Windows names) is converted to `UTC`. Floating times are `null`, the tenant zone.
  - An end at the next midnight is `timeTo: "24:00"`. A multi-day timed Event has `endDate`. All-day: `endDate` is the last day, `null` for one day. DURATION is honoured.
  - RRULE text is `icalRecurrenceRuleText.ts` `ruleText`, moved out of `subscribedEventsImport.ts` unchanged. ical.js reorders parts, e.g. `FREQ=WEEKLY;COUNT=10;BYDAY=WE`.
  - EXDATE and RECURRENCE-ID are local starts in the series' zone.
  - `alertMinutes` comes from the first DISPLAY VALARM with a duration trigger at or before the start; an absolute, end-related or later trigger gives `null`.
  - ORGANIZER and ATTENDEE become `{ email (mailto stripped), name (CN), role, status }`. The organizer has role and status `null`. Participants with a value over 2048 characters are dropped; at most 500 are kept.
  - Left out, counted and logged as `unreadable`: a resource without a VEVENT (VTODO), without a UID, unreadable by ical.js, or over the payload bounds. Such a resource is synced whole or not at all.
- Writes:
  - `putEvent` with `ref: null` makes `<calendar url><uuid>.ics` with `If-None-Match: *`. It writes a generated VTIMEZONE: every offset change from 1 year before to 25 years after, one RDATE per onset. `ref: null` with a `recurrenceId` throws a plain Error.
  - `putEvent` with a `ref` first multigets the resource. Gone, or an ETag other than `ref.etag`, is a conflict. It then rewrites only the properties whose modelled value changed (others, X- properties, attendees, other alarms and VTIMEZONEs stay byte for byte), updates DTSTAMP and PUTs with `If-Match`. An override missing from the resource is added.
  - `deleteEvent` of a series or single Event is a DELETE with `If-Match`; 404 counts as done. For an override, the occurrence is cancelled in its series' resource: the override is removed, EXDATE is added and the result is PUT with `If-Match`.
  - A 412 from the server, or an ETag mismatch before writing, throws `CalendarSyncEventConflictError`. That is not a `CalendarSyncProviderError`.
- Shared files touched (minimal, beyond the registry entry):
  - `calendar/sync/adapter.ts`: `CalendarSyncAccount.serverSettings: { username, caldavUrl } | null`.
  - `calendarAccountSyncJob.ts`: the prologue reads `getMailServerSettings` and passes it on.
  - `net/outboundAddressPolicy.ts`: `redirect: "manual"` is passed through; everything else is still `error`.
- Schema, edited in the baseline: `mail_account_server_settings.caldav_url text` with CHECK `char_length ≤ 2048`. `MailServerSettings.caldavUrl` is always present; `MailServerSettingsInput.caldavUrl` is optional. `normalizeCalDavUrl` requires the stored-URL baseline (https, default port, no userinfo, no IP literal or localhost) plus the mail host name rules, and drops the fragment.
- Connect route `POST /api/mail/mailboxes`:
  - New optional body field `caldavUrl`, generic only; iCloud gets 400 `validation_failed` on field `caldavUrl`.
  - With it, after the IMAP/SMTP verification, the new dependency `mailServerClients.verifyCalDav` runs CalDAV discovery to the calendar home with the same user name and password. Refusals are 400 `caldav_auth_failed`, `caldav_server_unreachable` (refused address or unreachable, identical) or `caldav_server_invalid`.
  - A body with `caldavUrl` on a mount without the member fails before the throttle with a configuration error.
  - `semprec-api/serve.ts` wires `verifyCalDav: verifyCalDavServer`, exported from `@semprec/data`.
- Docs: a "CalDAV" bullet in `domain-calendar-tasks-and-media.md`, `caldav_url` and the connect order in `domain-mail-and-inbox.md`, and the `redirect: "manual"` rule in `edges-io-and-errors.md`.
- Dependency: `tsdav` 2.5.0 (exact; 2.5.1 was 3 days old), with `xml-js` and `sax` as its own dependencies. The lockfile is prettier-formatted as in the repo, and `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` passes.

**Deviations and spec seams.**
- "compare getctag then list etags and fetch changed hrefs with calendar-multiget": the adapter keeps no ETags of its own, and the cursor (`syncToken`, `ctag`) has no room for them. A changed ctag therefore fetches every resource of the window. Fetching only changed hrefs needs the stored ETags (`event_remote_state`) passed into `fetchChanges`, a contract change.
- Incremental sync-collection falls back to a full fetch on any deletion or changed series. A deleted href names no UID, and a changed series may have lost an override; the payload's deletions are remote ids, so neither can be expressed without the stored state. Remaining hole: a resource that stops being recurring, changed incrementally, leaves its old overrides until the next full fetch.
- "verify it at connect when Calendars is requested": the connect body has no Calendars switch (accounts start with Calendars off). Verification runs when the body carries `caldavUrl`. An iCloud account and a generic one without `caldavUrl` are not verified at connect; their CalDAV login is first tried by the first pass after Calendars is switched on.
- `caldavUrl` must use the default https port: the stored-URL baseline refuses explicit ports, so e.g. Nextcloud on `:8443` cannot be configured.
- Credentials are sent to every redirect hop (well-known delegation to a provider host), as Apple and DAVx5 clients do. Every hop is https and policy-checked.

## What was delivered

- Shared: `CollectionDatabase.facetBarProperty`; `CollectionViewModel` reads that property's facet with the screen and
  after every query change and exposes `facetBar` (label, color, count, selected); chips toggle the same selections as
  the filter sheet; the bar is absent without a declaration or without values. `PropertyWrites.addOption` and
  `AddPropertyOption`; `ItemAction.Edit.AddChoice` adds an option then sets it, failures land in `choiceFailure`.
- Apple: `FilterChip` in DesignSystem, a facet bar above the collection list, a Tags section with creating a tag in
  `ProjectSettingsSheet`, tag chips in the project detail, tags in the collection row subtitle.

## Contracts dependents build on

- A database gains a chip bar by declaring `facetBarProperty` on the server; no app code.
